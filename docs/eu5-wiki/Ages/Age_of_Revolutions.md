The **Age of Revolutions** is the sixth and final [age](/Age "Age") in [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V"). It starts on January 1st, 1737 when the [Age of Absolutism](/Age_of_Absolutism "Age of Absolutism") ends, and lasts until the game ends.

## Advances

Age of Revolutions has three advance trees; each tree requires embracing an institution.

All Age of Revolutions advances require an additional **+75%** Research Progress from the base cost of **25**. Once this age has begun, advances from the previous Ages cost **-8** flat Research Progress, with the the previous Age increase modifiers still applying.
