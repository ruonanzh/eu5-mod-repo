Traditionally a land of diverse beliefs and disparate clans, we have finally united the Albanian people under a single banner and purpose. Now that we have expelled unwelcome outsiders and created a nascent state, Albania shall emerge as the heroic home nation of all Albanian people!

[![Flag of Albania](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ALB.png&width=24)](/Albania "Albania") Albania is a [Catholic](/Catholicism "Catholicism") monarchy in the Balkans region. In 1337, it is a vassal of [![Flag of Naples](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NAP.png&width=24)](/Naples "Naples") [Naples](/Naples "Naples") and ruled by the Anjou dynasty in 1337. Albania itself has the vassals [![Flag of Arianiti](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ANT.png&width=24)](/Arianiti "Arianiti") [Arianiti](/Arianiti "Arianiti"), [![Flag of Mataranga](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MTR.png&width=24)](/Mataranga "Mataranga") [Mataranga](/Mataranga "Mataranga"), [![Flag of Muzaka](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MZK.png&width=24)](/Muzaka "Muzaka") [Muzaka](/Muzaka "Muzaka"), [![Flag of Thopia](/thumb.php?f=Flag_THP.png&width=24)](/Thopia "Thopia") [Thopia](/Thopia "Thopia"). The rest of the Albania area is divided between [![Flag of Serbia](/thumb.php?f=Flag_SER.png&width=24)](/Serbia "Serbia") [Serbia](/Serbia "Serbia") in the north and [![Flag of Epirus](/thumb.php?f=Flag_EPI.png&width=24)](/Epirus "Epirus") [Epirus](/Epirus "Epirus") and [![Flag of Byzantium](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BYZ.png&width=24)](/Byzantium "Byzantium") [Byzantium](/Byzantium "Byzantium") in the south.
