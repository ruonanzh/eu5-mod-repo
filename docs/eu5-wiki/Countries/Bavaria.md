The once-lofty Duchy of Bavaria was a bastion of economic and diplomatic power, with its enviable location and gifted population. But despite its bright past, it now lies in pieces, divided and weak. We must reunite its former territories and reclaim the glories of the past if we wish to be more than a mere footnote in history!
