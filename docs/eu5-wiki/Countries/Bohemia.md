The lands of Bohemia are in the heart of the Germanic [Holy Roman Empire](/Holy_Roman_Empire "Holy Roman Empire"), and inhabited by Slavic people. After a long period under the Přemyslid dynasty, the current de Luxembourg rulers may drastically change matters by tightening the country's existing ties with the Empire, although King Jehan the Blind de Luxembourg is more French than German, having been educated in the royal court of Paris. Keeping both the [![Estate nobility.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_nobles.png&width=24)](/Nobility "Nobility") [Nobility](/Nobility "Nobility") and the [![Estate commoners.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_peasants.png&width=24)](/Commoners "Commoners") [Commoners](/Commoners "Commoners") satisfied under a foreign King, while navigating the political intricacies of the Holy Roman Empire, would prove a hurdle for anybody. Fortunately, Bohemia is a strong Kingdom, but will it succeed in its ambitions?

## Strategy

Around the year 1400 (+/- a few years) Hussite will spawn and you'll face a major crisis. Shortly after its appearance, an event will fire that will force the player to choose between keeping Catholicism and losing 50 stability immediately, for 10 years your estate equilibrium will be -50% along with other maluses, or converting to Hussitism and drawing repeat invasions from the Papal States backed by most of the Catholic world. Either way, Bohemia will face major internal unrest as well. This is one way you can actually LOSE the game before the end.

How do deal with this:

Try to get your stability close to 100 in the years leading up to the events. This will slow a lot of the bad stuff down. Legitimacy helps too, but it's very expensive to keep and I find it not worth the cost personally (at this point anyway).
Get some cash on hand, at least a few hundred, to allow for expenses while it's underway. For instance, you need money to replace a departed cabinet member. Plus random events keep firing, and there's parliament, etc.
Have LOTS of nearby allies: Hungary, Poland, Austria, Naples, Teutonic, France, etc. You don't need all of these, but you'll probably need a few. You will have multiple civil-war like revolts where most of your country will rebel. So you're facing 15-20k levies and you have very little. Your subjects won't be enough, even if the AI were actually smart enough to stack them (which it isn't). You need allies that can spawn a large enough to take on the rebels and recapture the territory; multiple times! Plus, there are other smaller revolts that will spawn and you'll need help there. It's probably worth being friends to all of your big neighbors until after this crisis has passed. Then consider invading Poland, Hungary, Austria, etc.
Don't plan on starting any wars while it's underway, and for a couple of years after.
