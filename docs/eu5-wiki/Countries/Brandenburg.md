Brandenburg

![Brandenburg.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BRA.png&width=128)

The Heartland of the Holy Roman Empire has endured the long period of the Great Interregnum, and looks to the future with grave concern. In the face of Holy Roman Emperor Ludwig IV. von Wittelsbach's advancing age, the families of von Wittelsbach, de Luxembourg, and von Habsburg once more prepare for competition over the crown. While Imperial authority south of the Alps is deteriorating, German merchants scramble to secure their trade routes into Italy, as the Duchy of Hansa relish their dominance over the region of Northern Germany.

The County of Brandenburg was established as the Northern March in the Slavic Wends' territory. The region features loamy uplands and depressions with abundant water and pine trees, heat, and dry, sandy soil, earning it the nickname 'the sandbox of the Holy Roman Empire'.

In 1157, after claiming these lands from Jaxa of Köpenick, Elector Albrecht the Bear Askanier officially became Elector. Initially limited to Havelland and Zauche, he encouraged the Ostsiedlung's process towards the Neumark east of the Oder, gradually incorporating it into his possessions. Colonists coming from Flanders and the Rhineland were invited to settle, fortifying their towns in the process.

After his death in 1170, the Askanier dynasty continued this expansion for over 150 years, acquiring neighboring regions like the Oder Lagoon and the Uckermark, which expanded their influence to the Baltic Sea. Unfortunately, this led to conflict with Denmark. However, the last Askanier, Elector Henrik II., died without a direct heir in 1320.

Now, the von Wittelsbach dynasty has arrived, but their lack of interest in ruling these lands casts a shadow over the future of the Electorate of Brandenburg.

**Brandenburg** is a Central European member of the [Holy Roman Empire](/Holy_Roman_Empire "Holy Roman Empire") existing at the start of the game in 1337.

## Starting position

Despite being larger than many other German states and being one of seven Electors of the empire, Brandenburg in 1337 is in a very difficult position. With the extinction of the Brandenburgish branch of the House of Askanier, Brandenburg has come under the rule of the House of Wittelsbach, whose neglect has left it greatly weakened. At the beginning of the game, the Count of Brandenburg is Ludwig von Wittelsbach, son of the Holy Roman Emperor. Brandenburg begins in a personal union with [![Flag TES.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TES.png&width=24)](/File%3AFlag_TES.png) [Allstedt](/Allstedt "Allstedt"), and with [![Flag SOR.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_SOR.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_SOR.png) [Lusatia](/Lusatia "Lusatia") as a fiefdom. They also begin in an alliance with [![Flag DAN.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_DAN.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_DAN.png) [Denmark](/Denmark "Denmark").

Historically, a member of the House of Hohenzollern became the Elector of Brandenburg in 1411. Under the Hohenzollerns, Brandenburg would grow to become the core of the Kingdom of [![Flag PRU.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_PRU.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_PRU.png) [Prussia](/Prussia "Prussia"), which famously became one of Europe's most formidable great powers due to their military prowess. Though it did so after the game's end date in 1837, Prussia eventually succeeded in unifying [![Flag GER.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GER.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_GER.png) [Germany](/Germany "Germany").

## Strategy

### General opening strategies

Creating a vassal to manage your east provinces where control is low will help with your balance at the start of the game.

You will, within the first few ticks, trigger the **Expand the Tangermunde Castle** event and, with the prestige reward, allowing you to instantly ask the Emperor to increase your country rank to duchy.

Denmark and Meissen are two viable allies. Joining the wars they call you into will let you build favors which you can then use to call them into your wars. Most of the time, they will have enough levies to deal with their own wars so you will not need to raise your own.

Be careful with annexing too much land, as antagonism can build up quickly when annexing lands in the HRE and a coalition will form. There is a "border dispute" event that appears every 15 years that will give you an "annex" casus belli on one of your northern neighbors, giving a -50% annexation cost. Focus on creating vassals instead of annexing land directly as control will be low. When you have dealt with the Brandenburg disaster, you will be able to start increasing your overall control so you can start annexing vassals.

It is not necessary to become emperor, as your goal is not to unify the HRE, and you will not have enough manpower and "influence" to fight off France and other countries taking land in the HRE. It is recommended instead to choose a country you can have good relations with to become emperor. This is very important later on when forming Prussia, as having good relations with the emperor will let you form Prussia and become a Kingdom (tier 3). If relations aren't high, you will still be able to form Prussia, but will stay a duchy (tier 2).
