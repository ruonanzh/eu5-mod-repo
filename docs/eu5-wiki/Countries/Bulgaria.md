**Bulgaria** is a European kingdom in the Balkan Peninsula, existing at the beginning of the game in 1337.

## Starting position

Though the [![Flag BYZ.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BYZ.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_BYZ.png) [Byzantine Empire](/Byzantium "Byzantium") had succeeded in reconquering the lands of the First Bulgarian Empire just over three hundred years ago, their decline in the following centuries gave the Bulgarians ample opportunity to form another empire in 1185. The Second Bulgarian Empire is past its zenith, but it remains an influential force in the Balkans. Neighbors like [![Flag WAL.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_WAL.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_WAL.png) [Wallachia](/Wallachia "Wallachia"), [![Flag SER.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_SER.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_SER.png) [Serbia](/Serbia "Serbia"), a fractured [![Flag MOL.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MOL.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_MOL.png) [Moldavia](/index.php?title=Moldavia&action=edit&redlink=1 "Moldavia (page does not exist)"), and the chronically deteriorating Byzantines can serve as useful allies or potential avenues for expansion. Nevertheless, Bulgaria may also have to face powerful opponents in the near future. In the north, the powerful kingdom of [![Flag HUN.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HUN.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_HUN.png) [Hungary](/Hungary "Hungary") historically maintained interests in the Balkans, while the nascent [![Flag TUR.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TUR.png&width=26)](/File%3AFlag_TUR.png) [Ottoman Beylik](/Ottomans "Ottomans") is poised to expand rapidly in all directions, including into Bulgaria.

At the beginning of the campaign, Bulgaria is the leader and sole member of the [![IO autocephalous patriarchate.png](/thumb.php?f=IO_autocephalous_patriarchate.png&width=24)](/File%3AIO_autocephalous_patriarchate.png) [Autocephalous Patriarchate](/Autocephalous_Patriarchate "Autocephalous Patriarchate") of Tarnovo.
