In 1337, Burgundy is an Appanage of [![Flag of France](/thumb.php?f=Flag_FRA.png&width=24)](/France "France") [France](/France "France") under the house of de Bourgogne. It is in a marriage union with [![Flag of Artois](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ARS.png&width=24)](/Artois "Artois") [Artois](/index.php?title=Artois&action=edit&redlink=1 "Artois (page does not exist)") and [![Flag of Franche-Comté](/thumb.php?f=Flag_FCB.png&width=24)](/Franche-Comt%C3%A9 "Franche-Comté") [Franche-Comté](/index.php?title=Franche-Comt%C3%A9&action=edit&redlink=1 "Franche-Comté (page does not exist)"). It takes part in the Hundred-Years War Situation.

## Strategy

![Strategy](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/5/54/Information.png/36px-Information.png "Strategy") *The below is **one of many** player suggested strategies for **Burgundy**. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.*

(For version 1.2) The initial goal of Burgundy has to be to escape the French stranglehold while securing as much of the French minors as possible in preparation. The first step is to get permission to attack other French vassals and to use Parliament to get CB's. Do not try to take territory, possibly with the exception of [![Flag of Nevers](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NEV_flanders.png&width=24)](/Nevers "Nevers") [Nevers](/index.php?title=Nevers&action=edit&redlink=1 "Nevers (page does not exist)"), rather try to take whole states and release them as vassals again. [![Flag of Nevers](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NEV_flanders.png&width=24)](/Nevers "Nevers") [Nevers](/index.php?title=Nevers&action=edit&redlink=1 "Nevers (page does not exist)") and [![Flag of Flanders](/thumb.php?f=Flag_JUL.png&width=24)](/Flanders "Flanders") [Flanders](/Flanders "Flanders") should be the main target, though Flanders might need two wars. In this case, rebels will probably spawn. After winning against them use the cores they created to release a vassal. [![Flag of Bourbon](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BOU.png&width=24)](/Bourbon "Bourbon") [Bourbon](/index.php?title=Bourbon&action=edit&redlink=1 "Bourbon (page does not exist)"), [File:Marche.png](/index.php?title=Special:Upload&wpDestFile=Marche.png "File:Marche.png") [Marche](/index.php?title=Marche&action=edit&redlink=1 "Marche (page does not exist)") and [![Flag of Forez](/thumb.php?f=Flag_FRZ.png&width=24)](/Forez "Forez") [Forez](/index.php?title=Forez&action=edit&redlink=1 "Forez (page does not exist)") are also possible targets. Note that it is impossible to attack into the HRE even then and no-CB wars are also not possible, so Parliament is initially your only choice.

Joining the HRE is possible with itinerant court and the small strip of land south of Dijon that is part of the HRE. Move your court there and you are part of the HRE. Voila.

Getting independence from France requires you to be disloyal. Use cabinet members to saw disloyality and use the Hundred-Years actions to betray France so that you get enough of it can start an independent movement. Ideally you have improved with enough countries as France has a massive advantage become so close to you. [![Flag of Castile](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CAS_castile_leon.png&width=24)](/Castile "Castile") [Castile](/Castile "Castile"), [![Flag of Bohemia](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BOH.png&width=24)](/Bohemia "Bohemia") [Bohemia](/Bohemia "Bohemia") or [![Flag of Austria](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HAB.png&width=24)](/Austria "Austria") [Austria](/Austria "Austria") (as HRE Emperor) and [![Flag of Hungary](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HUN.png&width=24)](/Hungary "Hungary") [Hungary](/Hungary "Hungary") should be feasible. The more you add the more are willing to join, so securing your first is the most difficult. Don't try [![Flag of England](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ENG_monarchy.png&width=24)](/England "England") [England](/England "England"), France might attack them and then you are in a war where England is typically not very useful.

Congratulations, you have done the hard part. Now save enough money to ensure that you can convert your PUs (once both your mother and father have passed away the marriage union turns into a PU) into one where your members actually support you. Integration will take time and it might be wiser to focus on your other vassals first. Do not forget to accept the various cultures of your conquered territories, you won't get cores otherwise (very unlike from EU 4).
