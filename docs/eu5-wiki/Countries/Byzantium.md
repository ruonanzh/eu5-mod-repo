![Byzantium.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BYZ.png&width=128)

Once a proud Empire that stretched from Egypt and the Levant to Iberia and Italy, Byzantium now faces a period of almost inexorable decline caused by the shock of the Fourth Crusade, even after the recovery of Kōnstantinoúpolis by the Palaiologos. During his reign, Basileus Andrónikos II Palaiologos saw fit to dissolve several key institutions, such as the [Navy](/Navy "Navy"), in an attempt to save the dying economy of the country, while the [![Government reform theme system.png](/thumb.php?f=Government_reform_theme_system.png&width=24)](/File%3AGovernment_reform_theme_system.png)[![Theme System](/thumb.php?f=Frame_2_er.png&width=24)](/Government_reform#Theme_System "Theme System") Theme System continued to be a shadow of what it was, as the Empire lost territory across all fronts.
His successor, Basileus Andrónikos III Palaiologos, is now faced with the difficult task of safeguarding what remains of our once-glorious Empire. To our east, [![Flag of Ottomans](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TUR.png&width=24)](/Ottomans "Ottomans") [Ottomans](/Ottomans "Ottomans") have started amassing a large army to wipe out our remaining holdings in Anatolia, threatening to set foot across the straits. At the same time, King Stefan Uroš IV Dušan Nemanjić eyes Thessaloníki and Northern Epirus like a vulture. Meanwhile, our treasury runs dry of precious coin, and the country may yet be thrown into a perilous civil war, as the family of our Basileus conspires with the [![Estate nobles.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_nobles.png&width=24)](/File%3AEstate_nobles.png) [Dynatoí](/Nobility "Nobility") behind closed doors. Can the Basileus lift this once-great nation from the ashes?

**Byzantium**, also called the *Eastern Roman Empire*, is a declining empire in southeastern Europe. As of 1337, their holdings are limited to most of the southern Balkans and four [![Province.png](/thumb.php?f=Province.png&width=24)](/File%3AProvince.png) [provinces](/Province "Province") in Anatolia - one of which is an exclave. They are in an alliance with [![Flag of Trebizond](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TRE.png&width=24)](/Trebizond "Trebizond") [Trebizond](/Trebizond "Trebizond") and have [![Flag of Epirus](/thumb.php?f=Flag_EPI.png&width=24)](/Epirus "Epirus") [Epirus](/Epirus "Epirus") as a vassal.

## Formation

Byzantium can be formed by any [![Religion orthodox.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion_orthodox.png&width=24)](/Orthodox "Orthodox") [Orthodox](/Orthodox "Orthodox") country with a Greek [![Primary culture.png](/thumb.php?f=Primary_culture.png&width=24)](/Primary_culture "Primary culture") primary culture who owns the [![Location.png](/thumb.php?f=Location.png&width=24)](/Location "Location") [location](/Location "Location") of Constantinople and at least 50% of the Balkan and Anatolia [![Region.png](/thumb.php?f=Region.png&width=24)](/File%3ARegion.png) [regions](/Region "Region").[[1]](#cite_note-1)

### Formation

:   *The glorious Byzantine Empire is long gone, but as long as Greeks live in Kōnstantinoúpolis, there is hope of its resurrection. Like a phoenix swirling back to life from a pile of ashes, so too must the remnants of Eastern Rome. But before we can reunite into a vital empire, we must quell internal dissent and ensure we are all working towards the same goal. After all, we can take the place of the Byzantine Empire in spirit, but we cannot take its name until we prove worthy of it...*

#### Conditions

* Is not [![Flag of Trebizond](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TRE.png&width=24)](/Trebizond "Trebizond") [Trebizond](/Trebizond "Trebizond")
* State culture is in the Greek culture group
* State religion is [![Religion orthodox.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion_orthodox.png&width=24)](/File%3AReligion_orthodox.png) Orthodox
* Owns location Constantinople (5877)
* Owns 50% of locations within the following regions:
  + Balkans
  + Anatolia

#### Effects

* If capital is not Constantinople or Rome, sets capital to Constantinople
* Sets country rank to Empire
* If [![DLC Fate of the Phoenix.png](/thumb.php?f=DLC_Fate_of_the_Phoenix.png&width=24)](/File%3ADLC_Fate_of_the_Phoenix.png) [Fate of the Phoenix](/Fate_of_the_Phoenix "Fate of the Phoenix") is enabled:
  + Unlocks Restore Roman Borders casus belli

### Formation (Trebizond)

:   *We of the Komnenos line are the rightful heirs to the throne the Latins stole in 1204. From our refuge upon the Pontic shore the imperial flame yet burns. Reclaim Kōnstantinoúpolis and rally the Greek lands under our banner, and the Roman Empire shall rise once more from Trebizond.*

#### Conditions

* Is [![Flag of Trebizond](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TRE.png&width=24)](/Trebizond "Trebizond") [Trebizond](/Trebizond "Trebizond")
* [![Flag of Byzantium](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BYZ.png&width=24)](/Byzantium "Byzantium") Byzantium does not exist
* State culture is in the Greek culture group
* State religion is [![Religion orthodox.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion_orthodox.png&width=24)](/File%3AReligion_orthodox.png) Orthodox
* Owns location Constantinople (5877)
* Owns 15% of locations within the following regions:
  + Balkans
  + Anatolia

#### Effects

* If capital is not Constantinople or Rome, sets capital to Constantinople
* Sets country rank to Empire
* Unlocks the government reforms Reformed Kritai Katholikoi and Modern Imperial Army
* Unlocks the policies Stavraton Coins, Komnénian Prónoia, Palaiológian Hereditary Prónoia, and Reformed Prónoia System
* If [![DLC Fate of the Phoenix.png](/thumb.php?f=DLC_Fate_of_the_Phoenix.png&width=24)](/File%3ADLC_Fate_of_the_Phoenix.png) [Fate of the Phoenix](/Fate_of_the_Phoenix "Fate of the Phoenix") is enabled:
  + Researches Byzantine Bureaucracy advance
  + Unlocks Restore Roman Borders casus belli

## Strategy

![Strategy](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/5/54/Information.png/36px-Information.png "Strategy") *The below is **one of many** player suggested strategies for **Byzantium**. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.*

This strategy assumes you have the Fate of the Phoenix DLC, but the majority of this advice should still apply without it.

Like with most countries, there's a lot to do before even unpausing the game. Your immediate problem as Byzantium is the economy. You begin the game in hundreds of ducats of debt, with a large deficit, and high inflation. If you don't change this quickly, you'll find yourself bankrupt before the end of the 1340s, which is likely a death sentence for the empire.

1. Open your economy panel and click on the Trading tab, the majority of your expenses are Trade Expenses which is due to the game starting with highly unprofitable fine cloth trade so quickly right click to cancel the trade
2. Return to your balance tab, put every slider on the minimum maintenance and stop minting, your first goal will be to reduce inflation to at least 5%. Make sure to automate your taxs and set the tax estate satisfaction equilibrium target to 30% for all estates and 1% for the nobles to make sure you are taxing the most money possible as soon as you can
   * Legitmacy is not an issue early on, you cannot have either of the low legitimacy disasters during a disaster so use the extra ducats you would have spent on cost of court on paying off your debt or building silk/ tailors guilds in Constantinople, also be very careful that your peasants do not go below 30% satisfaction or you will lose most of your levies
3. Disband both your army and navy, delete all your forts with the exception of Constantinople and Kalipolis, and from the production panel turn off every building which does not generate money aside from marketplaces
4. Click on the government panel, click on the clergy estate actions and redeem the +15 government power, use it to add 3 estate privileges: Primacy of the nobility, Strengthened Faith, and Common Militias
5. Use one of your cabinet slots to start encouraging the communalism societal value with the rest on integrating land
   * In combination with the expel the sick action during the black death this should push communinalism to 100 granting -50% revoke privilege cost, in combination with a corrupt advisor this can substainially reduce the stability cost to remove the corruption priviligies which provide the biggest roadblock to taxing your estates
6. Now that your budget should be sorted click on the ottomans and declare a no casus beli war, make sure stability is above -50
   * The ottomans become a large issue the longer they remain, therefore your goal should be to annex them before they grow too large
7. Once unpaused fully annex the ottomans then focus on developing your economy increasing your monthly income

After these opening moves and the war with the ottomans your main focus will be to increase your monthly income by building rgos and urban buildings near Constantinople to eventually sustain the cost to reach 95 legitmacy and remove the corruption privileges starting with the nobles. The Byzantine Succession Crisis will put you at risk of a civil war, but so long as you keep your estates happy, you'll likely completely avoid this. The Fate of the Phoenix wont really impact you during the actual disaster, so you don't have to react to it immediately. Your economy will remain in poor condition for quite a bit of the early game, but as long as you aren't losing ducats, its ok. Should the civil war become unavoidable click on the pretender rebellion and accept their demands, you will be given a small debuff but fighting such a civil war is far more costly. Additionally your next goals will be to conquer your core territories in Anatolia however do not over extend into Anatolia as early on the terrain of inner Anatolia is difficult to control raising your economic base and by extension cost of court.

To end the Fate of the Phoenix disaster, you have to make sure no Turkish Beylik controls more than 100 locations after 1400 and you have more than 150 locations

Your greatest threats will be from the north namely Serbia and Bulgaria who combined have more levies than you. To the west Naples may also cause issues but is not as directly a threat so long as you prevent any large landings of troops, they only have a few transport ships therefore only small portions of their army can be transported at a time.

After the fate of the phoenix Eygpt should be your next major conquest however fighting the mamluks currently is very difficult but the ai can be exploited

1. Before fighting the Mamluks make sure there is a buffer state between you and the Mamluks who won't give the Mamluks military access
   * This can be accomplished easily by creating a vassal then releasing it ideally with orthodox as its religion
2. In preparation create a fleet larger than what the mamluks have
   * This is easier the earlier in the campaign you attempt this, ideally a little after the end of the fate of the phoenix in 1410-1450 before the Mamluks start construction Heavy Ships
3. Create a tiny vassal in anatolia then click on the mamluks, transfer the subject to them
4. Using a parliament casus beli targeting your former vassal declare on the mamluks and quickly occupy the location then wipe out whatever fleet the Mamluks have and begin blockading all their ports
   * Do not use the restore roman boarders casus beli, you will not be able to prevent the defender ticking which can allow the Mamluks to force a white peace
5. wait until their war enthusiasm ticks down, dont expect much from the peace deal even at zero war enthuisasm given the low war score so quickly landing and occupying land in the levant can help gain even a little more territory
   * Currently war enthusiasm decreases by 1% every month with no limit so this is by no means a quick method
6. repeat this process slowly conquering parts of upper and lower eygpt to hopefully reduce their amount of levies to a comparable number
   * Ideally attempt to split upper and lower Eygpt first than cut off lower Eygpt from the Levant, by then the Mamluks shouldnt be able to control many locations and therefore have much less levies than normal

After fate of the phoenix your other major goal should be winning the Italian Wars for their substantial rewards. After 1450 if any great power declares war in Italy the situation will begin, at the beginning of the situation all foreign leagues are created once the Balkan league only forms for **christian nations,** if you are Hellenist at the start of the situation you **will not** form the Balkan league and will be ineligible to win the situation unless you move your capital to Italy and join/create an Italian league. By now your economy should be able to handle some professional armies which is recommended to prevent constant transportation of levies across the Mediterranean
