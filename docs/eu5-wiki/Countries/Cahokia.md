**[Capital](/Capital "Capital")**

Cahokia

A Settled Tribal country of County rank that on game start straddles the border of the Illinois and Missouri areas in North America.

## Start

Cahokia is a settled minor tribe located on the North American continent, which historically collapsed prior to European contact and is therefore based only on archaeological sources.

On 1 June 1337, the Sunset of Cahokia event triggers, allowing the player to choose between Exodus of Cahokia (historical) or Preservation of Cahokia. Each unlocks new events, though those for Preservation are generally stronger. Both enable the New Golden Age for Cahokia event, despite only the Preservation option mentioning the requirements. It is important to note that as this is an event chain and not a situation, this is the only time you will be told those requirements. Regardless, achieving the Golden Age is highly recommended as, besides turning Cahokia into a city for free, it also prevents the Shadow of Cahokia event which triggers 100 years of depopulation. The most difficult part of the requirement will be the 50 prestige, which requires aggressively turning as many nearby societies of pops into your tributaries as possible.

Tin and Copper are not visible within the player's maps; stealing maps from the Lakota people allow you to go west (High Plains, Ali Shon, and Southwest to the coast).
Stealing maps from Oklahoman tribes as well allows for the exploration of the Gulf of Mexico and the discovery of Mesoamerica which may enable the acquisition of legalism. Copper can be found north along some of the unexplored regions of the Great Lakes. By using Settle Frontier until the locations have 50% plus Cahokian residents and then switching to standard colony charters, the player may populate west while also getting RGOs otherwise unable to build until European colonization, such as Iron. Using Settle Frontier only will take 10 years and will NOT guarantee an RGO.

The tribes to the south along the Ohio river hold highly populated land well connected to the Cahokia market. These pops, and the territory they hold, can be acquired by declaring war with a Tribal Feud CB, subjugating the pops in each province, and then accepting (not tolerating) the cultures of the local areas. After this is complete, these areas can be easily added by starting a colonial charter on them.
