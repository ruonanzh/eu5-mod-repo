![Carthage.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CTH.png&width=128)

The foul dogs of the Roman Empire burned the Carthaginian homeland to the ground, trying to erase every single memory of this ancient civilization. Gone may be their identity, culture and religion, but as long as a single Roman pretender exists, so does the need for a foil to these charlatans. We shall become the scourge to all those fools who glorify the Romans; we shall become the Heirs of Carthage!

## Formation

### Requirements

* Controls the Location of Tunis
* Controls 75% of the locations in the areas of Tunis, Tripoli, Sicily, and Al-Maġrib al-Awsat
* Game rules allow for ahistorical nations

### Effects

* If capital is not Tunis, set it to Tunis
* Rename Tunis to *Qart-ḥadašt*
