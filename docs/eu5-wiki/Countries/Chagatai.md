![Chagatai.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CHG.png&width=128)

The great Khān forged an empire of unparalleled strength and ambition for all Mongol peoples, bringing into his fold great swathes of land from Europe to China. People everywhere learned to fear the sound of the Mongol armies galloping through, and the might and power of the Great Khān.

As heirs of this legacy, our dream of restoring the glory of the greatest Mongol Horde burns passionately within us all.

Chagatai Khan was the son of Genghis Khān, and therefore the heir to his legacy and empire.

Chagatai's situation at the center of the different Mongol states is of great importance, as the Silk Road crosses through our lands, and many of its most important cities are under our control. That puts Chagatai in the best position to control trade and communication between the other heirs of the Great Khān. And if any of those other heirs objects, we shall deal with them... decisively.

Chagatai is one of the four successor states of the [![Flag of Mongol Empire](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MGE.png&width=24)](/Mongol_Empire "Mongol Empire") [Mongol Empire](/Mongol_Empire "Mongol Empire") and one of three that are intact in 1337.
