Croatia is a small [![Religion catholic.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion_catholic.png&width=24)](/Catholic "Catholic") [Catholic](/Catholic "Catholic") kingdom in the western Balkans that is currently the junior partner in a personal union with [![Flag of Hungary](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HUN.png&width=24)](/Hungary "Hungary") [Hungary](/Hungary "Hungary"). The historic territory of Croatia is divided between Croatia itself and their Hungarian partner.

## Formation

Croatia is considered a *plausible* formable and as such, if annexed, can be reformed by any country with a [Croatian](/Culture#Croatian "Culture") [![Primary culture.png](/thumb.php?f=Primary_culture.png&width=24)](/Primary_culture "Primary culture") primary culture who owns the Zagreb [![Location.png](/thumb.php?f=Location.png&width=24)](/Location "Location") [location](/Location "Location") and at least 50% of locations in the Croatia and Slavonia [![Area.png](/thumb.php?f=Area.png&width=24)](/File%3AArea.png) [areas](/Area "Area").[[1]](#cite_note-1)
