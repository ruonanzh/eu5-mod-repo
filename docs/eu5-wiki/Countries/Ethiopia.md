The Ethiopian Empire is an ancient land rich with history and traditions. Nestled in the Horn of Africa, it boasts lush highlands, windswept plateaus, and the incomparable Great Rift Valley. Newcomers are captivated by its beauty, entranced by the golden sunsets against the Simien Mountains.

Ethiopia's enchanting tapestry weaves together diverse ethnic groups like the Amhara, Afar, and Tigre. United by trials, Ethiopa remains resilient like an ancient baobab tree, enduring through time.

Inspired by these ancient origins, Ethiopia cherishes legendary unions like that of the Queen of Sheba and King Solomon. The legacy of their son Menelik, the first member of the Solomon dynasty, echoes through the ages, connecting people of this land to the ancient Israelites.

Through the years, Ethiopia's epic tale unfolds, shaped by destiny and the unwavering spirit of its people. Triumphs and tribulations weave a rich tapestry of life's ebb and flow.

However, this nation is a realm encircled by adversaries who eagerly await any opportunity to strike. Managing not only to survive, but thrive, will be a challenge.

Ethiopia is an empire situated in the Horn of Africa that begins the game as the strongest of the Miaphysite countries, but it is precariously close to several powerful adversaries, most notably the [![Flag of Mamlūk Egypt](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MAM.png&width=24)](/Maml%C5%ABk_Egypt "Mamlūk Egypt") [Mamlūk Sultanate of Egypt](/Maml%C5%ABk_Egypt "Mamlūk Egypt") and [![Flag of Yemen](/thumb.php?f=Flag_YEM.png&width=24)](/Yemen "Yemen") [Yemen](/Yemen "Yemen"). At the start of the game, Ethiopia has five vassals: [![Flag of Ifat](/thumb.php?f=Flag_IFA.png&width=24)](/Ifat "Ifat") [Ifat](/index.php?title=Ifat&action=edit&redlink=1 "Ifat (page does not exist)"), [![Flag of Hadiya](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HDY.png&width=24)](/Hadiya "Hadiya") [Hadiya](/index.php?title=Hadiya&action=edit&redlink=1 "Hadiya (page does not exist)"), [![Flag of Dawaro](/thumb.php?f=Flag_DAW.png&width=24)](/Dawaro "Dawaro") [Dawaro](/index.php?title=Dawaro&action=edit&redlink=1 "Dawaro (page does not exist)"), [![Flag of Ennarea](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ENN.png&width=24)](/Ennarea "Ennarea") [Ennarea](/index.php?title=Ennarea&action=edit&redlink=1 "Ennarea (page does not exist)"), and [![Flag of Bale](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BLE.png&width=24)](/Bale "Bale") [Bale](/index.php?title=Bale&action=edit&redlink=1 "Bale (page does not exist)").

## Formation

If Ethiopia ceases to exist, it can be re‑formed by any country that meets the following requirements.

* The [![Religion.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion.png&width=24)](/Religion "Religion") [religion](/Religion "Religion") of the country must be [![Religion miaphysite.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion_miaphysite.png&width=24)](/Miaphysite "Miaphysite") [Miaphysite](/index.php?title=Miaphysite&action=edit&redlink=1 "Miaphysite (page does not exist)").
* The [![Primary culture.png](/thumb.php?f=Primary_culture.png&width=24)](/Primary_culture "Primary culture") Primary culture must be either:
  + Agaw
  + Amhara
  + Tigre
  + Tigrinya

* The country must own at least one of the following [![Location.png](/thumb.php?f=Location.png&width=24)](/File%3ALocation.png) [locations](/Location "Location"):
  + Axum
  + Lalibela
  + Gonder
