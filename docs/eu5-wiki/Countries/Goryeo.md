After the national unification was achieved, the glorious formation of the Kingdom of Goryeo brought about a golden period of prosperity and unprecedented unity to its people. Art, literacy, diplomacy, and unwavering faith flourished during this legendary time, forever changing the cultural landscape of Korea.

Even after bending to kublai\_khan, Goryeo continues to be an significant power, both due to its highly educated population and its mighty naval force.

Now the winds of change are blowing, as the corruption of Buddhism ideals and the galvanizing, revolutionary ideas of Neo-Confucianism are changing minds all over the country. Will Goryeo transform into the strongest soldier in China's army, or will it break free from those shackles and dictate its future?

**Goryeo** is the largest state in the Korean Peninsula in 1337 and a vassal of [![Flag of Yuán](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CHI_Yuan.png&width=24)](/Yu%C3%A1n "Yuán") [Yuán](/Yu%C3%A1n "Yuán").
