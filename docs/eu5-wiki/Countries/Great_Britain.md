By uniting our political development domestically and extending our influence beyond our borders, we will be able to reinforce our position in world affairs and transform into one of the foremost European powers, the British Empire.

**Great Britain** is a Tier III Formable Country located in the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Region.png&width=30)](/File%3ARegion.png "Traditions") British [region](/Region "Region") of the [subcontinent](/Subcontinent "Subcontinent") of Western Europe.

## Formation

* Own 176 locations in the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Region.png&width=30)](/File%3ARegion.png "Traditions") British region.
* [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=30)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") Primacy culture group is British
* The Hundred Years' War Situation has ended.

## Scripted content

*Main article: [English content](/English_content "English content")*

Great Britain has access to 12 unique advances, 9 unique government reforms and 3 policies, all of which are also available for [![Flag of England](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ENG_monarchy.png&width=24)](/England "England") [England](/England "England"). Great Britain's unique content guides the player through key themes like internal political reforms and the rise of institutions like Parliament and the Royal Navy. Mechanically, Great Britain's unique content pushes the player towards naval dominance, trade, and gradual state modernization. Its advances and reforms emphasize things like stronger fleets, improved administration, and elite infantry.
