![Greece.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GRE.png&width=128)

Since the earliest days of our civilization, Hellas has been divided, whether internally by its many competing kingdoms, or occupied by foreigners at the hands of our common enemies. Over the centuries, our power dwindled as we were conquered, split up, and our treasures taken by conquerors. It is an insult to our extensive legacy of art, sport, and invention to exist in such a fragmented state! To unite the Greek lands under one flag and one ruler is our only path back to the greatness of our past!

**Greece** is a Tier II Formable Country located in the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Region.png&width=30)](/File%3ARegion.png "Traditions") Balkan [region](/Region "Region") of the [subcontinent](/Subcontinent "Subcontinent") of Eastern Europe.
