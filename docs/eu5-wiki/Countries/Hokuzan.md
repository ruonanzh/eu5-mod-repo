Located southwest of Japan and northeast of Taiwan, Hokuzan is a starting country in Europa Universalis 5. It is a single-location country with County rank. It starts in 1337 with Haneji Haniji as its ruler. Its primary culture is Ryūkyū culture, its religion is Utaki, and its common language, court language, and liturgical language are all Ryūkyū. It is neighbored to the north by the unclaimed Satsunan Islands, and to the south by the counties of [![Flag of Chūzan](/thumb.php?f=Flag_KNB.png&width=24)](/Ch%C5%ABzan "Chūzan") [Chūzan](/Ch%C5%ABzan "Chūzan") and [![Flag of Nanzan](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NZN.png&width=24)](/Nanzan "Nanzan") [Nanzan](/Nanzan "Nanzan"), and the unclaimed Sakishima Islands, all of which sharing Ryūkyū culture and Utaki.
