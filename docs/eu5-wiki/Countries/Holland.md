The nations in the Low Countries have been bounded and bossed around by both the [![Flag of France](/thumb.php?f=Flag_FRA.png&width=24)](/France "France") [Kingdom of France](/France "France") or the [![IO hre.png](/thumb.php?f=Is_hre_elector.png&width=24)](/Holy_Roman_Empire "Holy Roman Empire") [Holy Roman Empire](/Holy_Roman_Empire "Holy Roman Empire") since their creation, often becoming a fief of one or the other and not rarely switching sides. Like a big prize waiting for a claimant, as one of the richest regions in Europe, the countries of the region are in a position where they have to decide whether they will become an integral part of other nations or will defend their independence with fury.
Despite starting out as a dismal swamp, the County of Holland has slowly emerged as a serious contender for regional supremacy. The arrival of the Avesnes dynasty has brought us advantageously closer to the [![Flag of Hainaut](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HAI.png&width=24)](/Hainaut "Hainaut") [County of Hainaut](/index.php?title=Hainaut&action=edit&redlink=1 "Hainaut (page does not exist)"), and our plans for expansion put the tantalizing the [![Flag of Frisia](/thumb.php?f=Flag_FRI.png&width=24)](/Frisia "Frisia") [Republic of Frisia](/Frisia "Frisia") directly in our path. However, conflict between our [![Estate nobility.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_nobles.png&width=24)](/File%3AEstate_nobles.png) Adel and our [![Estate burghers.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_burghers.png&width=24)](/File%3AEstate_burghers.png) Burgerij seem to be reigniting, which is always bad for business...

**Holland** is a location located in the North Germany [region](/Region "Region") of the [subcontinent](/Subcontinent "Subcontinent") of Western Europe. At the start of the game, Holland shares borders with [![Flag of Utrecht](/thumb.php?f=Flag_UTR.png&width=24)](/Utrecht "Utrecht") [Utrecht](/index.php?title=Utrecht&action=edit&redlink=1 "Utrecht (page does not exist)") and [![Flag of Guelders](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GEL.png&width=24)](/Guelders "Guelders") [Guelders](/Guelders "Guelders") to the east and [![Flag of Brabant](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BRB.png&width=24)](/Brabant "Brabant") [Brabant](/index.php?title=Brabant&action=edit&redlink=1 "Brabant (page does not exist)") and [![Flag of Zeeland](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ZEE.png&width=24)](/Zeeland "Zeeland") [Zeeland](/index.php?title=Zeeland&action=edit&redlink=1 "Zeeland (page does not exist)"), a [Fiefdom](/Subjects#Subject_types "Subjects") of Holland, to the south. Holland also starts in a union with [![Flag of Hainaut](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HAI.png&width=24)](/Hainaut "Hainaut") [Hainaut](/index.php?title=Hainaut&action=edit&redlink=1 "Hainaut (page does not exist)") and is a member of the [Holy Roman Empire](/International_organization#Holy_Roman_Empire "International organization").

## Unique Content

Holland has access to 9 unique advances and 1 unique government reform. As a country starting with Low Franconian culture, Holland can access the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=30)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") [Netherlandish](/Netherlandish_content "Netherlandish content") and [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=30)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") [German](/German_content "German content") culture groups content. Additionally, if you opt to form the [![Flag of Netherlands](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NED.png&width=24)](/Netherlands "Netherlands") [Netherlands](/Netherlands "Netherlands"), you can access its unique content as well. Holland's mechanics and events greatly reward developing the low countries, research, and trade. Early Holland advances focus on development, goods production, and naval power. Later game advances include literacy, naval, army, trade, artistic, crown power, and defensive perks. The [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=30)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") [German](/German_content "German content") culture group content provides access to the Landsknechte Regular Regiment, as well as river and mountain toll buildings that provide trade perks, and the Saiger Process advance that increases Silver and Lead output.

### Unique Government Reforms

Holland has access to the Dutch Shipbuilding Industry government reform, along with the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=30)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") German culture group reform Join Ruling. Holland can also access 3 additional government reforms if it forms the [![Flag of Netherlands](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NED.png&width=24)](/Netherlands "Netherlands") [Netherlands](/Netherlands "Netherlands"), which provide legislative, naval, and trade bonuses.
