As the heart of the Renaissance, our nation's culture is blossoming. We seem to have just entered a period of prolonged artistic innovation, especially in the fields of architecture and literature, and are reaping the rewards accordingly. This cultural evolution does not help us maintain peace within our borders, however, and our country is continuously stricken by foreign states seeking power and territorial gain. Perhaps it is time to unite in a decisive demonstration of our power — if for no other reason than to intimidate would-be usurpers by displaying a united front.

## Formation

* Primary culture is in the Italian culture group
* Controls 75% of the locations in the region of Italy
* Controls the locations of Milan, Florence, and Naples
* Game Rule allows for Plausible nations
