Jerusalem is the promised land, a sacred city that people of many faiths look to as the birthplace of their beliefs. Thus, a Kingdom of Heaven shall be proclaimed over this holy land. Let all who worship be amazed at our splendor!

## Formation

* Religion is Catholicism
* is either:
  + [![Flag of Cyprus](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CEP.png&width=24)](/Cyprus "Cyprus") [Cyprus](/Cyprus "Cyprus")
  + [![Flag of Naples](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NAP.png&width=24)](/Naples "Naples") [Naples](/Naples "Naples")
  + Has the [Government Reform](/Government_reform "Government reform") *Military Order*
* Controls the Location of Jerusalem
* Controls 75% of the province of Filastin
