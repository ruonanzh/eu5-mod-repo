The great Genghis Khān forged an empire of unparalleled strength and ambition for all Mongol peoples, bringing into his fold great swathes of land from Europe to China. People everywhere learned to fear the sound of the Mongol armies galloping through, and the might and power of the Great Khān.

As heirs of this legacy, our dream of restoring the glory of the greatest Mongol Horde burns passionately within us all. It was Borjigin Jochi Khān, son of Genghis Khān, and his son Borjigin Batu Khān who carried on the legacy of the Great Khān and established the Golden Horde.

Controlling the westernmost expansion of the great empire, we are the rulers of the vast steppes. Many nations in Europe are subservient to us, afraid of our legendary might. As they should be...

**Jochi** or **The Golden Horde** is a massive country in eastern Europe and northern Asia, occupying much land in the Pontic Steppe, Ural, West Siberia and Horasan [![Region.png](/thumb.php?f=Region.png&width=24)](/Region "Region") [regions](/Region "Region"). While they are large, they are surprisingly fragile and tend to fall early in the game.
