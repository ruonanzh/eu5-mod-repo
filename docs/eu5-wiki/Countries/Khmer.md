![Khmer.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_KHM.png&width=128)

The great Empire of Khmer has endured for centuries as the undisputed center of Indochina. Ever since, their conversion to Theravāda, Khmer has slowly lost its grasp on the undisputed rule of the region. Growing powers both east and west of the old capital of Angkor threaten to upset the power dynamic of all Indochina.
Burdened with the difficult upkeep of their irrigation systems and extensive temple complexes, Khmer will have to protect itself from foreign invasion to survive and reclaim its position as the hegemon of Indochina.
