Vilnius

Situated between the lands of [![Flag of Poland](/thumb.php?f=Flag_POL.png&width=24)](/Poland "Poland") [Poland](/Poland "Poland") and the mighty [![Flag of Golden Horde](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GLH.png&width=24)](/Golden_Horde "Golden Horde") [Golden Horde](/Golden_Horde "Golden Horde"), the **Grand Duchy of Lithuania** have a proud history as one of the last bastions of Paganism. Under the Gediminaitis dynasty, the Lithuanian people have tasted success by expanding their domain to envelop parts of the Kievan Rus'. However. growing pressure and an ongoing crusade spearheaded by the [![Flag of Teutonic Order](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TEU.png&width=24)](/Teutonic_Order "Teutonic Order") [Teutonic Order](/Teutonic_Order "Teutonic Order"), the [![Flag of Livonia](/thumb.php?f=Flag_LIV.png&width=24)](/Livonia "Livonia") [Livonian Order](/Livonia "Livonia") and the King of [![Flag of Bohemia](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BOH.png&width=24)](/Bohemia "Bohemia") [Bohemia](/Bohemia "Bohemia") serve as grave reminders that religious friction may yet contribute to their demise. Various attempts to embrace the [![Religion catholic.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion_catholic.png&width=24)](/File%3AReligion_catholic.png)[Catholic](/Catholicism "Catholicism") faith have been unsuccessful, and raising tensions across our borders threaten out stability. It is in this tense and precarious environment that we must determine our next steps...
