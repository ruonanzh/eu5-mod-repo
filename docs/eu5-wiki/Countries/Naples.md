The Kingdom of Naples stands at the crossroads of its own troubled history. Born from the ambitions of powerful dynasties seeking dominion over the lands of southern Italy, Naples rises as a bastion of resilience and cultural fusion that extends from ancient civilizations such as the Greeks and Romans, to the more recent dynasties of the Hauteville, di Hohenstaufen, and Anjou, each leaving an indelible mark on Neapolitan identity.

In the year 1337, the Kingdom finds itself under the rule of a visionary leader, King Robert the Wise Anjou. A wise and just ruler known for his sagacity and keen judgment, he has navigated the intricate web of political alliances with finesse. His rule has brought a period of relative peace and a welcome respite from the turbulence of the past.

What will become of Naples when his steady hand falters? What will his successor do to stave off all those who covet the Neapolitan lands? The tale of Naples's future is as yet unwritten...

**Naples** is a Neapolitan culture nation that begins as one of the [Catholic](/Catholicism "Catholicism") kingdoms of Europe in 1337. Naples is one of only two nations that can form the [![Flag of Jerusalem](/thumb.php?f=Flag_KOJ.png&width=24)](/Jerusalem "Jerusalem") [Kingdom of Jerusalem](/Jerusalem "Jerusalem") without being a Military order. At the start Naples has two vassals, [![Flag of Albania](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ALB.png&width=24)](/Albania "Albania") [Albania](/Albania "Albania") and [![Flag of Achaea](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ACH.png&width=24)](/Achaea "Achaea") [Achaea](/Achaea "Achaea").

## Strategy

![Strategy](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/5/54/Information.png/36px-Information.png "Strategy") *The below is **one of many** player suggested strategies for **Naples**. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.*

Naples has a two formables: [![Flag of Two Sicilies](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TWS_angevine.png&width=24)](/Two_Sicilies "Two Sicilies") [Two Sicilies](/index.php?title=Two_Sicilies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Two Sicilies (page does not exist)") and [![Flag of Italy](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ITA.png&width=24)](/Italy "Italy") [Italy](/Italy "Italy"). This strategy will employ how to get both along with making a lot of money.

1. Declare war on [![Flag of Sicily](/thumb.php?f=Flag_SIC.png&width=24)](/Sicily "Sicily") [Sicily](/Sicily "Sicily") and form [![Flag of Two Sicilies](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TWS_angevine.png&width=24)](/Two_Sicilies "Two Sicilies") [Two Sicilies](/index.php?title=Two_Sicilies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Two Sicilies (page does not exist)")
2. Declare war on the [![Flag of Papal States](/thumb.php?f=Flag_PAP.png&width=24)](/Papal_States "Papal States") [Papal States](/Papal_States "Papal States") to vassalize them to get 20% of their income (make sure to take their dye location)
3. Go naval value and continue growing your kingdom up the boot of Italy and profit.

## Development Trivia

### Color Change

[![Naples color old.png](/thumb.php?f=Naples_color_old.png&width=300)](/File%3ANaples_color_old.png)

During the Development of EU5 Naples originally had a purple color akin to that seen in other Paradox games, seen in the Tinto Maps posts about Italy. This was changed as due to feedback of Naples' map color in particular the color given to its subjects in [![Flag of Albania](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ALB.png&width=24)](/Albania "Albania") [Albania](/Albania "Albania") being too similar to the color of [![Flag of Byzantium](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BYZ.png&width=24)](/Byzantium "Byzantium") [Byzantium](/Byzantium "Byzantium").
