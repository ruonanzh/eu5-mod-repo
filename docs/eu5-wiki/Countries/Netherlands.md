The Dutch are emerging as one of Europe's most capable naval and colonial powers. Lucrative trade opprotunities, locally and overseas, have resulted in a swift evolution that has transformed the country into one of the first capitalist nations in history. Complementing this superlative economic acumen are the Dutch talents for innovation and creativity, which turn our culture into a light shining all over the world. A unified Dutch state, therefore, stands to be one of the wealthiest and most influential of all time.

**Netherlands** is a Tier II Formable Country located in the North Germany [region](/Region "Region") of the [subcontinent](/Subcontinent "Subcontinent") of Western Europe. The player must own 28 locations in the low countries to form the Netherlands.

## Formation

* Primary culture is in the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=24)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") Netherlandish culture group.
* Own 28 locations in the low countries.

## Scripted content

*Main article: [Dutch content](/Dutch_content "Dutch content")*

The Netherlands has access to 6 unique advances and 4 unique government reforms. The Netherlands also has access to the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=24)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") [Netherlandish](/Netherlandish_content "Netherlandish content") and [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Culture_group.png&width=24)](/File%3ACulture_group.png "Traditions") [German](/German_content "German content") culture groups content. The Netherlands unique mechanics and events focus on developing the low countries with goods production, services, and research, along with expanding trade across the globe.
