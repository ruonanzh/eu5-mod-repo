The land once conquered and Germanized by the Teutonic Order will always require protection against rapacious outside forces. But should the Order fail in this duty, we can take their place and usher in a new era of prosperity and Prussian discipline.

Prussia is a very historically relevant German formable nation on the coast of the Baltic. Two prime candidates to form Prussia are [![Flag of Brandenburg](/thumb.php?f=Flag_BRA.png&width=24)](/Brandenburg "Brandenburg") [Brandenburg](/Brandenburg "Brandenburg") and the [![Flag of Teutonic Order](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TEU.png&width=24)](/Teutonic_Order "Teutonic Order") [Teutonic Order](/Teutonic_Order "Teutonic Order").

## Formation

### Requirements

* **Culture group**: German
* Control 75% of the locations in the Area of Prussia
* At peace
* Capital not required
* Does **not** have the [Government Reform](/Government_Reform "Government Reform") *Military Order*
* Does **not** have the [Estate](/Estate "Estate") Privilege *Powerful Dioceses*
* Has the Estate Privilege *Land Rights*
* [![Estate clergy.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_clergy.png&width=20)](/File%3AEstate_clergy.png) Clergy Estate Power < 20%
* [![Estate nobles.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_nobles.png&width=20)](/File%3AEstate_nobles.png) Nobles Estate Power > 20%

### Effects

If not a [![Republic.png](/thumb.php?f=Republic.png&width=20)](/File%3ARepublic.png) Republic or [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=20)](/File%3AMonarchy.png) Monarchy:

* Change government type to Monarchy
* Gain **100** [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=20)](/File%3AMonarchy.png) [Legitimacy](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy")

If not part of the [![IO hre.png](/thumb.php?f=Is_hre_elector.png&width=20)](/File%3AIs_hre_elector.png) [Holy Roman Empire](/International_organization#Holy_Roman_Empire "International organization"):

* Set country rank to [![Rank kingdom.png](/thumb.php?f=Rank_kingdom.png&width=20)](/File%3ARank_kingdom.png) Kingdom
