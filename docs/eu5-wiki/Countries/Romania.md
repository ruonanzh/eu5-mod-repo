![Romania.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NAP_revolutionary_republic.png&width=128)

The last vestiges of the Roman Empire are gone, and although many pretenders claim the imperial crown, only we are its true descendants. When the lands of our ancestors have finally been reunited under a single flag, only then will we be rewarded with the prosperity that is our birthright. We are the children of Rome - the people Romania!
