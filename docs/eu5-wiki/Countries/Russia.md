![Russia.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_RUS.png&width=128)

Through conquest and diplomacy, Russia has managed to transform itself from a tiny settlement into a dominant power. After several expeditions in the east, the Mongol hordes have finally been subdued, and a centralized Russian state under the suzerainty of the Tsar is emerging. Influences from Western Europe are helping us modernize and reform our nation, and we are adopting a more modern style of education. Given our history of rapid growth and our modern political and cultural gifts, it seems only natural that the Russian Empire should rise and hold sway over lesser nations.

**Russia** is a [formable country](/Formable_country "Formable country") in Eastern Europe.

## Formation

### Requirements

* **Culture group**: East Slavic
* Control 50% of the locations in the Area of Russia, Ural, Pontic Steppe
* At peace
* Capital not required
* Does **not** have the [Government Reform](/Government_Reform "Government Reform") *Military Order*
* Does **not** have the [Estate](/Estate "Estate") Privilege *Powerful Dioceses*
* Has the Estate Privilege *Land Rights*
* [![Estate clergy.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_clergy.png&width=20)](/File%3AEstate_clergy.png) Clergy Estate Power < 20%
* [![Estate nobles.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_nobles.png&width=20)](/File%3AEstate_nobles.png) Nobles Estate Power > 20%

### Effects

If not a [![Republic.png](/thumb.php?f=Republic.png&width=20)](/File%3ARepublic.png) Republic or [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=20)](/File%3AMonarchy.png) Monarchy:

* Change government type to Monarchy
* Gain **100** [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=20)](/File%3AMonarchy.png) [Legitimacy](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy")
* Set country rank to [![Rank empire.png](/thumb.php?f=Rank_empire.png&width=20)](/File%3ARank_empire.png) Empire
