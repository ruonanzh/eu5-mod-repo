Our island has long been bitterly divided between our Judicates and foreign rulers. These dark times must end, and a ruler who unifies Sardinia shall govern us all, bringing peace and prosperity to our people... and war and devastation to our enemies. As they justly deserve.

Sardinia Is an Italian formable nation in the Western Mediterranean on the island of the same name. After Being formed it can go on to form [![Flag of Italy](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ITA.png&width=24)](/Italy "Italy") [Italy](/Italy "Italy").

## Formation

### Requirements

* Primary culture is Sardinian
* Controls 85% of the locations in the Area of Sardinia
