The Duchy of Saxony was once an influential stem duchy within the old empire. While its core territories have shifted over time, the title has retained its prestige and renown. If we wish to be recognized as the rightful heir to the Saxon title, we must persuade the Emperor of the Holy Roman Empire to become an elector, a role which will be given to the Duke of Saxony.
