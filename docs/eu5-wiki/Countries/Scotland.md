Scotland is once again in peril.

A mere generation ago, our national hero, King Robert de Bruce, freed us from the [![Flag of england](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ENG_monarchy.png&width=24)](/England "England") [English](/England "England") rule, alongside the famous martyr, William Wallace.

Now his successor — the bairn King David II de Bruce — lies exiled in [![Flag of France](/thumb.php?f=Flag_FRA.png&width=24)](/France "France") [France](/France "France") as our loyal nobility continues their struggle against [![Flag of Balliol](/thumb.php?f=Flag_SBL.png&width=24)](/Balliol "Balliol") [Balliol](/Balliol "Balliol"), the English king's lackey state that falsely claims the Scots crown.

Though the people of our Kingdom are currently united in our cause, this is not guaranteed to last. Embedded in our kingdom are deep cultural and societal divisions, especially between the wealthy English-speaking Scots burghs of the lowlands and the Highland people who dwell in the inland hills.

Long ago, Roman invaders built a wall against us, so fearsome was the Scots reputation. Perhaps it is time to reinvigorate that ancient ferocity...

The **Kingdom of Scotland** is a monarchy in northern Britain, ruling over its vassals [![Flag of Ross](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ROS.png&width=24)](/Ross "Ross") [Ross](/index.php?title=Ross&action=edit&redlink=1 "Ross (page does not exist)") and [![Flag of Moray](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MOY.png&width=24)](/Moray "Moray") [Moray](/index.php?title=Moray&action=edit&redlink=1 "Moray (page does not exist)"). Recently freed from [![Flag of England](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ENG_monarchy.png&width=24)](/England "England") [English](/England "England") domination, the realm once again faces an existential threat, beginning the game amid the Second War of Scottish Independence. With its rightful king displaced, a rival claimant of the [![Flag of Balliol](/thumb.php?f=Flag_SBL.png&width=24)](/Balliol "Balliol") [Balliol](/Balliol "Balliol") dynasty—backed by English power—contests the Scottish throne.
