![Siam.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_SIA.png&width=128)

The Thai people live divided by mountains and river valleys, between overlapping spheres of influence, and thus have always gravitated towards local centers of power rather than a central authority. But a unified Thai homeland would be more beneficial than our current dispersed nature — that consolidation of our power would make it much easier to lead our people to greatness!

**Siam** is a Tier III Formable Country located in the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Region.png&width=30)](/File%3ARegion.png "Traditions") Indochina [region](/Region "Region") of the [subcontinent](/Subcontinent "Subcontinent") of Southeast Asia.

## Formation

* Own 75% of the areas in the [![Culture Group](/thumb.php?f=Region.png&width=30)](/File%3ARegion.png "Traditions") Indochina region.
* **Culture group**: Thai
* **Areas**:
  + Chao Phraya
  + Northern Tai Highland
