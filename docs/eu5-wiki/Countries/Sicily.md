The Kingdom of Sicily is a country in southern Italy. In 1337 Sicily owns the whole Sicily area except the islands *Malta* which is owned by [![Flag of Salona](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MRS.png&width=24)](/Salona "Salona") [Salona](/Salona "Salona"), and *Lampedusa* and *Pantellerija* which are impassable. It is guaranteed by [![Flag of Papal States](/thumb.php?f=Flag_PAP.png&width=24)](/Papal_States "Papal States") [Papal States](/Papal_States "Papal States") and [![Flag of Aragon](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ARA.png&width=24)](/Aragon "Aragon") [Aragon](/Aragon "Aragon") and has the vassal [![Flag of Athens](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ATH.png&width=24)](/Athens "Athens") [Athens](/Athens "Athens").
