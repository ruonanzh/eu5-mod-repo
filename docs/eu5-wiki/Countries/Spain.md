Since the defeat of the Visigoths by the Muslim invasion in the 8th century, Iberia has been the seat of several independent kingdoms. Numerous would-be conquerors have since attempted to unify the region, proclaiming themselves rulers of 'Hispania,' 'Spania,' or — as it is now known — 'Spain.' But it is we who shall finally unify the Peninsula once and for all!

The Iberian Peninsula has been divided since the Muslim Invasion of the Visigothic Kingdom in 711. Should one of the Christian powers be able to unify the peninsula and defeat the last Muslims in the [![Flag of Granada](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GRA.png&width=24)](/Granada "Granada") [Emirate of Granada](/Granada "Granada") they can form Spain. Historically Spain was formed from the dynastic union between the Crowns of [![Flag of Castile](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CAS_castile_leon.png&width=24)](/Castile "Castile") [Castile](/Castile "Castile") and [![Flag of Aragon](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ARA.png&width=24)](/Aragon "Aragon") [Aragon](/Aragon "Aragon") and the conquest of the [![Flag of Granada](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GRA.png&width=24)](/Granada "Granada") [Sultanate of Granada](/Granada "Granada") and Iberian [![Flag of Navarre](/thumb.php?f=Flag_NAV.png&width=24)](/Navarre "Navarre") [Navarre](/Navarre "Navarre"). **Spain** is ideally situated to colonize the New World if formed by [![Flag of Castile](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CAS_castile_leon.png&width=24)](/Castile "Castile") [Castile](/Castile "Castile") or expand around the Mediterranean if formed by [![Flag of Aragon](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ARA.png&width=24)](/Aragon "Aragon") [Aragon](/Aragon "Aragon").

## Formation

* Iberian culture group
* Christian
* All nations in Iberia are Christian
* Own 75% locations in of Iberia region
