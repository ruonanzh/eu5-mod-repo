![Tawantinsuyu.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_INC.png&width=128)

The Altiplano is divided among many nations, each inhabiting the valleys and worshiping different gods. We are a people who speak many tongues and have diverse perspectives, yet together we represent a cultural and spiritual force few other nations could stand against. We shall bring all our scattered territories together to follow one strong leader. The sun shall cast its light over a unified realm!

**Tawantinsuyu**, also known as the Incan Empire is a major formable nation in South America

## Formation

### Requirements

* Culture has the Quechua language
* Religion is in the Andean Folk Religions group
* Controls 80% of the locations in the areas of Acamama and Puna

### Effects

* If country rank is less than [![Rank empire.png](/thumb.php?f=Rank_empire.png&width=25)](/File%3ARank_empire.png) Empire, set country rank to [![Rank empire.png](/thumb.php?f=Rank_empire.png&width=25)](/File%3ARank_empire.png) Empire
* If [Government Reform](/Government_Reform "Government Reform") *Andean Monarchy* is enacted, remove it
* If Government Reform *Reformed Tawantinsuyu Government* is **not** enacted:
  + Add Government Reform *Tawantinsuyu Monarchy*
* If not a [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=25)](/File%3AMonarchy.png) Monarchy:
  + Change government type to [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=25)](/File%3AMonarchy.png) Monarchy
  + Gain **100** [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=25)](/File%3AMonarchy.png) [Legitimacy](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy")
