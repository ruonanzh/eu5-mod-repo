Tlemcen

**Tlemcen** is a medium sized kingdom in North Africa. Tlemcen starts at war with [![Flag of Morocco](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MOR.png&width=24)](/Morocco "Morocco") [Morocco](/Morocco "Morocco") and is rivals with [![Flag of Tunis](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TUN.png&width=24)](/Tunis "Tunis") [Tunis](/Tunis "Tunis"). Tlemcen follows the [![Religious school maliki school.png](/thumb.php?f=Religious_school_maliki_school.png&width=25)](/File%3AReligious_school_maliki_school.png)Mālikī school of [![Religion sunni.png](/thumb.php?f=Religion_sunni.png&width=24)](/File%3AReligion_sunni.png)Sunnism.
