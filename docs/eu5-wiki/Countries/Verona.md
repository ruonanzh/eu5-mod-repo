## Strategy

### General opening strategies

Verona is uniquely equipped to expand rapidly due to the Scalinger war, more specifically the enforce scalinger demands treaty which resets antagonism to zero. Using this treaty means a large part of italy can be conquered within the opening years of the game with almost no antagonism if timed correctly. Your greatest challenge is Milan who alone has more levies than you, your first goal will be to separate peace them out immediately with no losses. Before unpausing begin building a spy network on Cesena or Pisa to later obtain a force into faction cb. Once Milan is out of the war your goal will be to reduce the enemy troop numbers, only fight on your forts and if Florence decides to siege Lucca this can easily wipe most of their forces out. If done quickly enough before they begin to consolidate forces you can entirely wipe out their armies piecemeal ensuring victory, however to best take advantage of the Scalinger peace deal refrain from seperating peacing any nation, instead siege down all nations still at war excluding Verona, then using your estate actions call an emergency parliment to obtain another casus beli on a neighboring power such as Aquileia or Genoa, feel free to also obtain as many as you are willing to siege. Additionally join the side either Pisa or Cesena are not in to obtain another cb from the force into faction casus beli. One by one declare war then siege out each nation before moving on but do not peace out any war until you have fully sieged every capital. Once ready peace out all seperate wars for as much land, then seperate peace every other nation in the Scalinger war other than Venice who you should only enforce Scalinger demands on as Venice as they have an event during the 2nd age to build the Piazza San Marco in Venice which provides a large development buff and remains once conquered so you should ideally wait for the event to fire before taking Venice. If you want even more land you can no casus beli several more nations or wait the 2 years for the next parliament but you run the risk have having even more unintegrated land to integrate which can lead to a large rebellion if not managed correctly

#### Guelphs and Ghibellines

Joining the Guelps will allow the player to form Italy at reduced requirements, namely not needing Milan, Florence, and Naples. However since Verona begins as a Republic you cannot become Emporer so staying in the HRE only increases the antagonism cost to expansion. Ideally the player should side with the Guelphs and work on forcing as many of the ais to flip to your faction. The progress of the situation is based on the fraction of total tax base controlled by each side, conquering neighbors reduces the total tax base and often results in slower progress than if the nation was converted to the guelphs. Some luck is involved in hoping Bohemia isnt emporer as outside of assassination there isnt a way to prevent the overwhelming size of Bohemia's tax base winning the situation for the Ghibellines.
