Established by Borjigin Kublai Huángdì, the Great Yuán now controls the vast expanse of a great Empire. Having tamed China, and thriving under Kublai Huángdì's legendary leadership, they have reached stratospheric heights of prestige and splendor.
However, their influence over the subcontinent is waning. Internal strife, instability, and cultural friction have corroded what once was prosperous and magnificent, and those who wish to dethrone the Yuán and usurp their mandate are gathering their strength. Plotting and scheming within Yuán's borders, these traitors seek to find every possible weakness within the Empire's rule, vying for scraps of power and a passing mention in the scrolls of history. But while many may struggle and die for the honor, only one leader can sit on the Dragon Throne and fulfill the will of Heaven.

The [![Flag of Yuán](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CHI_Yuan.png&width=24)](/Yu%C3%A1n "Yuán") Great Yuán dynasty is the current imperial dynasty of China in 1337 and is the greatest of all Great Powers at the beginning of the campaign. Great Yuán is the undisputed hegemon of Asia, controlling no less than 1872 locations in China proper, having a [population](/Population "Population") of over 90 million and being the leader of the [Middle Kingdom](/International_organization#Middle_Kingdom "International organization") with 158 members, of which 125 are [subjects](/Subjects "Subjects") in China, Tibet and Korea; and it is easily the largest, richest and most powerful country on Earth. On the outside, Great Yuán seems like an invincible superpower, but on the inside serious trouble is brewing, as not only does Yuán start with a serious financial deficit and abysmal [Religious Unity](/Religious_Unity "Religious Unity"), many of Yuán's subjects are disloyal, Yuán's [primary culture](/Primary_Culture "Primary Culture") accounts for a miniscule percent of its population, and it faces an imminent disaster in the form of the [Red Turban Rebellion](/Situations#Red_Turban_Rebellions "Situations") that will break out at some point between 1345 and 1350.

At the start of the game, Great Yuán is ruled by Huāngdi (Emperor) **Borjigin** **Togon Temür** ([![Adm.png](/thumb.php?f=Adm.png&width=24)](/File%3AAdm.png)**57**/[![Dip.png](/thumb.php?f=Dip.png&width=24)](/File%3ADip.png)**51**/[![Mil.png](/thumb.php?f=Mil.png&width=24)](/File%3AMil.png)**66**) who is 16 years old and has the [![Trait architectural visionary.png](/thumb.php?f=Trait_architectural_visionary.png&width=24)](/Architectural_Visionary "Architectural Visionary") Architectural Visionary trait.

## Historical background

After Genghis Khan united the Mongols in 1206, he and his successors launched several military campaigns southward into China between 1205 and 1279, culminating in the complete conquest and unification of China under Mongol rule. What first begun as a series of raids into the territory of the Western Xia in 1205-1208 eventually led to the complete conquest, subjugation and ultimately destruction of that state in 1209-1227. This was then followed by the Mongol conquest and destruction of the Great Jin in Northern China in 1211-1234, leading to the capture of Beijing in 1215. Lastly, the Mongol armies led by Möngkhe Khan and later Kublai Khan struck south and conquered and destroyed the Song dynasty and the Dali kingdom in Central and Southern China in 1234-1279. With the defeat of the Song dynasty and with the desire to rule all of China, Kublai Khan officially proclaimed the establishment of the Yuán dynasty in 1279 and became Emperor of China, as Emperor Shizu of Yuán. The Mongols throughly looted, pillaged and ransacked China during their conquest and enslaved many Chinese, placing them at the bottom of the social hierarchy. The Imperial Keju Examinations were abolished and Han Chinese were expelled from the Imperial bureacracy, as the Mongoles entrenched their rule, with ordinary people experiencing prolonged and great hardship.

After Kublai Khan's death in 1294, the Mongol Empire fractured into four successor states; the Yuán dynasty, the [![Flag of Chagatai](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CHG.png&width=24)](/Chagatai "Chagatai") [Chagatai Khanate](/Chagatai "Chagatai"), [![Flag of Jochi](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GLH.png&width=24)](/Jochi "Jochi") [Golden Horde](/Jochi "Jochi") and the Ilkhanate as a civil war broke out. In 1304, a peace treaty was signed which established the Yuán dynasty as the nominal overlord of the other khanates, with the Yuán emperor as the Khagan of the whole Mongol Empire, but this had little effect in practice, as the four successor khanates continued to operate as separate states and ultimately fell separately as well. In the Yuán dynasty's case, this came in the form of military decline and factional strife within the court, coupled with a string of natural disasters and disease outbreaks in the 1330s and 1340s, which, when coupled with growing resentment by the Han Chinse against Mongol Rule, finally culminated in the outbreak of the Red Turban Rebellion, which eventually led to the collapse of the Yuán dynasty in 1368 and its succession by the [![Flag of Ming](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CHI_Ming.png&width=24)](/Ming "Ming") [Ming dynasty](/Ming "Ming"), founded in the same year by Zhu Yuanzhang, who became the Hongwu Emperor. The remnants of the Yuán government retreated north to Karakorum and continued to exist as a remnant state in Mongolia known as the Northern Yuán until 1635 when both it and the [![Flag of Oirat](/thumb.php?f=Flag_OIR.png&width=24)](/Oirat "Oirat") [Oirat](/index.php?title=Oirat&action=edit&redlink=1 "Oirat (page does not exist)") were conquered by the Manchu-led Later Jin, which would go on to conquer China from the Ming and found the [![Flag of Qīng](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CHI.png&width=24)](/Q%C4%ABng "Qīng") [Qīng](/index.php?title=Q%C4%ABng&action=edit&redlink=1 "Qīng (page does not exist)") dynasty.

## Overview

The early game of Great Yuán will be dominated by the Red Turban Rebellion, which will erupt at some point between 1345 and 1350. In the lead-up to the actual rebellion, a series of events will occur where Yuán can choose to either gain a bonus but also increase the negative modifier of the Red Turban Rebellion or gain a lesser bonus or a penalty but reduce the negative modifier.

Once the Red Turban Rebellion triggers, Yuán will lose [![Stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") **−20** Stability and [![Legitimacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Legitimacy.png&width=24)](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy") **−20** Legitimacy and every subject will gain **100%** Liberty Desire. The Middle Kingdom international organization will also lose [![May convert vassals to celestial governors.png](/thumb.php?f=May_convert_vassals_to_celestial_governors.png&width=24)](/File%3AMay_convert_vassals_to_celestial_governors.png) **−25** Celestial Authority. In addition Yuán will gain the negative *Red Turban Rebellions* modifier, which inflicts the following penalties, modified by the choices made in prior events:

* ![Efficiency of Our Cabinet](/thumb.php?f=Country_cabinet_efficiency.png&width=24 "Efficiency of Our Cabinet") −20% Efficiency of Our Cabinet
* ![Estates Satisfaction Equilibrium](/thumb.php?f=Global_estate_target_satisfaction.png&width=24 "Estates Satisfaction Equilibrium") −5% Estates Satisfaction Equilibrium
* ![Levy Size](/thumb.php?f=Global_levy_size_modifier.png&width=24 "Levy Size") −45% Levy Size
* ![Mercenary Size](/thumb.php?f=Global_mercenaries_modifier.png&width=24 "Mercenary Size") −75% Mercenary Size
* ![Monthly Legitimacy](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_legitimacy.png&width=24 "Monthly Legitimacy") −0.05 Monthly Legitimacy
* ![Monthly Rebel Growth](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_rebel_growth.png&width=24 "Monthly Rebel Growth") −5% Monthly Rebel Growth
* ![Stability Investment](/thumb.php?f=Stability_investment.png&width=24 "Stability Investment") −0.1 Stability Investment

When the rebellion breaks out, all members of the Middle Kingdom organization (except for Yuán itself) will have a 20% chance per month to secede. When this happens, they gain an Allegiance to Yuán value, which determines who they will side with:

* States with more than **50%** Yuán Allegiance will side with Yuán and rejoin the Middle Kingdom
* States with between **50%** and **0%** Yuán Allegiance will stay neutral
* States will less than **0%** Yuán Allegiance will side with the rebels and join Red Turban Rebels

Every time a state secedes and sides with the rebels, Yuán will lose [![Stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") **−20** Stability; the first state that secedes and sides with the rebels will become the leader of the Red Turban Rebels organization and Yuán will declare war on every state that sides with the rebels. Conversely, every state that sides with the loyalists will join Yuán's wars and Yuán will gain [![Stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") **+7** Stability. During the situation, Yuán will gain access to three actions to convice states to side with it, delay imminent rebellions, improve its stability and estate statisfaction and finally call loyalist states to war by Yuán's side against the rebels.

To end the situation and achieve victory, Yuán must survive for 20 years, conquer all rebel and neutral states and have all of the following:

* [![May convert vassals to celestial governors.png](/thumb.php?f=May_convert_vassals_to_celestial_governors.png&width=24)](/File%3AMay_convert_vassals_to_celestial_governors.png) **50** Celestial Authority (Middle Kingdom organization)
* [![Legitimacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Legitimacy.png&width=24)](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy") **90** Legitimacy
* [![Stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") **30** Stability

If Yuán wins the Red Turban rebellion, then the following will occur:

* Yuán will gain:
  + [![Prestige.png](/thumb.php?f=Prestige.png&width=24)](/Prestige "Prestige") **+100** Prestige
  + [![Legitimacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Legitimacy.png&width=24)](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy") **+50** Legitimacy
  + [![Stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") **+50** Stability
  + [![May convert vassals to celestial governors.png](/thumb.php?f=May_convert_vassals_to_celestial_governors.png&width=24)](/File%3AMay_convert_vassals_to_celestial_governors.png) **+20** Celestial Authority
  + Cores on every location in the regions of West China, South China, East China, North China and Xinjiang AND the *Unify China* casus belli against every independent country within these regions.
* Yuán will lose the Red Turban Rebellions modifier
* Every state that seceded from Yuán and has 50% Yuán Allegiance will be annexed by Yuán.

## Decisions

As long as Yuán has Mongolian primary culture and a ruler of the Borjigin dynasty, it can reform the [![Flag of Mongolia](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MGO.png&width=24)](/Mongolia "Mongolia") [Mongol Empire](/index.php?title=Mongolia&action=edit&redlink=1 "Mongolia (page does not exist)") if it can reconquer most of the empire's historic territories, including;

* Owning the locations of Sarai, Kazan, Samarkand, Kaffa, Dàdū, Shàngyuán, Bāxiàn, Guǎngzhōu, and Karakorum
* Owning 85% of all locations in the regions of Mongolia, Persia, East China, North China, West China, Pontic Steppe, Russia, Khorasan, and Xinjiang

## Strategy

![Strategy](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/5/54/Information.png/36px-Information.png "Strategy") *The below is **one of many** player suggested strategies for **Yuán**. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.*

Starting situation particularities :

* Legacy of Kublai Khan [major reform](/Government_reform#Major_reforms "Government reform") is trivialised by [Middle Kingdom](/International_organization#Middle_Kingdom "International organization") leader statute bonuses so you can try to replace it by a more useful one.
* Sanjiao religion begin with 86% followers in Yuán, has 5% more literacy compared to your starting religion and give **+0.5** government power. Switching to it will also increase your clerics of the state religion bonus on stability.
* Yuán can't go through "Crisis of the dynasty XXX" [disaster](/Disasters "Disasters") until the end of the age of Renaissance so you can safely decrease your tributary payments until then.
* Start with professional troops and navy.
* Start with an unfilled government reform slot and unassigned laws.
