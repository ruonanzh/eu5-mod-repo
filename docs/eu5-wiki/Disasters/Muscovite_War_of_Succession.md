# Muscovite War of Succession

The  [![Disaster muscovite succession war.png](/thumb.php?f=Disaster_muscovite_succession_war.png&width=24)](/Muscovite_Succession_War "Muscovite Succession War") Muscovite Succession War is a unique [Disaster](/Disasters "Disasters") for  [![Flag of Muscovy](/thumb.php?f=Flag_MOS.png&width=24)](/Muscovy "Muscovy") [Muscovy](/Muscovy "Muscovy"). It has a 5% chance to occur as long as the War of Succession Pretender progress is higher than 25% and Muscovy is a [![Monarchy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=24)](/Monarchy "Monarchy") Monarchy with less than [![Legitimacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Legitimacy.png&width=24)](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy") 75 Legitimacy. Muscovite succession war disaster start with the "[The Muscovite War of Succession](/Muscovite_Succession_War#muscovite_succession_war.17 "Muscovite Succession War")" and ends with "[The Conclusion](/Muscovite_Succession_War#muscovite_succession_war#20 "Muscovite Succession War")".

## Basics

During the disaster country gains:

* [![Monthly legitimacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_legitimacy.png&width=24)](/Monthly_Legitimacy "Monthly Legitimacy") **-0.1** Monthly Legitimacy
* [![Stability cost.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability_cost.png&width=24)](/Stability_Cost "Stability Cost") **+25%** Stability Cost

In addition, during the disaster following events can happen each month:

* There is a 3% chance for "[An eye-gauging Experience](/Muscovite_Succession_War#muscovite_succession_war.18 "Muscovite Succession War")"
* There is a 3% chance for "[The Betrayal](/Muscovite_Succession_War#muscovite_succession_war.19 "Muscovite Succession War")"

*The internal strife which has brought havoc to our country has finally come to an end with the recapture of rebel strongholds along with the death of any dynasty members who openly sided against us. Let us pray that this chain of events is never repeated.*

---

*Historical info:*

*The Muscovite War of Succession came to an end when the last son of Yury Dmitrievich was defeated in battle and subsequently poisoned by the order of Vasily II. The high mortality rate within the ruling family made it challenging for the dynasty to sustain itself, but ultimately it managed to cling on to power and rule Russia until the end of the sixteenth century.*
