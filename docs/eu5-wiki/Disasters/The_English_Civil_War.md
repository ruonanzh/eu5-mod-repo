# The English Civil War

Please help with verifying or updating older sections of this article.
At least some were last verified for [version](/Europa_Universalis_5_Wiki%3AVersioning "Europa Universalis 5 Wiki:Versioning") 1.1.

The [![Disaster english civil war.png](/thumb.php?f=Disaster_english_civil_war.png&width=24)](/English_civil_war "English civil war") English civil war is a unique [disaster](/Disasters "Disasters") for [![Flag of Great Britain](/thumb.php?f=Flag_GBR.png&width=24)](/Great_Britain "Great Britain") [Great Britain](/Great_Britain "Great Britain") or [![Flag of England](/thumb.php?f=Flag_ENG_monarchy.png&width=24)](/England "England") [England](/England "England"). It has a constant chance to occur as long as the game lasts until the [![Age of absolutism.png](/thumb.php?f=Age_5_absolutism.png&width=24)](/Age_of_Absolutism "Age of Absolutism") Age of Absolutism and either the [![Crown power.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_crown_estate_power.png&width=24)](/Crown_power "Crown power") Crown power is higher than 40% or the country is at least 25 towards [![Societal value absolutism.png](/thumb.php?f=Societal_value_absolutism.png&width=24)](/Absolutism "Absolutism") Absolutism. English civil war disaster starts with "[Rise of the Parliamentarians](/English_Civil_War#english_civil_war.251 "English Civil War")" event

## Basics

During the disaster the country gains:

* ![Pop Join Rebels Threshold](/thumb.php?f=Pop_join_rebel_threshold.png&width=30 "Pop Join Rebels Threshold") +5% Pop Join Rebels Threshold
* ![Monthly Rebel Growth](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_rebel_growth.png&width=30 "Monthly Rebel Growth") +1% Monthly Rebel Growth
* ![Monthly War Exhaustion](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_war_exhaustion.png&width=30 "Monthly War Exhaustion") +0.15 Monthly War Exhaustion
* ![Manpower](/thumb.php?f=Global_manpower_modifier.png&width=30 "Manpower") −33% Manpower

In addition, during the disaster following events can happen each month

* There is a %5.5 chance for "[Province Joins the Parliamentarians](/English_Civil_War#english_civil_war.255 "English Civil War")" and "[A Rebellion Rises](/English_Civil_War#english_civil_war.257 "English Civil War")" events.
* There is a %4.5 chance for "[Our Subjects and English Civil War](/English_Civil_War#english_civil_war.258 "English Civil War")"
* There is a %2.3 chance for "[Rebels Gain Influence](/English_Civil_War#english_civil_war.261 "English Civil War")""

*Historical info:*

*The English Civil War (1642-1651) stemmed from conflicts between King Charles I and the Parliament over royal authority, taxation, and religious reforms. Charles's autocratic rule, dismissal of procedures, and imposition of unpopular policies fueled widespread discontent.\n\nThe war pitted Royalists (supporters of the monarchy) against Parliamentarians, advocating constitutional governance. It resulted in the execution of Charles I, the abolition of the monarchy, and the establishment of the Commonwealth under Oliver Cromwell. Ultimately, the conflict reshaped English political structure, weakening the monarchy's power and laying the foundation for modern parliamentary democracy, though tensions persisted until the monarchy's restoration in 1660.*
