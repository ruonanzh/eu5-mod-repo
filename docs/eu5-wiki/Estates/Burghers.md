Burghers is the [estate](/Estate "Estate") made up of pops with the [![Pop burghers.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_burghers.png&width=24)](/Burghers_%28pop%29 "Burghers (pop)") Burghers social class. Every pop produces 4 Power and 40 Tax.

## Estate modifiers

While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Burgher Estate](/thumb.php?f=Burghers_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Burgher Estate") +50% Maximum Tax for Burgher Estate
* Up to ![Prefers Court Language to be Market Language](/thumb.php?f=Court_language_is_liturgical_language_importance_modifier.png&width=30 "Prefers Court Language to be Market Language") −6 Prefers Court Language to be Market Language

While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Burgher Estate](/thumb.php?f=Burghers_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Burgher Estate") −50% Maximum Tax for Burgher Estate
* Up to ![Trade Capacity](/thumb.php?f=Global_merchant_capacity_modifier.png&width=30 "Trade Capacity") +100% Trade Capacity
* Up to ![Navy Maintenance Efficiency](/thumb.php?f=Navy_maintenance_cost.png&width=30 "Navy Maintenance Efficiency") +50% Navy Maintenance Efficiency
* Up to ![Prefers Court Language to be Market Language](/thumb.php?f=Court_language_is_liturgical_language_importance_modifier.png&width=30 "Prefers Court Language to be Market Language") +12 Prefers Court Language to be Market Language

Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:

* [![Global merchant power.png](/thumb.php?f=Merchant_power_from_maritime.png&width=24)](/File%3AMerchant_power_from_maritime.png) **−25%** to **+25%** Trade advantage
* [![Global production efficiency.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_production_efficiency.png&width=24)](/File%3AGlobal_production_efficiency.png) **−2.5%** to **+2.5%** Production efficiency

## Opinion of other countries

The estate's opinion of other countries scales with trade capacity difference and is also affected by the following:

* **+5** if both countries have Republic government
* **−5** if target has a merchant in the home market
