Cossacks is an [estate](/Estate "Estate") available only to countries whose primary [culture](/Culture "Culture") is part of the East Slavic, Lithuanian or Polish culture group and only pops that have the [![Pop tribesmen.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_tribesmen.png&width=24)](/Tribesmen "Tribesmen") Tribesmen social class and Cossack can join it. Every pop produces 0.5 Power and 0.02 Tax.

## Estate modifiers

While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Cossacks](/thumb.php?f=Cossacks_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Cossacks") +50% Maximum Tax for Cossacks
* Up to ![Pop Join Rebels Threshold](/thumb.php?f=Pop_join_rebel_threshold.png&width=30 "Pop Join Rebels Threshold") −10% Pop Join Rebels Threshold

While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Cossacks](/thumb.php?f=Cossacks_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Cossacks") −30% Maximum Tax for Cossacks
* Up to ![Military Tactics](/thumb.php?f=Military_tactics.png&width=30 "Military Tactics") +0.1 Military Tactics
* Up to ![Monthly Progress to Offensive](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_towards_offensive.png&width=30 "Monthly Progress to Offensive") +0.33 Monthly Progress to Offensive

Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:

* [![Monthly war exhaustion.png](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_war_exhaustion.png&width=24)](/File%3AMonthly_war_exhaustion.png) **+0.025** to **−0.025** Monthly war exhaustion
* [![Global monthly food modifier.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_monthly_food_modifier.png&width=24)](/File%3AGlobal_monthly_food_modifier.png) **−5%** to **+5%** Food Production %

## Opinion of other countries

The estate's opinion of other countries is affected by the following:

* **+5** if both countries have the same common language
* **−5** towards countries with a heretic religion
* **−10** towards countries with a heathen religion
* **−20** towards rivals
