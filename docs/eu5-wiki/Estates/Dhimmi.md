Dhimmi is an [estate](/Estate "Estate") available only to countries whose primary [religion](/Religion "Religion") is part of the Islam religion group and only pops from a different religion group who aren't part of the [![Pop tribesmen.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_tribesmen.png&width=24)](/Tribesmen "Tribesmen") Tribesmen social class can join it. Every pop produces 0.02 Power and 1 Tax.

## Estate modifiers

While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate](/thumb.php?f=Dhimmi_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate") +50% Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate
* Up to ![Pop Join Rebels Threshold](/thumb.php?f=Pop_join_rebel_threshold.png&width=30 "Pop Join Rebels Threshold") −10% Pop Join Rebels Threshold

While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate](/thumb.php?f=Dhimmi_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate") −30% Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate
* Up to ![Monthly Progress to Humanist](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_towards_humanist.png&width=30 "Monthly Progress to Humanist") +0.33 Monthly Progress to Humanist

Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:

* [![Tolerance heathen.png](/thumb.php?f=Tolerance_heathen.png&width=24)](/File%3ATolerance_heathen.png) **−1** to **+1** Tolerance of Heathen Beliefs
* [![Country cabinet efficiency.png](/thumb.php?f=Country_cabinet_efficiency.png&width=24)](/File%3ACountry_cabinet_efficiency.png) **−5%** to **+5%** Efficiency of Our Cabinet
