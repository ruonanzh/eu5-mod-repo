# Communities

This article is [timeless](/Category%3ATimeless "Category:Timeless") and should be accurate for any version of the game.

The game's userbase maintains a number of **communities** across the internet, created by [Paradox Interactive](/Paradox_Interactive "Paradox Interactive") and by fans themselves.

## Official

The following are official Paradox Interactive accounts or resources for Europa Universalis V.

* [Paradox Plaza forum](https://forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/forums/europa-universalis-v.1171/)
* [Youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/%40EuropaUniversalisofficial)
* [Discord](https://discord.gg/europauniversalisofficial)
* [Twitter](https://twitter.com/E_Universalis)
* [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/EuropaUniversalis/)
* [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/europa_universalis_pdx/)
