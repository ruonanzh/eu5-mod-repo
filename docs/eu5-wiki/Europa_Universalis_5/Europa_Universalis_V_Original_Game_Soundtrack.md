# Europa Universalis V Original Game Soundtrack

**Europa Universalis V Original Game Soundtrack** is a soundtrack album from [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V") composed by Håkan Glänte and Mattias Wennlund and performed by the Norrköping Symphony Orchestra. It was released on June 19, 2025.

## Fate of the Phoenix Official Game Soundtrack

**Fate of the Phoenix Official Game Soundtrack** is a EP soundtrack album for [Fate of the Phoenix](/Fate_of_the_Phoenix "Fate of the Phoenix") immersion pack for Europa Universalis V. It was released alongside with the DLC on May 6, 2026.

| № | Title | Length | In-game description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Byzantine - Hymn Melodies | 11:18 | «A hymn based on the melodic tradition of Byzantine plainchant, shaped by the modal system of the eight echoi that guided Orthodox church music for centuries.» |  |
| 2 | Hymn No. 136 | 2:25 | «A setting of the Byzantine Psalm 136, beginning with the prayer 'Our Father' and the refrain 'Exomologiste to Kyrio, oti agathos' (Praise the Lord, for He is good) sung in the traditional call-and-response style of Orthodox chant.» |  |
| 3 | Byzantine - O Virgin Pure | 8:46 | «Agni Parthene (O Virgin Pure) is a Greek Marian hymn composed by St. Nectarios of Aegina in the late 19th century, first published in his Theotokarion in 1905.» |  |
