# Paradox Tinto

This article is [timeless](/Category%3ATimeless "Category:Timeless") and should be accurate for any version of the game.

[![](/images/c/cd/Paradox_Tinto.png)](/File%3AParadox_Tinto.png)

One version of Paradox Tinto's logo

**Paradox Tinto** is an internal development studio of [Paradox Interactive](/Paradox_Interactive "Paradox Interactive"), established in 2020. It is headquartered in Barcelona, Spain and focuses on the Europa Universalis franchise. Paradox Tinto is the developer of [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V") and developed the last several DLC for Europa Universalis IV, starting with Leviathan.
