**Tinto talks** are articles, sometimes accompanied by videos, published by [Paradox](/Paradox "Paradox") to describe the game design in detail. These are equivalent to *developer diaries* for other games.

Instead of releasing developer diaries only in the relatively short amount of time between announcing and releasing the game, there were regular **Tinto Talks**, [Tinto Flavor](#Tinto_Flavor), [Tinto Maps](#Tinto_Maps), [Tinto Flags](#Tinto_Flags), and Saturday building posts about the game **Project Caesar** before and after it was announced as **[Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V")**. The goal of these posts was to gather player feedback early enough in the development process that the game could be adapted to the feedback.[[1]](#cite_note-1) After the game's release date was announced, several recap developer diaries were posted as well.

All Tinto talk posts prior to #111 were titled by their number and date only, so descriptive titles in the tables below have been added by wiki contributors.
