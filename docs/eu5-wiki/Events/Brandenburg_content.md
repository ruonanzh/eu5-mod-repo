## Events

### Expand the Tangermunde Castle (1337.1.1 - 1450.1.1)

Trigger: Owns Stendal

Reward: Mild Prestige Bonus

### The Capital moves to Berlin (1390.1.1 - 1440.1.1)

Trigger: Owns Berlin, Berlin is not the capital

Reward: Capital moves to Berlin for no cost, but all gouvernment buildings in the current capital will be destroyed

**Construction of Berlin Palace (1440.1.1 -1480.1.1)**

Trigger: Owns Berlin, Berlin is the capital

Reward: Begin construction of the unique Berlin Palace gouvernment building

### Order of The Swan (1420.1.1 - 1460.1.1)

Trigger: Having a ruler with a dynasty and being catholic

Reward: Unlocking the **Order of Chivalry Law** and making the **Order of the Swan** available

**Important Note:** When you switch to any non-catholic religion after establishing the Order, it will become unavailable

### Frankfurt Oder emerging University (1480.1.1 - 1540.1.1)

Trigger: Owns Lebus

Reward: If the settlement type is rural, it will be upgraded to a town, if there is no university present, a university will be build and Lebus will be renamed to Frankfurt an der Oder

**The Kreditwerk takes charges of the finances (1530.1.1 - 1580.1.1)**

Trigger: Have less Gold then 5x your trade- and tax income combined

Reward: The Burgher privilege **Kreditwerk** will immideatly be granted. There is a chance to reform it later if you keep it around, see **Bankruptcy of the Kreditwerk**

**Bankruptcy of the Kreditwerk (1610.1.1 - 1640.1.1)**

Trigger: Has estate privilege **Kreditwerk**

Reward: Remove Burgher estate privilege **Kreditwerk** for no cost. You can choose to grant the Burgher privilege **Reformed Kreditwerk** for no cost.

### Hallescher Pietism and Prussian virtues (1637.1.1 - 1780.1.1)

Trigger: Has the religious aspect **Pietism**

Reward: Unlocks the unique **Pietism Society** policy

### Order of the Black Eagle (1690.1.1 - 1710.1.1)

Trigger: At least 25 Quality

Reward: Unlocking the **Order of Chivalry Law** and making the **Order of the Black Eagle** available

### The Soldier King of Prussia (1700.1.1 - 1740.1.1)

Trigger: Is a monarchy, has the unique [Prussia](/Prussia "Prussia") advance **The Soldier King** and a ruler with over 60 MIL

Reward: If the ruler has no nickname, he will gain the '[Name] the Soldier King' nickname. You will unlock the unique **Military Court** policy

### Peuplierung System (1700.1.1 - 1836.1.1)

Trigger: Has a ruler

Reward: Unlocks the unique **Peuplierung System** gouvernment reform

### Charité (1710.1.1 - 1836.1.1)

Trigger: Owns Berlin

Reward: Begin construction of the unique Charité Hospital gouvernment building

### Construction of Sanssouci (1730.1.1 - 1750.1.1)

Trigger: Owns Potsdam, has embraced the institution Enlightenment

Reward: Begin construction of the unique Sanssouci gouvernment building.
