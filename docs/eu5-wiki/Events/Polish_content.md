*The common enemy in Teutonic Order has aligned the interests of the Kingdom of Poland and the Grand Duchy of Lithuania. Up until recently, the two states were experiencing the all-too-common tale of two rival powers vying for hegemony in their local area. Now, with a foe that can defeat either one at any given time, the two countries are closer than ever. To solidify this state of affairs, the Lithuanian Bajorai have proposed a marriage between the Polish candidate and the Lithuanian candidate.*

---

This event happens *only once* during a campaign.
