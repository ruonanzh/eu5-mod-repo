### Eisteddfod of [location]

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for [version](/Europa_Universalis_5_Wiki%3AVersioning "Europa Universalis 5 Wiki:Versioning") 1.0. |

---

*The Eisteddfod is a Welsh cultural festival of ancient origin, a popular competition where bards and minstrels perform astounding feats of music and poetry. This year, the festival is set to take place in [location]. While the festival is a celebration of Welsh arts and culture, we fear that it may stoke anti-[Country's culture] sentiment and loosen our grip over [location]. While we could simply ignore the festival as nothing more than a rural fair, we could also dampen the political messages by ensuring that only licensed bards (who sing approved songs) can perform. We could of course also break up this rural ruckus with a detachment of armed brutes.*

---

This event happens *only once* during a campaign.
