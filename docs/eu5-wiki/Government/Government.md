Governments at the start of the game. Blue are monarchies, red are republics, white are theocracies, green are steppe hordes, and brown are tribes.

**Government** is a group consisting of the people and institutions that managing a [nation](/Country "Country"). Every country has its own government of a specific [government type](#Government_Types) located in its [capital](/Capital "Capital").

Governments consist of the [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler") and the [Cabinet](#Cabinet), and are shaped by [Societal Values](#Societal_Values), [Government Reforms](/Government_Reforms "Government Reforms"), and [Laws](/Laws "Laws").

## Government types

Each country has a government type, which defines the inner structure of its government. The government type determines the Government Power resource used and the [succession laws](/Succession_laws "Succession laws") available, but also unlocks specific laws, government reforms, estate privileges, and advances.

Countries that are at peace, do not have a disaster and are not bankrupt or part of an Union can change their government type at the cost of 50 [![Stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") Stability and 25 Government Power. This will also change the country type to [![Country location.png](/thumb.php?f=Country_location.png&width=24)](/File%3ACountry_location.png) Settled if not already. A very few formable countries will change government type when formed and some historical flavor events can change government type. [![Flag PAP.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_PAP.png&width=24)](/File%3AFlag_PAP.png) Papal States, [![Flag CHI Yuan.png](/thumb.php?f=Flag_CHI_Yuan.png&width=24)](/File%3AFlag_CHI_Yuan.png) Yuán and countries that are electors or emperor of the Holy Roman Empire cannot change government.

## Cabinet

[![](/thumb.php?f=UI_cabinet.png&width=320)](/File%3AUI_cabinet.png)

The cabinet of a kingdom

The Cabinet is the assembly of higher officials that help govern the country. Each country starts with 2 cabinet seats and the number can be increased by certain advances, the empire government rank. Appointing a cabinet member costs only a small amount of Gold but replacing a cabinet member costs 5 [![Stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") Stability. By default only male [characters](/Characters "Characters") can be appointed to the cabinet but there are a few ways to change this.

Countries that do not have [![Tribe.png](/thumb.php?f=Tribe.png&width=24)](/Tribe "Tribe") Tribe government can promote one of its cabinet members to Head of Cabinet. This costs 10 times as much [![Gold.png](/thumb.php?f=Resource_gold.png&width=24)](/Gold "Gold") Gold as appointing a cabinet member and also 5 Government Power. Having a Head of Cabinet increases the efficiency of the cabinet by **+25%** but the selected cabined member can no longer lead armies or navies or be removed from the cabinet and the Head of Cabinet cannot be changed until the current one dies.

* If the Head of Cabinet belongs to an estate other than the [![Estate crown.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_crown.png&width=24)](/File%3AEstate_crown.png) Crown, its estate will gain **+5%** Satisfaction and **+25%** Power.
* If the Head of Cabinet belongs to the [![Estate crown.png](/thumb.php?f=Estate_crown.png&width=24)](/File%3AEstate_crown.png) Crown estate the country gains **+25%** Crown Power and **+0.02** Monthly Government Power.

Each member of the cabinet can be assigned to a cabinet action. These are performed either until they are finished, or cancelled.

## Regency

A Regency starts when a country loses its ruler and either does not have a heir or the heir is a child. Under a regency the country will be ruled by a character known as the regent. The regency will continue until a proper ruler is available. Regencies caused because the ruler is underage will end when the ruler becomes an adult but can be extended by 5 years at any point at the cost of 20 Government Power. While a country has a regency each year there is a 20% chance for a regency-related event to trigger.

There are multiple types of regency possible. They follow a strict hierarchy, with the country using the first valid regency type from the following list.

## Societal values

[![](/thumb.php?f=UI_societal_values.png&width=320)](/File%3AUI_societal_values.png)

Societal values during the Age of Discovery

The societal values define the social and political structure of a [country](/Country "Country"), the customs of its people and the characteristics of its legal body. Its effects can have a profound impact on how the country is played, and some advanced Government Reforms require specific combinations of Societal Values to be enacted. Societal values also play an important role in [diplomacy](/Diplomacy "Diplomacy"), as countries with affine societal values will tend to have a higher Opinion of each other.

Each societal value is an axis with two opposite poles that can reach up to 100. A country's position on the axis is primarily influenced by laws, reforms, estate privileges, and cabinet actions, and it drifts slowly over time towards one of the two poles. The higher a societal value, the more factors are needed to push it further. Hovering over a societal value will display all currently available options of increasing it.

As a country progresses towards a societal value it will gain increasing bonuses and penalties, scaling from zero to the values displayed in the table below.

### Stability

Stability represents the internal peace of a country and ranges from **−100** to **+100**. Over time is tends towards 0 unless other factors impact it. Starting wars is one of the actions that will reduce the stability of a country.

Every point of Stability above 0 brings the following effects:

* [![Pop join rebel threshold.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_join_rebel_threshold.png&width=24)](/File%3APop_join_rebel_threshold.png) **+0.1%** Pop join rebels threshold
* [![Pop join rebel threshold.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_join_rebel_threshold.png&width=24)](/File%3APop_join_rebel_threshold.png) **+0.05%** Pop leave rebels threshold
* [![Global estate target satisfaction.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_estate_target_satisfaction.png&width=24)](/File%3AGlobal_estate_target_satisfaction.png) **+0.05%** Estates satisfaction equilibrium
* [![Global pop promotion speed.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_pop_promotion_speed.png&width=24)](/File%3AGlobal_pop_promotion_speed.png) **+1%** Pop promotion speed

Every point of Stability below 0 brings the following effects:

* [![Pop join rebel threshold.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_join_rebel_threshold.png&width=24)](/File%3APop_join_rebel_threshold.png) **−0.1%** Pop join rebels threshold
* [![Pop join rebel threshold.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_join_rebel_threshold.png&width=24)](/File%3APop_join_rebel_threshold.png) **−0.05%** Pop leave rebels threshold
* [![Global estate target satisfaction.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_estate_target_satisfaction.png&width=24)](/File%3AGlobal_estate_target_satisfaction.png) **−0.3%** Estates satisfaction equilibrium
* [![Global mercenaries modifier.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_mercenaries_modifier.png&width=24)](/File%3AGlobal_mercenaries_modifier.png) **+0.5%** Mercenary size

Stability decays by default with 0.5% of the current absolute amount of positive or negative stability per month towards 0. This can be modified for example by the Elite Council Government Reform.

### Prestige

Prestige represents how well the country is seen and ranges from 0 to 100. Each point of Prestige gives the following effects:

* [![Cultural influence.png](/thumb.php?f=Cultural_influence.png&width=24)](/File%3ACultural_influence.png) **+0.01** Cultural influence
* [![Diplomatic reputation.png](/thumb.php?f=Diplomatic_reputation.png&width=24)](/File%3ADiplomatic_reputation.png) **+0.05** Diplomatic reputation
* [![Create market cost modifier.png](/thumb.php?f=Create_market_cost_modifier.png&width=24)](/File%3ACreate_market_cost_modifier.png) **+0.05%** Market attraction
* [![Skill of new artists.png](/thumb.php?f=Skill_of_new_artists.png&width=24)](/File%3ASkill_of_new_artists.png) **+0.1%** Skill of new artists
* [![Antagonism monthly change.png](/thumb.php?f=Antagonism_monthly_change.png&width=24)](/File%3AAntagonism_monthly_change.png) **+0.0025** Antagonism change modifier
* [![Prestige](/thumb.php?f=Prestige.png&width=24)](/Economy#Prestige "Prestige") **−0.01** Prestige decay
