An army is a group of [Regiments](/index.php?title=Regiments&action=edit&redlink=1 "Regiments (page does not exist)") led by a [Character](/Character "Character"), which can fight [Battles](/index.php?title=Battles&action=edit&redlink=1 "Battles (page does not exist)") and [Siege](/Siege "Siege") down enemy [Fortifications](/Fortifications "Fortifications").

When a regular regiment is below its maximum strength and located within owned, subject, overlord, or war ally borders, it will attempt to reinforce, which will draw Manpower and Gold at the height of five time its normal maintenance cost[[1]](#cite_note-1).
