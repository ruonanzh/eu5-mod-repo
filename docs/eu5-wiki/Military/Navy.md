Navy

Navies are what allows a [Country](/Country "Country") to project power beyond its own land. Navies are groups of [Ships](/index.php?title=Ships&action=edit&redlink=1 "Ships (page does not exist)") led by a [Character](/Character "Character"), which can fight [Battles](/index.php?title=Battles&action=edit&redlink=1 "Battles (page does not exist)"), [Blockade](/Blockade "Blockade") [Hostile](/index.php?title=Hostile&action=edit&redlink=1 "Hostile (page does not exist)") coasts, disrupt enemy [Maritime Presence](/Maritime_Presence "Maritime Presence"), and transport [Armies](/Armies "Armies") where they are needed. They also help with logistics as they can [Distribute Food](/index.php?title=Distribute_Food&action=edit&redlink=1 "Distribute Food (page does not exist)") and [Gather Food](/index.php?title=Gather_Food&action=edit&redlink=1 "Gather Food (page does not exist)").

Every country has a Naval Range. If a navy moves beyond the naval range they will suffer attrition. Navies also take increasing amounts of attrition each month they are at sea.

If a ship is damaged and located in a port that is owned or belongs to a country that gave Fleet Basing Rights, it will attempt to repair itself, which will draw Sailors and Gold.

Navies can dock at any coastal location by clicking on it, regardless of whether the coastal location has a port or not.
