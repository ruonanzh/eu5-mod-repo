## New Features

### Gameplay

* Removed economic growth impact on trust.

### Content

* Added 10 generic bureaucracies for the 1600-1836 era.
* Added 8 new estate interactions have been added that allow players to sacrifice short term estate goodwill for immediate relief during wars and crises.
* Added Advances to Travunja, finished Advances of Sicily.
* Added a new Legal Code Law policy (Carta de Logu) for Sardinian culture.
* Added Advances for Maltese, Sardinian and Slovak cultures and for SLO tag.
* Added 17 new Government Reforms locked behind Societal Values.
* Added Advances for Corsican culture, COR Tag and Rhaetian Culture Group.
* Added Romagnol culture and dialect.
* Added advances for Emilian, Piedmontese and Romagnol Cultures.
* Added Advances for Bologna, Ferrara, Modena and Parma.
* Added 2 new government reforms for Sinicized and Unsinicized values.
* Added 14 Events about the Scaliger War.
* Added Scaliger War to the Setup.
* Added 10 DHEs for Pisa.
* Added 15 DHEs for Montferrat.
* Added 2 Yuan era Bureaucracies to China in 1337, and added 2 Ming era Bureaucracies to change them to.
* Added Advances for Lombard and Venetian cultures and for VER.
* Added 5 generic early-game town rights.
* Added 10 DHEs for Lucca.
* Added advances for Median, Venetian and Vivaro-Alpine cultures. Also a few extra for PAP or repurposed many for PAP, VEN or VER.
* Added 10 DHEs for Siena.
* Added advances for Tuscan culture. Also a few extra for FLO, PIS; SIE and TUS or repurposed many for FLO and TUS.
* Added advances for Ligurian culture. Also a few extra for GEN or repurposed many for GEN.
* Added the new "Twilight of the Tsardom" disaster for Bulgaria.
* Added advances for AQU, LUC, MAN, MFA, MOD, PAD, PAR, PCZ, PEA, PIE, RAV, SAL, SAR, SPL and URB.
* Added "Mōs Maiōrum", a new reform that pushes towards Latinitas.
* Added Occitania formable.
* Added a new Military Law Policy "Reformed Tágmata" for Byzantium, unlocked by completing the Fate of the Phoenix Disaster victoriously via the new DHE event "The Reformed Tágmata" (d008\_flavor\_byz.5).
* Added a "Bulgarian Tsardom" government reform and adjusted the Bulgarian content assets and setup to work with the "Twilight of the Tsardom" new Bulgarian disaster.
* Added the "Wojewodztwo", "Voievodate", and "Vojvodstvo" advances, giving Polish, Carpathian, and South Slavic countries access to the "Voivode" government reform.
* Added 10 new unique Building-based State and Family Banks: the Aragonese Taula de Canvi, the Berenberg family bank, the Bank of Hamburg, the Grimaldi, Spinola and Pallavicino Genoese banking families, the Portugues Mendes family, and the Venetian Banco del Giro. In parallel, the English Bank of England, the Dutch Wisselbank and the Swedish Riksbank are now developed upon the new mechanism for Central Banks.
* Added 6 new Achievements based on 1.3 mechanics and Italian flavour content.

### Art

* Added throne rooms for Guelphs and Ghibellines IOs
* Added new loading screen, about Renaissance Florentine Academia

### Interface

* Players can now permanently designate any owned location as its province capital via a new button in the location window.
* Split Cabinet automation into Members and Actions toggles.
* The court artist system has been fully reworked: artists are now tracked as typed individuals with passive modifiers that apply while at court (painters boost prestige income, architects reduce construction costs, etc.)
* Added Auto-Expand Buildings automation system.
* Added Automated Diplomacy system with capacity reserve slider and action flag checkboxes.
* Added "Unique" tab showing country-specific advances.
* Added new functionality to the Auto-Expand RGO. It automatically constructs the next tier of a producing building each month when conditions are met.
* Added a bug reporter that can be used to report issues from the game to the Paradox Forums.
* Added filters to the mercenary captain hiring view.

## Gameplay

### Government

* Increased crown estate cabinet member preference.
* Closed loophole allowing 2 heads of cabinet.
* Added base Estate Power modifiers allowing government reforms and privileges to structurally reshape the political balance between Estates at its foundation, with an initial application to the Peasant Republic reform.
* Estate Culture & Religion: Estates now have their own culture and religion, inherited from the pops with the most political weight inside them. An estate aligned with your primary/accepted culture and your state religion gains satisfaction and power; one stuck on a discriminated culture or a heretic/heathen religion loses both. Watch the new alerts — a powerful estate with the wrong culture or religion now actively works against you.
* Our Estates' opinions on other countries are now based on their own Culture and Religion instead of our Country.
* Estates have a Parliament Agenda to change Primary Religion to their own.
* Estates have a Parliament Agenda to change Court Language to one they prefer.
* The Union of Crowns succession law no longer checks for the whole extended family (so no more random cousins become king of either country). Additionally, female characters are now allowed for Hungary.
* Added estate satisfaction and legislative efficiency penalties to low republican tradition.
* Low devotion in devotion-based governments now reduces monthly religious influence by up to -0.1, mirroring the existing high-devotion bonus and making slipping devotion a genuine cost to your religious standing.
* Halved abdicate prestige cost, double legitimacy cost.
* Rebalance complacency drivers: halve passive gain from idle rival slots, double the swing from average control.
* Rebalance corrupt and schemer cabinet trait magnitudes.
* Selling a fiefdom no longer keeps your own ruler.
* Fixed promoting to head of cabinet not showing the skill increase immediately.
* Stopped union heirs moving countries when they are ruling somewhere else.
* Fixed unions with unified succession law not updating other countries' succession laws when the senior partner changes theirs.
* Fixed obscure case of a ruler switching countries when in a union with seniority but the less senior country was the first one they ruled.
* Fixed Settle the Frontier not recognising provinces bordering unowned territory as borders.
* Support Heir cost no longer persists after election.
* Fixed case where a ruler can remain in a country after being replaced despite also ruling somewhere else.
* Societal values cabinet action will stop if they reach max.
* Closed loophole allowing consorts to be cabinet members if they become consort after already being in the cabinet.
* Stopped countries from staying army-based after winning a nationalist/patriots revolt that had engulfed all their territory — the type-restore that added for civil wars now also fires when the revolt-war attacker is annexed (by the original or a third party) and when AutomaticWhitePeaces force-resolves a collapsed revolt war.
* Stopped the event "The Union of Crowns" (succession.101) from firing if the player has a female heir.
* Changed dissolved or disenfranchised Dhimmi estates to feed their political weight into the Nobles instead of the Peasants.
* Changed estate culture/religion to now be weighted by acceptance and tolerance.
* Changed estate dominant culture/religion to only flip when the challenger beats the incumbent by 10%.
* Updated Traditional Economy to now allow rural resource operations to grow up to 33% larger and removed the −10% building construction efficiency penalty.
* Reduced the penalty on estate satisfaction from individualism from 5.0% to 2.5%.
* HRE election no longer stalls indefinitely when an ineligible candidate (e.g. extraterritorial republics like the Hanseatic League) accumulates majority of elector votes.
* Junior union members no longer get caught in a Negotiate Succession Law opinion spiral through the heir-selection (succession law) path.
* Child union leader no longer renders an empty Union panel during a regency.
* Reduced estate satisfaction scaling from religious tolerance.
* The Charismatic Orator cabinet trait now correctly erodes noble estate satisfaction as its downside. The intended penalty was previously a no-op, making the trait a free bonus instead of the designed trade-off between parliamentary appeal and noble deference.
* Fixed the bureaucracy removal efficiency modifier to now apply correctly.
* Fixed frustrate annexation working in the wrong direction.

### Economy

* Fixed exploit with having subjects to allow multiple naval governiors.
* Building Upkeep now gradually increases as the years pass, starting unchanged but climbing steadily, about 50% higher after a century and several times higher by the late game. This makes maintaining a large number of buildings an increasing economic burden the longer your campaign runs.
* Allowed laborers to promote to soldiers and burghers.
* AI trade management now reduces capacity on inefficient trades instead of immediately cancelling them if they are unprofitable. This lets the AI keep useful routes alive when a trade is unprofitable but still has goods left to transport even it it could not transport the full desired amount, making trade behavior less abrupt and less chaotic.
* Made stockpiles now add trade supply gradually instead of switching on all at once. The extra supply starts ramping up once a market stockpile is above 50% capacity, reaches full effect at 75%, while at the same time fades out smoothly when stockpile decreases. This reduces sudden market balance and price shocks when stockpiles move around the threshold.
* AI Trade will know prefer trades with bigger surpluses if the profit difference is small, in order to promote stable and reliable trades.
* Replaced price band position metric with price deviation.
* Fixed cannons\_factory and putrefaction\_mill using manufactory\_max\_level instead of mills\_max\_level.
* Fixed putrefaction\_mill using manufactory\_build\_time instead of mills\_build\_time.
* Pop demands scales down if the prices of those exceed default.
* Rebalanced production method profit margin targets for guilds (10%), workshops (15%), manufactories (20%), and mills (25%), and adjusted all building production method inputs to match.
* Burgher Trade Range now scales with the age, unchanged at 600 in the Age of Traditions, then growing by 100 each age up to 1100 in the Age of Revolutions, so burgher auto-trade gradually reaches farther markets as the centuries progress.
* Halved mills\_employment to make Age of Revolution production buildings competitive.
* Added 50% mill construction cost discount via industrialization advance.
* Added regional production specialization modifiers.
* Added is\_mill flag and location-scaling build efficiency bonuses for Age of Revolution mills.
* Rebalance fine cloth production toward Brugge, reduce French genericism.
* Doubled the cost of expanding rgo's.
* Fixed gold/silver inflation impact not removed by non-metal coinage laws.
* Cities & Harbors: Cities and Megalopolises now provide some innate market attraction of their own, and good natural harbors make a location a stronger trade anchor, further concentrating trade around your major urban and coastal centers.
* Slave import trade orders now check pop culture/religion viability.
* Trade orders spread capacity across multiple routes.
* Gated production building levels by market access.
* Removed glass/cloth guilds from basic European town setups.
* Extracted rural\_glassmaker as standalone building.
* Market Attraction: Development, staffed building levels, and natural harbor capacity now contribute much more strongly to a location's market attraction, staffed building levels in particular pull around five times harder than before, development twice as hard, and good natural harbors now add attraction where they previously added none. As a result, your most developed, built-up, and well-harbored locations now dominate the trade map far more decisively, making investment in development, industry, and coastal infrastructure pay off more directly in trade gravity.
* Excluded foreign buildings from total effective building levels.
* Stopped satellite trade buildings from stacking infinitely.
* Trade Offices can now be built in non-ports as well.
* Mills add 0.05 possible burghers per level to their location.
* Boosted slave inflow from buildings and enable slave growth.
* Scaled privateer placement opinion hit by stolen income.
* Overlord fleets only project maritime presence onto subjects bordering the sea zone.
* Trade Automation now produces trades on the first monthly tick after the entry is auto-initialized.
* Stopped slaves from working RGO when allow\_rgo\_slave\_demand is inactive.
* Nerfed traders\_look\_abroad selling\_efficiency bonus from huge (+20%) to medium (+5%).
* Doubled the chance for food goods to be picked when the Columbian Exchange relocates goods between the Old and New Worlds.
* Slave centers can now enslave laborers in addition to peasants, picking the larger valid pop across both pools each month.
* Fixed ships not repairing sometimes despite having money and men to do it.
* Doubled per-ship maritime presence values.
* Fixed a market's stockpile of a good being counted as extra live supply when calculating that good's price, which dragged prices to the minimum and kept them there for any good with a full reserve. Stockpiles now only feed exports, not price formation, so full warehouses no longer cause minimum-price lock-in.
* Stockpile Capacity: Market stockpile capacity has been significantly reduced, base values cut from 200–250 down to 25–50, with late-game growth now earned through the market-center building chain (Entrepôt through Clearing House). A basic 1337 market town drops from 455 to 55; a late-game megalopolis from ~2400 to ~630.
* Changed pops to buy fewer goods when the estate they belong to is running an income shortfall: an estate covering only half its expenses pulls every pop's goods demand down by 25% at the default scale, and an estate with no income at all halves demand entirely, with the contribution surfaced as a new line in the per-good demand tooltip that names which estate is responsible and shows the resulting multiplier.
* Market price discounts on multi-good build costs no longer stack past the -33% cap — buildings and military units recruited in a market with several goods well below default price were getting a combined discount equal to the sum of each good's impact instead of the intended capped average.
* Auto-expand (buildings and RGO) is no longer silently throttled by the invisible AI desired-buffer heuristic; only the player-set automation gold slider now gates the spend.
* Expired loans no longer drift into negative remaining interest when the "we can repay a loan" notification is suppressed.
* Reworked the "Granary Town" urban right to reduce Food Decay via a -25% modifier instead of a flat -0.20%, matching the modifier type used by the Granary building so the bonus is comparable, scales with the food stockpile, and reads as a meaningful effect.
* Fixed economy discrepancy between what it says your balance is and what actually happens on the 1st of the month.
* Increased granary food storage.
* Fixed road cost not having a breakdown tooltip.
* Fixed military sponsorship payments not updating after reloading.
* Fixed an issue with correct markets not being chosen for some locations.
* Trade route maintenance now reflects the actual travel path rather than being capped to the straight-line range distance; routes that detour around terrain are no longer artificially discounted in their upkeep cost.
* Pop demand for luxury goods now switches on fully once an estate crosses the wealth threshold, rather than ramping up gradually as wealth approaches it — below the threshold a pop demands nothing; at or above it they demand the full amount.
* Allowed construction to now start without required goods, stalling until they become available.
* Added an open-sea proximity cost for landing into a non-port sea zone.

### Geopolitics

* Changed smallpox to now spawn in subtropical climates and caps its distance-based stagnation, so outbreaks no longer cluster overwhelmingly in compact Europe while large Asian continents go relatively unscathed.
* Extended colonial migration from just capital region to include anywhere joined by land or strait to the capital too.
* Smallpox outbreaks have been shortened and rarefied. Spawn chance halved (0.1 -> 0.05), calc\_interval slowed to match influenza ({2 8} -> {4 12}), and added a 15% location\_spread\_threshold so lightly-infected edges no longer creep into neighbors forever.
* Ports with negative natural\_harbor\_suitability no longer drain maritime presence.
* Extraterritorial countries can no longer lead an HRE Imperial Circle.
* Conquistadors now spawn with a supply unit so they don't instantly starve.
* Reduced stagnation chances for bubonic\_plague as it's dying out too soon
* Locations with pops migrating in no longer cause diseases to continue indefinitely.

* Scaled repeating advance modifiers across ages to preserve relative value.
* Reduced Age of Revolution mill advance research cost by 75%.
* Being the birthplace of Global Trade is now +10% market attraction instead of +2.5%.
* Average Control now have a bigger impact on research speed.
* Rebalanced overtuned Italian minor advances.
