**Holy Site** is a place of special interest to a particular [religion](/Religion "Religion"). A holy site may provide various bonuses to the l[ocation](/Location "Location") or [owner](/Country "Country"), which are scaled by its importance.

## Holy Sites types

Each Holy Site belongs to one of the types that defines what bonuses that this site will provide for the location or country that owns it.
