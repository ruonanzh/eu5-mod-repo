## Cultures

The German Culture Group is made up of the following cultures: Baltic German, Walloon, Angrian, Brandenburgish, Eastern Pomeranian, Eastphalian, Hanseatic, Holsantian, Lower Saxon, Markish, Prussian, Western Pomeranian, Westphalian, East Franconian, Carpathian German, Silesian German, Transylvanian German, Low Franconian, Moselle Franconian, Rhine Franconian, Ripuarian Franconian, Saxon, Danube Bavarian, High Alemannic, Rhine Alemannic, Southern Bavarian, Swabian, Upper Palatine, Ashkenazi, Frisian, and Czech
