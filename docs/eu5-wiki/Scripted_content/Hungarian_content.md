---

*With the country about to disintegrate, after the disastrous Battle of Varna, the death of King Wladyslaw and the loss to the Ottomans, the Voivode János Hunyadi called together a diet in an attempt to unite the country again. The division was almost unmanageable and anarchy was close.*

---

*Historical info:*

*Following the catastrophic defeat at the Battle of Varna in 1444 and the death of King Władysław III, Hungary was plunged into a severe political crisis. With the heir, Ladislaus the Posthumous, being a minor and held by the Holy Roman Emperor, the country lacked a functional central authority. In 1446, a Diet was convened to stabilize the realm. János Hunyadi, a renowned military leader and "White Knight" who had spent years fighting the Ottomans, was the natural choice for leadership. The Diet eventually elected him as Regent-Governor of Hungary, granting him near-royal powers to defend the kingdom and restore order.*

### The Reforms of Mathias Corvinus

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. It was last verified for [version](/Europa_Universalis_5_Wiki%3AVersioning "Europa Universalis 5 Wiki:Versioning") 1.0. |

---

*During his reign, Mathias Corvinus quite significantly changed the Hungarian state and society. He managed to increase the royal income dramatically by making the poorer members of the Diet who were unable to attend it regularly agree to grant taxes several years in advance, outmaneuvering the high nobility, by establishing a regular, functional system of tax collection and by having the treasury administered by specialists with separate functions.*

---

*Historical info:*

*King Matthias Corvinus (r. 1458–1490) transformed Hungary into a powerful centralized state. He outmaneuvered the high nobility by creating a loyal professional bureaucracy and the 'Black Army,' a permanent mercenary force. His tax reforms increased royal revenue significantly, allowing Hungary to become a major European power.*

### Academia Istropolitana

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. ---

*The Academia Istropolitana in Pressburg was the first University of Hungary. It was founded by Pope Paul II on the request of King Matthias Corvinus. It had many well-known lecturers from Austria and Italy, including Galeotto Marzio and Johann Vitez. Sadly the University closed after the death of Mathias Corvinus.*

---

*Historical info:*

*The **Academia Istropolitana** was the first university founded in the Kingdom of Hungary. Established in **1465** in Pressburg (Bratislava) by King **Matthias Corvinus**, it reflected his ambition to make Hungary a Renaissance powerhouse. Founded with the blessing of **Pope Paul II**, it hosted famous lecturers like Galeotto Marzio and Johann Vitez. However, the university was short-lived and closed shortly after Matthias Corvinus's death in 1490.*

*1473 saw the first book printed in Hungary, the Chronica Hungarorum, relating the history of Hungary from the earliest times until the coronation of King Matthias. It was printed by András Hess, in his new printing press.*

---

*Historical info:*

*The year **1473** marked a technological revolution in Hungary with the publication of the* **Chronica Hungarorum***. It was the first book printed in the country, making Hungary one of the first nations outside Germany and Italy to adopt the printing press. Established by **András Hess** in Buda under the patronage of King Matthias Corvinus, it highlighted the King's ambition to modernize his realm through Renaissance humanism.*

Allegedly, she tried to live like a vampire and slaughtered more than 600 virgins in an attempt to renew her youth by bathing in their blood. She was trialed and imprisoned until her death in 1614. ### Hungarian Peasant Revolt

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this infobox. *A plan to assemble an army for a future crusade against the [![Flag of Ottomans](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TUR.png&width=24)](/Ottomans "Ottomans") [Ottomans](/Ottomans "Ottomans") has backfired badly. The task to recruit and train the troops was given to György Dózsa, an experienced veteran from wars against the Turks, but also a soldier of fortune with questionable loyalties. Dózsa proceeded to train and equip a huge army drawing from some of the poorest and most disenfranchised parts of Hungarian society.*

With weapons in hand and a bit of training these peasants, students and country priests have turned out to be far more eager to turn on their masters than to march against the [![Flag of Ottomans](/thumb.php?f=Flag_TUR.png&width=24)](/Ottomans "Ottomans") [Ottomans](/Ottomans "Ottomans"), and one of the largest peasant rebellions in the history of [![Flag of Hungary](/thumb.php?f=Flag_HUN.png&width=24)](/Hungary "Hungary") [Hungary](/Hungary "Hungary") is now in full swing. *Historical info:*

*The Hungarian Peasant Revolt of 1514, led by György Dózsa, began as a crusade against the Ottoman Empire but quickly turned into a massive uprising of peasants against the nobility. The revolt was brutally suppressed, and its aftermath led to harsher conditions for the peasantry in Hungary.*
