## About this site

[![Paradox Interactive logo.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/a/a8/Paradox_Interactive_logo.png/60px-Paradox_Interactive_logo.png)](/Paradox_Interactive "Paradox Interactive")

***[Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V")*** is a grand strategy wargame developed by [Paradox Tinto](/Paradox_Tinto "Paradox Tinto") and published by [Paradox Interactive](/Paradox_Interactive "Paradox Interactive"). This community wiki's goal is to be a repository of Europa Universalis V related knowledge, useful for both new and experienced players and for modders.

Anyone can contribute to the wiki. If you want to become a contributor please register and check out the **[work needed page](/Europa_Universalis_5_Wiki%3AWork_needed "Europa Universalis 5 Wiki:Work needed")**. If you spot anything in need of improvement, don't hesitate to make an edit.
