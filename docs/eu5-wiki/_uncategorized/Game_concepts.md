# Game concepts

This is a list of game concepts from the europedia. It was generated with PyHelpersForPDXWikis. The sections can be moved to other pages and automatically updated there, as long as the section tags are left in place. The links can also be fixed automatically.

## Uncategorized

### $burghers$ Trading

*Aliases: Burghers Exporting, Burghers Importing*

The Burghers will attempt to address needs for [Goods](/Goods "Goods") in the [Market](/Market "Market") that their [Urban Location](#Urban_Location) is located in, by doing their own [Trades](#Trades).

### Action

*Aliases: Actions*

An action is a button which causes an [Effect](#Effect) in the game world. Such actions are usually in the form of [Country Interactions](#Country_Interactions), [Situation Actions](#Situation_Actions) or [Unit Abilities](#Unit_Abilities).

### Administrative Efficiency

This impacts how quickly a [Country](/Country "Country") can implement [Laws](/Laws "Laws"), [Government Reforms](/Government_Reforms "Government Reforms"), and [Estate Privileges](/Estate#Estate_privileges "Estate").

### Adolescent

*Aliases: Adolescents*

A [Character](/Character "Character") who is between the age of [game\_concept\_ShowMinAdolescentAge](/index.php?title=Game_concept_ShowMinAdolescentAge&action=edit&redlink=1 "Game concept ShowMinAdolescentAge (page does not exist)") and [game\_concept\_ShowMaxAdolescentAge](/index.php?title=Game_concept_ShowMaxAdolescentAge&action=edit&redlink=1 "Game concept ShowMaxAdolescentAge (page does not exist)") years old is an adolescent. They are not yet an [Adult](/Adult "Adult") but not really a [Child](#Child) anymore.

### Alliance

*Aliases: Allies, Ally, Allied, Alliances*

An alliance is a [Treaty](#Treaty) between two [Countries](/Countries "Countries"). [Countries](/Countries "Countries") that are allied will not be able to declare [War](#War) upon each other and can also be called to help in each other's [Wars](#Wars).

### Annexation

*Aliases: Annexed, Annexing, Annex*

When an [Overlord](#Overlord) completely takes over one of its [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects").

The [Overlord](#Overlord) will gain all of the [Subject](/Subject "Subject")'s [Owned](#Owned) [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), [Cores](#Cores), and [Characters](/Characters "Characters"). The [Subject](/Subject "Subject") will no longer exist as a [Country](/Country "Country").

The subject can slow the progress with the Frustrate Annexation [Cabinet Action](/Cabinet_Action "Cabinet Action"), or declare [War](#War) with their special Resist Annexation [Casus Belli](/Casus_Belli "Casus Belli").

A [Disloyal Subject](#Disloyal_Subject) cannot be annexed.

### Antagonism Received

This is how much hostile diplomatic actions like [War](#War) will impact our [Antagonism](/Antagonism "Antagonism").

### Area

*Aliases: Areas*

An Area is a group of [Provinces](/index.php?title=Provinces&action=edit&redlink=1 "Provinces (page does not exist)"), and is part of a [Region](#Region).

### Army Based Country

*Aliases: Army Based Countries*

An Army Based Country is a [Country](/Country "Country") that can [Own](#Own) [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), but its existence is relying entirely on its [Armies](/Armies "Armies"). If those are destroyed, then the Country collapses.

### Army Levy

*Aliases: Army Levies*

An army levy is a [Regiment](#Regiment) that has been raised directly from [Pops](#Pops). When the unit is recruited, the [Pops](#Pops) they get recruited from are reduced, and when the unit is disbanded, any survivors return to the [Pops](#Pops). These people are less trained than a regular [Army](/Army "Army") recruited from [Manpower](/Manpower "Manpower"), and they do not reinforce.

A levy [Regiment](#Regiment) also has the following effect:

is\_army\_levy

### Army Maintenance

We need to pay, equip, feed, and clothe our [Armies](/Armies "Armies"), or they will not be very effective. Army Maintenance is a way of measuring how many [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") we are diverting towards making sure our [Armies](/Armies "Armies") have everything they need. Without this, [Morale](/Morale "Morale") will suffer, and our troops will be less effective.

### Artist

*Aliases: Artists*

An artist is a [Character](/Character "Character") that creates [Works of Art](/index.php?title=Works_of_Art&action=edit&redlink=1 "Works of Art (page does not exist)"). An artist can be any of the types below.
artist

### Artist Salary

The amount of [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") an [Artist](#Artist) will earn.

### Assault

An assault is when the attacker in a [Siege](#Siege) tries to storm the [Fortification](#Fortification) in order to take it as quickly as possible. An assault can be rather costly, as the attacker will take heavy casualties.

### Attacker

The Attacker in a [Battle](#Battle) may have several disadvantages, as they will get any [Topography](/Topography "Topography"), [Vegetation](/Vegetation "Vegetation"), or [Crossing](#Crossing) penalties. The other side is referred to as the [Defender](#Defender).

### Autocephalous Patriarchate

*Aliases: Autocephalous Patriarchates*

An Autocephalous Patriarchate is a group of [Countries](/Countries "Countries") of the same eastern Christianity [Religion](/Religion "Religion") that have as the head of their Church one bishop who does not answer to any other within the [Religion](/Religion "Religion"). The [Leader](#Leader) of the Autocephalous Patriarchate is conferred extra benefits, and being a member can have benefits too, until the member [Country](/Country "Country") outgrows the [Leader](#Leader), at which time it may be beneficial to branch out and create their own. If the patriarchate has a [Holy Site](/Holy_Site "Holy Site") within it, then the seat of that patriarchate can be relocated there for extra benefits.

### Available Population

*Aliases: Available, Available, Available Population*

[Pops](#Pops) can be [Employed](#Employed) either in a [Building](/Building "Building") or in an [R.G.O.](/R.G.O. "R.G.O."). Those that are not doing that are considered Available.

### Avatar

*Aliases: Avatars*

Avatars are the different aspects, manifestations, or incarnations that a particular [God](#God) can take in Hinduism.

### Average Morale

This is the average [Morale](/Morale "Morale") of all [Regiments](#Regiments) or [Ships](#Ships) in a [Unit](#Unit).

### Average Satisfaction

This is the average [Pop Satisfaction](#Pop_Satisfaction) in a [Location](/Location "Location"). This has a negative impact on the [Prosperity](/Prosperity "Prosperity") and the [Control](/Control "Control") of the location.

### Bankrupt

*Aliases: Bankruptcy*

A [Country](/Country "Country") choosing to go bankrupt will clear all its [Debt](#Debt). However, this will bring a 5-year period of harsh [Modifiers](#Modifiers).
A [Country](/Country "Country") can also be forced into bankruptcy if it runs out of money while unable to take more [Loans](/index.php?title=Loans&action=edit&redlink=1 "Loans (page does not exist)").

### Blockade

*Aliases: Blockades, Blockaded, Blockading*

A blockade is when a [Hostile](#Hostile) [Navy](/Navy "Navy") is present in the [Seazone](/Seazone "Seazone") outside a [Port](#Port), the amount of [Ships](#Ships) needed depends on the [Population](#Population) and [Development](/Development "Development") of the [Ports](#Ports) affected.

The most important effects of blockade:

* Reduce [Food](/Food "Food") Production
* Reduce [Maritime Presence](/Maritime_Presence "Maritime Presence")
* Reduce [Development](/Development "Development")
* Reduce [Control](/Control "Control")
* Reduce [Prosperity](/Prosperity "Prosperity")

### Blockade Capacity

This determines how good a [Ship](#Ship) is at blockading. This is directly compared to a value in the target [Location](/Location "Location") depending on its [Development](/Development "Development"), [Population](#Population), [Imports](#Imports), and [Exports](#Exports) it has.

### Blocked Treaties

This makes it impossible for a [Country](/Country "Country") to sign any sort of [Treaty](#Treaty) with the [Country](/Country "Country") they are blocked from doing it with.

### Bombard

*Aliases: Bombarding*

[Regiments](#Regiments) that can bombard will deal their [Combat Power](/Combat_Power "Combat Power") as damage during the opening [Bombard Phase](#Bombard_Phase) of [Combat](#Combat).

### Bombard Efficiency

This is the chance that artillery [Regiments](#Regiments) have to fire during the [Bombard Phase](#Bombard_Phase).

### Bombard Phase

The Bombard Phase is the opening [Phase](#Phase) of [Combat](#Combat) which takes place before the main [Combat Phase](#Combat_Phase). It lasts 5 hours and is exclusively utilized by [Regiments](#Regiments) that can [Bombard](#Bombard), such as Artillery.

Every hour of the Bombard Phase, valid [Regiments](#Regiments) have a chance to [Bombard](#Bombard), which will deal their [Combat Power](/Combat_Power "Combat Power") as damage to an enemy [Regiment](#Regiment) on the same [Section](/Section "Section").

### Bombard Strength

Artillery [Regiments](#Regiments) have the strength to bombard [Fortifications](#Fortifications) with their guns. The strength depends on their [Combat Power](/Combat_Power "Combat Power") and current strength.

### Budget

*Aliases: Balance, Finances*

The monthly Balance is based on the total amount of [Incomes](#Incomes) and [Expenses](#Expenses) that a [Country](/Country "Country") has. The result impacts the country's [Treasury](#Treasury).

### Building Category

*Aliases: Building Categories*

[Building Types](#Building_Types) are grouped in different Categories, which is primarily to identify which ones are similar.

### Building Subsidies

Some [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") may not be profitable, but we need the [Goods](/Goods "Goods") they produce for other reasons. Those buildings can be given a [Subsidy](#Subsidy).

### Building Type

*Aliases: Building Types*

A Building Type is the blueprint of a [Building](/Building "Building"), and belongs to a [Building Category](#Building_Category). Most of a [Building](/Building "Building")'s features are dependent on what Building Type it is.

### Call to Arms

A call to arms is a [Diplomatic Action](#Diplomatic_Action) which can be done during [War](#War) to every [Ally](#Ally) and other [Countries](/Countries "Countries") which have sworn to participate in military conflicts.

Countries that are the [Defender](#Defender) will automatically call their allies into the war, while countries that are the [Attacker](#Attacker) have to manually call them, and it is more difficult for them to convince them to join.

### Candidate

*Aliases: Candidates*

These are the [Countries](/Countries "Countries") that are currently considered as the next [Leader](#Leader) of an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization").

### Celestial Authority

This is the measure of the legitimacy of the leader of the Middle Kingdom. It is used to pass [Laws](/Laws "Laws") and [Policies](#Policies) within that [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization").

### Center

This is the center [Section](/Section "Section") of a [Unit](#Unit). If there is no opponent in the center, they can easily reach either the left [Flank](#Flank) or the right [Flank](#Flank).

### Character Interaction

*Aliases: Character Interactions*

These are the [Actions](#Actions) we can do with a [Character](/Character "Character"). Examples include Marry a Noble, Commit Seppuku, or Resign as Grandmaster

### Character Modifier

*Aliases: Character Modifiers*

A [Modifier](#Modifier) that applies to the [Character](/Character "Character"). Modifier types which are supposed to target the [Country](/Country "Country") are only active if the character is the [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler") of that country.

### Child

*Aliases: Children*

A [Character](/Character "Character") who is between the age of [game\_concept\_ShowMinChildAge](/index.php?title=Game_concept_ShowMinChildAge&action=edit&redlink=1 "Game concept ShowMinChildAge (page does not exist)") and [game\_concept\_ShowMaxChildAge](/index.php?title=Game_concept_ShowMaxChildAge&action=edit&redlink=1 "Game concept ShowMaxChildAge (page does not exist)") years old is a child. They can not serve their [Country](/Country "Country"), but if they belong to the [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler")'s [Dynasty](/Dynasty "Dynasty"), you can give them a [Child Education](#Child_Education).

### Civil Construction

*Aliases: Civil Constructions*

Civil Constructions are anything that is currently being built in a [Location](/Location "Location") that is not an [Army](/Army "Army") or [Navy](/Navy "Navy") unit, for example [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"), moving your [Capital](/Capital "Capital"), creating a [Market](/Market "Market") or building [Roads](/index.php?title=Roads&action=edit&redlink=1 "Roads (page does not exist)").

### Civil War

*Aliases: Civil Wars*

A civil war is a special type of [War](#War) in which a [Country](/Country "Country") is at [War](#War) not with another [Country](/Country "Country") but with a part of itself. It also differs from normal [Wars](#Wars) in that there is no [Occupation](#Occupation) of [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), but instead, they will switch [Controller](#Controller) immediately once they are [Sieged](#Sieged).

### Co-belligerent

*Aliases: Co-belligerents*

A [Country](/Country "Country") that is called into a [War](#War) as a Co-belligerent will be able to call its own [Allies](#Allies) into the [War](#War).

Taking [Locations](/Locations "Locations") from a Co-belligerent [Country](/Country "Country") in a [Peace Treaty](#Peace_Treaty) will have the same cost as the war leader's.

### Colonial Migration

[Colonial Migration](#Colonial_Migration) is when [Pops](#Pops) [Migrate](/index.php?title=Migrate&action=edit&redlink=1 "Migrate (page does not exist)") from the [Core](#Core) [Locations](/Locations "Locations") to a [Colonial Charter](/Colonial_Charter "Colonial Charter").

### Colonial Range

This is simply how far away we can [Colonize](/index.php?title=Colonize&action=edit&redlink=1 "Colonize (page does not exist)").

### Combat

*Aliases: Combats, Battle, Battles*

If two or more [Armies](/Armies "Armies") meet in a [Location](/Location "Location"), or two or more [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)") in a [Seazone](/Seazone "Seazone"), and they are [Hostile](#Hostile) towards each other, a battle will start between them.
The battle usually starts with a [Bombard Phase](#Bombard_Phase) and then continues in the [Combat Phase](#Combat_Phase). Each [Regiment](#Regiment) or [Ship](#Ship) will attempt to [Engage](#Engage) the enemy, and fight until one side [Retreats](#Retreats).

### Commander

*Aliases: Commanders*

A Commander is an [Admiral](#Admiral) or [General](#General) in charge of a [Unit](#Unit).

### Common Goods

*Aliases: Base Production*

Some [Goods](/Goods "Goods") like Stone or Clay can be produced everywhere without the need for an [R.G.O.](/R.G.O. "R.G.O."), and this is considered a common good. As normal Goods, they are then provided to the [Market](/Market "Market").

### Conquistador

*Aliases: Conquistadors*

A Conquistador is a [Character](/Character "Character") who has been assigned the task to conquer a foreign land and create a new [Subject](/Subject "Subject") of our [Country](/Country "Country").

### Construction

*Aliases: Constructions*

The process of building something in a [Location](/Location "Location"), such as a [Building](/Building "Building"), a [Regiment](#Regiment), or a City.

Constructions require [Goods](/Goods "Goods") from the [Market](/Market "Market") of the [Location](/Location "Location") in which they are taking place. If the needed [Goods](/Goods "Goods") aren't available, [Construction](#Construction) will stall.

### Construction Material

*Aliases: Construction Materials*

Construction Materials are [Raw Goods](/Raw_Goods "Raw Goods") which play a significant role in the [Construction](#Construction) of [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"). They are produced in a [R.G.O.](/R.G.O. "R.G.O."), and are usually essential for the development of [Urban Locations](#Urban_Locations).

### Continent

*Aliases: Continents*

The world is divided into a few continents, and these continents are then divided into [Subcontinents](#Subcontinents).

### Controller

*Aliases: Controlled, Controls*

The [Country](/Country "Country") currently able to exercise de facto power over a [Location](/Location "Location").

Typically, this is the [Owner](#Owner), but during a [War](#War) this could become an [War Enemy](#War_Enemy) if the [Location](/Location "Location") becomes [Occupied](#Occupied).

### Conversion

*Aliases: Converted, Convert, Converting, Converts*

This is the process by which [Pops](#Pops) change from one [Religion](/Religion "Religion") to another over time. The [Cabinet](/Cabinet "Cabinet") is the most reliable medium for a [Country](/Country "Country") to influence Conversion, but [Laws](/Laws "Laws"), [Privileges](/Privileges "Privileges"), [Advances](/Advances "Advances"), and [Societal Values](/Societal_Values "Societal Values") also play a substantial role.

### Cost of Colonies

It costs [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") to send ships, people, and supplies to maintain a [Colonial Charter](/Colonial_Charter "Colonial Charter"). This cost is represented here.

### Cost of the Court

*Aliases: Court*

The court is the extended household of the [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler"), including all those who regularly attend on them. In the largest courts, this number can grow to many thousands, comprised of camarilla, household staff, clergy, gentry, bodyguards, harem, concubines, eunuchs, and many more roles. All these people do not come for free, and this figure represents the cost of having them around.

### Counterespionage

Counterespionage is how good a [Country](/Country "Country") is to actively stop the build up of [Foreign](#Foreign) [Spy Networks](/index.php?title=Spy_Networks&action=edit&redlink=1 "Spy Networks (page does not exist)") in their country.

### Country Interaction

*Aliases: Country Interactions*

These are the [Actions](#Actions) we can do with a [Country](/Country "Country"). This is either through [Diplomacy](#Diplomacy) or by interacting with a [Subject](/Subject "Subject").

Examples include Sabotage Reputation, Pay off Debt or Cede Location.

### Country Modifier

*Aliases: Country Modifiers*

A [Modifier](#Modifier) that applies to an entire [Country](/Country "Country").

### Country Strength

This is an estimate of the power of a [Country](/Country "Country"). It takes into account its [Military Strength](#Military_Strength), [Income](#Income), and [Population](#Population).

### Courtier

*Aliases: Courtiers*

Any [Character](/Character "Character") in a [Country](/Country "Country") who does not hold a specific [Role](#Role) is considered a Courtier. Courtiers can be employed as [Cabinet Members](#Cabinet_Members), [Generals](#Generals), [Admirals](#Admirals), or [Explorers](#Explorers).

### Crossing

*Aliases: River-Crossing*

An [Army](/Army "Army") crossing a river between two [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), crossing a [Strait](/Strait "Strait"), or landing from a [Seazone](/Seazone "Seazone") will get a penalty in [Battle](#Battle) if they are the [Attacker](#Attacker).

Crossing a [River](#River) imposes a penalty on the [Attacker](#Attacker) of [GetDefine('NCombat','RIVER\_CROSSING\_DICE')|R] on the dice roll. Crossing a [Strait](/Strait "Strait") imposes a penalty of [GetDefine('NCombat','STRAIT\_CROSSING\_DICE')|R].

### Crown Power

This is the power that the crown has in a [Country](/Country "Country"). The bigger it is, the more powerful and efficient the [Country](/Country "Country") becomes. If too many [Estate Privileges](/index.php?title=Estate_Privileges&action=edit&redlink=1 "Estate Privileges (page does not exist)") have been given to the [Estates](/Estates "Estates"), this may be so low that the country gets penalties.

There are [Advances](/Advances "Advances") through the [Ages](/index.php?title=Ages&action=edit&redlink=1 "Ages (page does not exist)") that increase the [Crown Power](#Crown_Power), especially as we advance into the Absolutism.

### Cultural Influence

This is the attack power of a [Culture](/Culture "Culture") in the context of [Culture War](/index.php?title=Culture_War&action=edit&redlink=1 "Culture War (page does not exist)"), representing how impressive it appears to other cultures, and the reach it has beyond its own borders.

It has a monthly increase upon the completion of a [Work of Art](/Work_of_Art "Work of Art") and has some monthly increase from many sources such as [Prestige](/Prestige "Prestige"), and certain [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"), [Advances](/Advances "Advances"), [Laws](/Laws "Laws"), and many others. But beware, it also has a natural monthly decay.

### Cultural Status

Every [Culture](/Culture "Culture") is viewed as one of these by a [Country](/Country "Country"):

* [Primary Culture](/Primary_Culture "Primary Culture")
* [Accepted Culture](/Accepted_Culture "Accepted Culture")
* [Discriminated Culture](/Discriminated_Culture "Discriminated Culture")
* [Tolerated Culture](/Tolerated_Culture "Tolerated Culture")
* [Foreign Culture](#Foreign_Culture)

### Cultural Tradition

This is the defense power of a [Culture](/Culture "Culture") in the context of [Culture War](/index.php?title=Culture_War&action=edit&redlink=1 "Culture War (page does not exist)"), representing how strongly this culture's traditions resonate with its people.

Tradition has a monthly change dependent on relevant [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"), [Societal Values](/Societal_Values "Societal Values"), [Advances](/Advances "Advances"), and many other factors.

### Curia

The Curia is the administrative body of the Holy See and therefore of the Catholicism Church itself. Any Catholicism [Country](/Country "Country") can interact with the Curia and spend [Religious Influence](/Religious_Influence "Religious Influence") to propose a [Curia Action](#Curia_Action).

### Curia Action

*Aliases: Curia Actions*

These are actions taken by the [Curia](#Curia) affecting the whole Catholicism world. Any Catholicism [Country](/Country "Country") is able to propose a debate to the [Curia](#Curia), and every [Country](/Country "Country") with a [Cardinal](/Cardinal "Cardinal") will be able to influence the outcome of the debate by either supporting or opposing it.

If passed, the effects will depend on which Curia Action was taken, but will apply to all Catholicism [Countries](/Countries "Countries").

### Debt

*Aliases: Debts*

This is the total amount of [Loans](/index.php?title=Loans&action=edit&redlink=1 "Loans (page does not exist)") a country has, including any remaining [Interest](#Interest). If this debt grows too large, the [Country](/Country "Country") will go [Bankrupt](#Bankrupt).

### Demand

*Aliases: Demands*

This is the quantity of the [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") that [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") need for their input, how much the [Pops](#Pops) need for their lives, how much various [Constructions](#Constructions) require, and how much [Exports](#Exports) are requested from a [Market](/Market "Market").

### Demote

*Aliases: Demotion, Demoted*

[Pops](#Pops) will demote to Peasants when there are too many of their current [Social Class](/Social_Class "Social Class") in their [Location](/Location "Location").

### Dialect

*Aliases: Dialects*

A [Dialect](#Dialect) is a variation on a [Language](/Language "Language"). It mostly affects the names of things like [Characters](/Characters "Characters") and [Locations](/Locations "Locations").

### Diplomacy

Diplomacy is an interaction between two [Countries](/Countries "Countries") within [Diplomatic Range](#Diplomatic_Range) of each other. It is facilitated by [Diplomats](#Diplomats).

It covers everything from [Alliances](#Alliances), [Treaties](#Treaties), [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects"), and [Overlords](#Overlords), and governs [War](#War) and [Peace](#Peace).

High [Prestige](/Prestige "Prestige") makes it easier to do diplomacy, while high [Antagonism](/Antagonism "Antagonism") makes it harder.

### Diplomat

*Aliases: Diplomats*

This represents how much [Diplomacy](#Diplomacy) a [Country](/Country "Country") can do in a given time. Some [Advances](/Advances "Advances") or [Societal Values](/Societal_Values "Societal Values") will increase this amount.

### Diplomatic Action

*Aliases: Diplomatic Actions*

This is an action done by one [Country](/Country "Country") to another using [Diplomats](#Diplomats). This includes forming [Alliances](#Alliances), declaring [War](#War) or many other things.

### Diplomatic Expense

*Aliases: Diplomacy*

Whether by [Treaty](#Treaty) or by force, this is the amount being extracted from our [Treasury](#Treasury) by other [Countries](/Countries "Countries").

### Diplomatic Income

*Aliases: Diplomacy*

This is the amount entering our [Treasury](#Treasury) from other [Countries](/Countries "Countries"), whether they like it or not.

### Diplomatic Range

Diplomatic Range limits the physical distance our diplomats can travel to conduct diplomacy. The distance to be traveled is from one capital to the other.

### Diplomatic Reputation

A [Country](/Country "Country")'s Diplomatic Reputation represents how highly regarded it is in international relations. In particular, it affects how [Trusted](/index.php?title=Trusted&action=edit&redlink=1 "Trusted (page does not exist)") it is by all other countries.

### Discover

*Aliases: Discovered, Explore, Explored, Discovering*

When a [Location](/Location "Location") is discovered, the [Terra Incognita](#Terra_Incognita) is removed from it, and is now visible and interactable with.

### Disease Outbreak

*Aliases: Outbreak, Disease Outbreaks*

When a [Disease](/Disease "Disease") breaks out in a particular [Location](/Location "Location"), it can then spread from there by various means...and it may or may not claim victims along the way.

### Disease Resistance

This impacts how likely [Pops](#Pops) and [Characters](/Characters "Characters") are to survive [Diseases](/Diseases "Diseases"), like the Bubonic Plague.

### Disloyal Subjects

*Aliases: Disloyal Subject*

When a [Subject](/Subject "Subject") has less than 50 [Subject Loyalty](/Subject_Loyalty "Subject Loyalty") towards its [Overlord](#Overlord), it becomes a Disloyal Subject.

When in such a state, it will no longer help in the [Wars](#Wars) of its [Overlord](#Overlord) and cannot be [Annexed](#Annexed).

### Distributing Food

*Aliases: Distribute Food*

This will send [Food](/Food "Food") from the [Navy](/Navy "Navy") to any [Army](/Army "Army") in an adjacent [Location](/Location "Location") that can not get [Food](/Food "Food") from its current [Location](/Location "Location") or a nearby [Supply Depot](#Supply_Depot).

### Dominant Dialect

The Dominant Dialect is the [Dialect](#Dialect) with the most [Pops](#Pops) speaking it in a [Location](/Location "Location").
This does not necessarily mean that it is the majority.

### Dominant Language

The Dominant Language is the [Language](/Language "Language") with the most [Pops](#Pops) speaking it in a [Location](/Location "Location").
This does not necessarily mean that it is the majority.

### Dynamic Historical Event

*Aliases: Dynamic Historical Events*

These are historical [Events](#Events) that will happen to a specific [Country](/Country "Country") within a specified time period if the conditions are right for it.

### Dynastic Power

*Aliases: Hausmacht*

Dynastic Power is the value of political power a [Dynasty](/Dynasty "Dynasty") holds in an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization"). It plays a major role within the Holy Roman Empire, and is calculated by multiplying the [Great Power Score](/Great_Power_Score "Great Power Score") and ownership over [Pops](#Pops) within the [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization") [Countries](/Countries "Countries") have.

### Economic Base

This is based on the total [Wealth](#Wealth) and [Population](#Population) of a [Country](/Country "Country"), regardless of [Control](/Control "Control").

The number of [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects") and [Institutions](/Institutions "Institutions") embraced also increases it, as well as any [Income](#Income) from [Interest](#Interest) or [Foreign Buildings](#Foreign_Buildings).

### Economic Support

This is a [Treaty](#Treaty) where one [Country](/Country "Country") is sending [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") each month to support the [Finances](#Finances) of the other [Country](/Country "Country"). This can often be the case when a country does not want to directly be involved in a [War](#War) but still wants to support the effort.

### Effect

*Aliases: Effects*

Effects are used in order to affect the game dynamically. They can range from the simple addition of [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") to more complex actions like the unlocking of a [Government Reform](/Government_Reform "Government Reform"). Effects usually happen in an [Event](#Event) or are the rewards from [Missions](/Missions "Missions").

### Effective Demand

This is based on the [Demand](#Demand) of a [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") in a [Market](/Market "Market") but the impact of [Exports](#Exports) is reduced.

### Effective Supply

This is based on the [Supply](#Supply) of a [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") in a [Market](/Market "Market") but the impact of [Imports](#Imports) is reduced.

### Election

*Aliases: Elections*

Depending on the rules of the [Succession Law](/Succession_Law "Succession Law"), an election may occur at regular intervals, or upon the death of the previous [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler").

If there is no [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler"), then the [Government](/Government "Government") will temporarily be ruled by a [Regency](/Regency "Regency") until the election concludes.

### Elector

### Eligibility Rules

The Eligibility Rules of a [Succession Law](/Succession_Law "Succession Law") determine which [Characters](/Characters "Characters") are eligible to become [Heir](#Heir) of a [Country](/Country "Country").

### Embarking

### Embrace

This means that the country has fully understood the [Institution](/Institution "Institution"), and can [Research](#Research) [Advances](/Advances "Advances") that rely on it.

### Emperor

The Emperor is the [Leader](#Leader) of the [Holy Roman Empire](#Holy_Roman_Empire). It is the duty of the Emperor to protect the [Members](#Members) from external and internal threats.

### Exploration

*Aliases: Explorations, Exploring*

An exploration is a mission led by an [Explorer](#Explorer) with the intention of [Discovering](#Discovering) an uncharted [Area](#Area) of land or sea within [Naval Range](/Naval_Range "Naval Range").

Every exploration has a progress cost, increased by the distance from [Capital](/Capital "Capital"), but reduced by [Naval Range](/Naval_Range "Naval Range"). The speed at which an [Explorer](#Explorer) works through this progress is dependent on their [Military Ability](/Military_Ability "Military Ability").

Each exploration has a monthly [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") cost, which an [Explorer](#Explorer) will reduce proportionately to their [Administrative Ability](/Administrative_Ability "Administrative Ability").

Once the exploration reaches the end of its [Exploration Progress](#Exploration_Progress), the [Area](#Area) will be [Discovered](#Discovered), and the [Explorer](#Explorer) will return.

### Exploration Progress

Each [Area](#Area) has a cost to be fulfilled before it is fully [Explored](#Explored). This depends on a lot of factors, but primarily on the skill of the [Explorer](#Explorer).

### Fog of War

*Aliases: Vision*

The shroud that obscures [Discovered](#Discovered) parts of the map is called the [Fog of War](#Fog_of_War).

[Allies](#Allies), [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects"), and [Overlords](#Overlords) share their vision with each other.

Some [Locations](/Locations "Locations") will always be in the [Fog of War](#Fog_of_War), for example those with Forest [Vegetation](/Vegetation "Vegetation") or Mountains [Topography](/Topography "Topography").

[Seazones](/index.php?title=Seazones&action=edit&redlink=1 "Seazones (page does not exist)") with the Narrows [Topography](/Topography "Topography") will only obstruct vision from other [Seazones](/index.php?title=Seazones&action=edit&redlink=1 "Seazones (page does not exist)").

### Fort Maintenance

[Forts](#Forts) are useful for repelling invading [Armies](/Armies "Armies"), but they use [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") in upkeep to make sure they are capable of doing their job. Fort Maintenance is a measure of how many resources a [Country](/Country "Country") is diverting towards ensuring their [Forts](#Forts) are in good shape. The more resources spent, the more effective the forts will be.

### Fortification

*Aliases: Fortifications, Fort, Forts*

These are defensive [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") designed to protect the [Country](/Country "Country")'s own [Locations](/Locations "Locations") against [War Enemy](#War_Enemy) [Armies](/Armies "Armies"). A [Location](/Location "Location") with a fort can not be [Sieged](#Sieged) instantly, but instead will require a prolonged [Siege](#Siege). Certain [Advances](/Advances "Advances") will allow the construction of more effective forts, with each level increasing the difficulty of the [Siege](#Siege) required to [Occupy](#Occupy) them.

Forts have a [Zone of Control](#Zone_of_Control) over adjacent [Locations](/Locations "Locations").

Forts have [Fort Maintenance](#Fort_Maintenance) costs that go directly to the Country's [Treasury](#Treasury), and depend on the prices of [Goods](/Goods "Goods") in their [Market](/Market "Market"). All [Countries](/Countries "Countries") have a [Fort Limit](#Fort_Limit) to the number of forts they can maintain efficiently, and surpassing it increases the [Fort Maintenance](#Fort_Maintenance) considerably.

### Free City

The value is also used as a currency in some Subject Interactions.

### Loan Capacity

This is how much money that a [Country](/Country "Country") can [Lend](/index.php?title=Lend&action=edit&redlink=1 "Lend (page does not exist)") from its internal sources. The [Estates](/Estates "Estates") can invest in this, and will earn all the [Interest](#Interest) that a [Country](/Country "Country") pays from these loans. Some [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"), [Advances](/Advances "Advances") or [Government Reforms](/Government_Reforms "Government Reforms") can impact this as well.

### Location

The physical size of a [Location](/Location "Location"). Not every location is the same size.

### Location Importance

This is determined based on a multitude of factors such as the [Location](/Location "Location")'s harbor suitability, its [Population](#Population) size, how many [Roads](/index.php?title=Roads&action=edit&redlink=1 "Roads (page does not exist)") lead to it, how many [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") it has, whether or not it is our [Capital](/Capital "Capital"), if it has a Castle, as well as its [Market Access](#Market_Access).

### Location Modifier

*Aliases: Location Modifiers*

A [Modifier](#Modifier) that applies to a [Location](/Location "Location").

### Logistics Distance

This is how far away an [Army](/Army "Army") can trace its supply lines to a [Food Source](#Food_Source). Armies will gather food from a [Province](/Province "Province") where it has [Food Access](#Food_Access), a friendly [Supply Depot](#Supply_Depot), or a [Navy](/Navy "Navy") that is [Distributing Food](#Distributing_Food). A [Hostile](#Hostile) army or [Zone of Control](#Zone_of_Control) will block this.

### Major Reform

### Maritime $game\_concept\_merchant\_power$

Your Maritime Trade Advantage represents the ability of your [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)") to effectively protect and influence seaborne trade. It determines the amount of [Trade Advantage](#Trade_Advantage) that a [Country](/Country "Country") generates from having [Maritime Presence](/Maritime_Presence "Maritime Presence") in the coastal [Seazones](/index.php?title=Seazones&action=edit&redlink=1 "Seazones (page does not exist)") of a [Market](/Market "Market").

### Market Access

Market Access represents how easily a [Location](/Location "Location") can interact with its [Market](/Market "Market"), and depends on the distance to the [Market Center](/Market_Center "Market Center").

Market Access is important in three key ways:

* Locations will have higher [Market Attraction](#Market_Attraction) towards [Markets](/index.php?title=Markets&action=edit&redlink=1 "Markets (page does not exist)") with higher access. That means that [Locations](/Locations "Locations") will tend to belong to the closest [Market](/Market "Market").
* [Locations](/Locations "Locations") in a [Market](/Market "Market") buy [Goods](/Goods "Goods") and [Food](/Food "Food") in order of their Market Access.
* Low Market Access harms local industry by reducing the [Output](#Output) of [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"), and the profit of [R.G.O.](/R.G.O. "R.G.O.")

Market Access propagates better over seas and [Rivers](#Rivers), and can be improved by constructing [Roads](/index.php?title=Roads&action=edit&redlink=1 "Roads (page does not exist)") and Naval Buildings, or by creating a new [Market](/Market "Market").

### Market Attraction

This impacts how much pull a [Market Center](/Market_Center "Market Center") has over a [Location](/Location "Location") to make it a part of its [Market](/Market "Market").

It is countered by [Market Protection](#Market_Protection).

### Market Balance

*Aliases: Balance*

The Market Balance of a [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") is the difference between the [Supply](#Supply) and the [Demand](#Demand) of that [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") inside a [Market](/Market "Market").

### Market Language

This is the [Language](/Language "Language") spoken by the majority of the Burghers in the [Market Center](/Market_Center "Market Center").

Burghers who already speak their market's Market Language are reluctant to give it up. Their [Assimilate](/Assimilate "Assimilate") rate toward cultures that don't share that language is reduced in proportion to the location's [Market Access](#Market_Access) — strong commercial ties slow assimilation, while weak ties barely affect it.

### Market Owner

The Market Owner of a [Market](/Market "Market") is the [Country](/Country "Country") that [Owns](#Owns) its [Market Center](/Market_Center "Market Center"). The Market Owner can destroy or relocate the [Market](/Market "Market"), and prevent other [Countries](/Countries "Countries") from trading in it.

### Market Price

*Aliases: Market Prices*

The price at which a certain [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") is traded at the [Market Center](/Market_Center "Market Center").
The market price is incrementally adjusted towards the [Target Price](#Target_Price).
The [Sell Price](#Sell_Price) in other locations may differ from the market price, based on [Market Access](#Market_Access).

### Max Tax

*Aliases: Maximum Tax*

This is the largest percentage of [Tax](#Tax) that can be taken from an [Estate](/Estate "Estate").

### Member

*Aliases: Members*

These are the [Countries](/Countries "Countries") that are part of an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization").

### Mercenary Income

This is the [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") we get from renting our [Armies](/Armies "Armies") or [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)") as [Mercenaries](/index.php?title=Mercenaries&action=edit&redlink=1 "Mercenaries (page does not exist)") to other [Countries](/Countries "Countries").

### Migration Attraction

When [Pops](#Pops) live in a [Location](/Location "Location") with negative Migration Attraction, they will want to move to the [Location](/Location "Location") with the highest Migration Attraction in the same [Market](/Market "Market"). Some types of [Pops](#Pops) like Slaves are permanently restricted from migrating, while others like Peasants may only move if they are allowed to by their [Estate Privileges](/index.php?title=Estate_Privileges&action=edit&redlink=1 "Estate Privileges (page does not exist)").

### Military Access

Military Access allows our troops to move through the land of the other [Countries](/Countries "Countries").

### Military Objective

*Aliases: Military Objectives*

[Armies](/Armies "Armies") and [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)") can be assigned objectives that they will attempt to fulfill on their own.

### Military Strength

The Military Strength of a [Country](/Country "Country") is calculated from its maximum [Manpower](/Manpower "Manpower"), [Army](/Army "Army") size, and [Levy](/Levy "Levy") power.

### Min Tax

*Aliases: Minimum Tax*

This is the lowest percentage of [Tax](#Tax) that can be taken from an [Estate](/Estate "Estate").

### Mission Task

*Aliases: Mission Tasks, Task, Tasks*

A task is a sub-element of a [Mission](/index.php?title=Mission&action=edit&redlink=1 "Mission (page does not exist)"). They are made of a requirement and can contain a reward if the aforementioned condition is fulfilled. All tasks have to be completed in order to finish a [Mission](/index.php?title=Mission&action=edit&redlink=1 "Mission (page does not exist)").

Modifiers are values that influence how the game works, by for example adding or multiplying to a certain calculation.
Modifiers are attached to a certain entity, such as [Country Modifiers](#Country_Modifiers) to [Countries](/Countries "Countries"), [Location Modifiers](#Location_Modifiers) to [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), [Character Modifiers](#Character_Modifiers) to [Characters](/Characters "Characters") and [International Organization Modifiers](#International_Organization_Modifiers) to [International Organizations](/International_Organizations "International Organizations").

This is the monthly gain of new [Manpower](/Manpower "Manpower") for the [Country](/Country "Country").

### Mothballed

*Aliases: Mothball*

Mothballed [Ships](#Ships) require little [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") to maintain them but are not kept in a [Combat](#Combat) ready state, so can be defeated much more easily.

### Natural Harbor Suitability

How well the [Location](/Location "Location") naturally lends itself to the docking of ships and loading/unloading of men and material, before any man-made improvements. It makes port [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") cheaper to construct, and improves [Harbor Capacity](#Harbor_Capacity).

### Naval Levy

*Aliases: Naval Levies*

A naval levy is a transport [Ship](#Ship) that has been commandeered directly from [Pops](#Pops). When the unit is recruited, the [Pops](#Pops) that own the ship and their crew are taken from [Pops](#Pops) in [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), and when the unit is disbanded, any survivors return to the [Pops](#Pops). These ships are generally flimsier than a [Navy](/Navy "Navy") recruited from [Sailors](/Sailors "Sailors"), and should be guarded by proper ships with cannons.

A levy [Ship](#Ship) also has the following effect:

is\_navy\_levy

### Naval Strength

The Naval Strength of a [Country](/Country "Country") is calculated from its maximum [Sailors](/Sailors "Sailors"), [Navy](/Navy "Navy") size, and [Naval Levy](#Naval_Levy) power.

### Navy Based Country

*Aliases: Navy Based Countries*

A Navy Based Country is a [Country](/Country "Country") that can [Own](#Own) [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), but its existence is relying entirely on its [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)"). If those are destroyed, then the Country collapses.

### Navy Maintenance

It is lovely when we have a grand, shiny fleet of [Ships](#Ships) ready to set sail. These vessels do not just maintain themselves, though: [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") must be spent on keeping them shipshape. Navy Maintenance is a way of measuring how many resources we are diverting towards keeping our ships in good condition.

At lower maintenance, [Morale](/Morale "Morale") will suffer, and our ships will be less effective, both in combat and at increasing [Maritime Presence](/Maritime_Presence "Maritime Presence").

### Non-Sovereign Subject

*Aliases: Non-Sovereign Subjects*

A Non-Sovereign Subject is a type of [Subject](/Subject "Subject") that cannot act independently of its [Overlord](#Overlord) and thus has limited capacity to conduct [Diplomacy](#Diplomacy) and cannot fight [Wars](#Wars) independently.

### Occupation

*Aliases: Occupied, Occupier, Occupy, Occupying, Occupations*

Occupation is when the [Controller](#Controller) of a [Location](/Location "Location") is different from its [Owner](#Owner). This happens when a [Location](/Location "Location") falls within the [Zone of Control](#Zone_of_Control) of a [Fortification](#Fortification) that is [Controlled](#Controlled) by an [War Enemy](#War_Enemy), or when an [Army](/Army "Army") successfully [Sieges](#Sieges) the location.

In many cases, a [Location](/Location "Location") needs to be occupied before it can be added to a [Peace Treaty](#Peace_Treaty).

### Output

*Aliases: Produces, Producing*

The amount of [Goods](/Goods "Goods") that is created either from a [Building](/Building "Building") as [Produced Goods](/Produced_Goods "Produced Goods"), or as [Raw Goods](/Raw_Goods "Raw Goods") from a [Location](/Location "Location").

### Overlord

*Aliases: Overlords, Direct Overlord*

An Overlord is a [Country](/Country "Country") that has any number of [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects") as its subordinates.

In return for protecting each [Subject](/Subject "Subject") in [Wars](#Wars), it typically receives [Subject Taxation](#Subject_Taxation), and exacts a certain percentage of the [Subject](/Subject "Subject")'s [Great Power Score](/Great_Power_Score "Great Power Score"). The exact rules are dependent on each [Subject](/Subject "Subject")'s [Subject Type](/Subject_Type "Subject Type").

Any Overlord may in turn be the [Subject](/Subject "Subject") of other [Countries](/Countries "Countries"), allowing long chains of [Subject](/Subject "Subject")-Overlord relationships.

### Overlord Taxation

Overlord Taxation is the [Gold](/Gold "Gold") that a [Subject](/Subject "Subject") pays to its [Overlord](#Overlord). The exact amount is based on the Economy of the [Subject](/Subject "Subject"), and is then scaled by the rules in the [Subject Type](/Subject_Type "Subject Type").

### Owner

*Aliases: Owned, Owns, Own, Owners, Owner's, Ownership*

The [Country](/Country "Country") that is recognized to have de jure possession over a [Location](/Location "Location"). Even if the [Controller](#Controller) currently may be another country.

### Papal Authority

Papal Authority is a measure of how respected and acknowledged the [Pope](/index.php?title=Pope&action=edit&redlink=1 "Pope (page does not exist)") is. It is an [International Organization Value](#International_Organization_Value) of the Catholic Church, with monthly changes driven by: [Reform Desire](/Reform_Desire "Reform Desire"), internal peace, doctrines, [Cardinals](/index.php?title=Cardinals&action=edit&redlink=1 "Cardinals (page does not exist)"), [Excommunications](#Excommunications), and other factors.
Effects apply to all Catholicism [Countries](/Countries "Countries"):
• [Papal Authority](#Papal_Authority) ≥ 75: high\_papal\_authority
• [Papal Authority](#Papal_Authority) < 25: low\_papal\_authority

### Papal Bull

*Aliases: Papal Bulls*

A Papal Bull is an international edict, enacted with [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats"), which affects all [Countries](/Countries "Countries") of the Catholicism [Religion](/Religion "Religion"). While some Papal Bulls have [Effects](#Effects) and an enacted [Law](/Law "Law"), all Papal Bulls have [Modifiers](#Modifiers) for the countries which scale in their effectiveness with the [Papal Authority](#Papal_Authority).

### Parliament Agenda

### Peace

### Protectionism

*Aliases: Market Protection*

### Province Modifier

*Aliases: Province Modifiers*

A [Modifier](#Modifier) that applies to a [Province](/Province "Province").

### Proximity

The Proximity of a [Location](/Location "Location") measures how close it is to the [Capital](/Capital "Capital") of its [Owner](#Owner), and it determines how much [Control](/Control "Control") [Locations](/Locations "Locations") can have.

[Proximity](#Proximity) starts at 100% in the [Capital](/Capital "Capital") of every [Country](/Country "Country"), and jumps to nearby [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), giving them [Proximity](#Proximity). Some [Proximity](#Proximity) is lost with every subsequent jump, depending on the terrain and the infrastructure present in the [Location](/Location "Location"). The [Proximity](#Proximity) that is lost in jumping from one [Location](/Location "Location") to the next is called Proximity Cost.

The Proximity cost between [Locations](/Locations "Locations") varies greatly with [Topography](/Topography "Topography") and [Vegetation](/Vegetation "Vegetation"). On land, it can be reduced by building [Roads](/index.php?title=Roads&action=edit&redlink=1 "Roads (page does not exist)"), increasing [Development](/Development "Development"), and the Land [Societal Value](/Societal_Value "Societal Value"). On sea, it can be reduced by [Harbor Capacity](#Harbor_Capacity), [Maritime Presence](/Maritime_Presence "Maritime Presence") and the Naval [Societal Value](/Societal_Value "Societal Value").

A Region is a group of [Areas](#Areas) that is part of a [Subcontinent](#Subcontinent). Scandinavia would be an example of a Region.

### Regular Army

Refers to the standing [Army](/Army "Army") that a [Country](/Country "Country") has, excluding [Levies](/index.php?title=Levies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Levies (page does not exist)").

### Regular Regiment

*Aliases: Regular Regiments, Regular, Regulars*

Regular Regiments are recruited manually by a [Country](/Country "Country") using most commonly [Manpower](/Manpower "Manpower") and [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats"). These form the standing [Armies](/Armies "Armies"), and are rather limited in numbers until you get the [Advances](/Advances "Advances") from later [Ages](/index.php?title=Ages&action=edit&redlink=1 "Ages (page does not exist)").

### Reinforce

*Aliases: Reinforcement, Reinforced*

When a [Regiment](#Regiment) is below its [Maximum Strength](/Maximum_Strength "Maximum Strength"), it will attempt to reinforce, by drawing [Manpower](/Manpower "Manpower") and [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") from its [Country](/Country "Country"). Only a [Regular Regiment](#Regular_Regiment) can reinforce, and unless in a [Friendly](#Friendly) [Location](/Location "Location") it will not be possible.

### Relation Type

*Aliases: Relation Types*

These are relations we can form with a [Country](/Country "Country"). This is either through [Diplomacy](#Diplomacy) or by interacting with a [Subject](/Subject "Subject").

Examples include embargo\_nation, Demand Sound Toll Exemption or scutage.

### Religious Faction

*Aliases: Religious Factions*

They are different organizations inside a single [Religion](/Religion "Religion"), representing different currents of thought inside it. They will try to vie among each other for the [Country](/Country "Country")'s patronage, and the [Country](/Country "Country") itself can interact with them and obtain different benefits.

### Religious Focus

*Aliases: Religious Focuses*

Some [Religions](/Religions "Religions") have attributes that a [Country](/Country "Country") can focus on to gain extra benefits. Once enough focuses have been completed, the [Religion](/Religion "Religion") can reform.

### Religious Opinion

*Aliases: Religious Opinions*

Every [Religion](/Religion "Religion") has a Religious Opinion of every other [Religion](/Religion "Religion"). This impacts the [Opinion](/Opinion "Opinion") a [Country](/Country "Country") with one [Religion](/Religion "Religion") has of a [Country](/Country "Country") of another [Religion](/Religion "Religion"), and it also impacts the [Pop Satisfaction](#Pop_Satisfaction) of a [Pop](#Pop).

### Religious Reform

A Religious Reform is sometimes required or embraced for the best outcome of a [Religion](/Religion "Religion"), representing the constant adaptation of the rites and faith due to the desires of its believers and institutions.

### Religious Status

This is how a [Religion](/Religion "Religion") is categorized compared to [Primary Religion](/Primary_Religion "Primary Religion").

* [Primary Religion](/Primary_Religion "Primary Religion")
* [Heretic](/Heretic "Heretic")
* [Heathen](/Heathen "Heathen")

### Religious Unity

Religious unity is the percentage of the [Country](/Country "Country")'s [Population](#Population) that follows the official state [Religion](/Religion "Religion").

### Religious War

*Aliases: Crusade, Jihād*

A religious war is declared by an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization") of countries of the same faith to a [Heathen](/Heathen "Heathen") country. The most prominent examples in the game are the Crusades and Jihads of the Christianity and Islam [Religions](/Religions "Religions").

### Repair

*Aliases: Repaired*

When a [Ship](#Ship) is damaged, it can be repaired in one of our own [Ports](#Ports) or where we have [Fleet Basing Rights](#Fleet_Basing_Rights), by drawing [Sailors](/Sailors "Sailors") and [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") from its [Country](/Country "Country").

### Research

*Aliases: Researching, Researched*

Researching an [Advance](/Advance "Advance") takes time, and a [Country](/Country "Country") can only research one advance at a time. We get the benefit of the advance when our [Research Progress](#Research_Progress) reaches the cost of the advance.

The speed at which we progress towards new [Advances](/Advances "Advances") depends mainly on the power of our [Liturgical Language](/Liturgical_Language "Liturgical Language"), the average [Literacy](/Literacy "Literacy") of our [Pops](#Pops), the [Estate Satisfaction](/Estate_Satisfaction "Estate Satisfaction") of our Clergy, and the number of [Institutions](/Institutions "Institutions") we have [Embraced](#Embraced).

### Research Progress

Our [Research Progress](#Research_Progress) depends mainly on the power of our [Liturgical Language](/Liturgical_Language "Liturgical Language"), the average [Literacy](/Literacy "Literacy") of our [Pops](#Pops), the [Estate Satisfaction](/Estate_Satisfaction "Estate Satisfaction") of our Clergy, and the number of [Institutions](/Institutions "Institutions") we have [Embraced](#Embraced).

### Reserves

These [Regiments](#Regiments) or [Ships](#Ships) are not actively fighting in a [Battle](#Battle), but depending on need, they may move up to the [Center](#Center) or either [Flank](#Flank).
Each [Regiment](#Regiment) or [Ship](#Ship) rolls a [D20](#D20) and if the roll is lower than its [Combat Speed](/Combat_Speed "Combat Speed"), it will be able to join the battle. However, only enough units for the possible [Frontage](/Frontage "Frontage") of the battle attempts to reinforce each hour.

### Resolution

*Aliases: Resolutions, Vote, Votes, vote*

These are actions taken by [International Organizations](/International_Organizations "International Organizations") affecting the whole organization. Any [Member](#Member) [Country](/Country "Country") is able to propose a resolution to the other [Members](#Members), and every [Member](#Member) will be able to influence the outcome of the debate by either supporting or opposing it.

If passed, the effects will depend on which resolution was taken.

### Retreat

*Aliases: Retreating, Retreats*

An [Army](/Army "Army") or [Navy](/Navy "Navy") may be forced to retreat from losing a battle, due to their [Morale](/Morale "Morale") becoming too low, or it could voluntarily decide to [Retreat](#Retreat). While retreating, it will not be able to fight again, or be given new orders, until it has reached its destination.

### Revolter

*Aliases: Revolt*

A revolt is when a group of [Locations](/Locations "Locations") break away as a new [Country](/Country "Country") and start a [War](#War) against the original [Owner](#Owner).

### Revolutionary

*Aliases: Revolution*

The [Revolution](#Revolution) is a unique transformation of the society of a [Country](/Country "Country") during the Revolutions. States which go through the Revolution! and fail to defend their absolute state might become [Revolutionary](#Revolutionary), granting them access to powerful [Actions](#Actions), [Reforms](/Reforms "Reforms") and [Laws](/Laws "Laws") at the cost of becoming a potential target of non-[Revolutionary](#Revolutionary) states.

### Revolutionary Target

This is the beating heart of the Revolution and the most powerful [Revolutionary](#Revolutionary) [Country](/Country "Country").

### Rival

*Aliases: Rivals*

[Countries](/Countries "Countries") can declare their hostility towards other countries by declaring them as Rivals. Countries have some additional hostile actions against their rivals, like the ability to fabricate a [Casus Belli](/Casus_Belli "Casus Belli") and the option to intervene in their [Wars](#Wars).

A Rival Country will not accept friendly [Treaties](#Treaties) like [Alliances](#Alliances), but [Countries](/Countries "Countries") with common rivals will have an easier time signing [Treaties](#Treaties) between them.

Rivalry is not reciprocal, meaning that it is not necessary nor automatic for a Country to rival back the ones that have rivaled it. A [Country](/Country "Country") that has declared another as its Rival is known as its [Enemy](#Enemy).

Only [Countries](/Countries "Countries") with similar [Great Power Score](/Great_Power_Score "Great Power Score") and not very good [Opinion](/Opinion "Opinion") can be declared Rivals, and the maximum number of Rivals a [Country](/Country "Country") can have depends on its [Country Rank](/Country_Rank "Country Rank").

### River

*Aliases: Rivers*

A [Location](/Location "Location") may have a River, a moving body of water.

Rivers are not navigable by [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)"), but they impose a [Crossing](#Crossing) combat malus on [Armies](/Armies "Armies") who attempt to enter a [Location](/Location "Location") with a River. Additionally, they slightly increase the [Population Capacity](/Population_Capacity "Population Capacity") of a location.

### Role

*Aliases: Roles*

Roles are different positions that an [Adult](/Adult "Adult") [Character](/Character "Character") may fulfill in a [Country](/Country "Country"). The [Abilities](/index.php?title=Abilities&action=edit&redlink=1 "Abilities (page does not exist)") of a Character will have different effects depending on their Role, and they will be able to earn different [Traits](/index.php?title=Traits&action=edit&redlink=1 "Traits (page does not exist)").

Normally a Character may only perform one Role at a time, but sometimes [Rulers](/index.php?title=Rulers&action=edit&redlink=1 "Rulers (page does not exist)") and [Regents](/index.php?title=Regents&action=edit&redlink=1 "Regents (page does not exist)") can act as [Generals](#Generals) or [Admirals](#Admirals).

### Satisfaction Volatility

This is how quickly the [Estate Satisfaction](/Estate_Satisfaction "Estate Satisfaction") of an [Estate](/Estate "Estate") will move towards the [Satisfaction Equilibrium](/Satisfaction_Equilibrium "Satisfaction Equilibrium") each month.

### Score

This is a way to track how well a [Country](/Country "Country") is performing. Each country is ranked in [Administrative](/index.php?title=Administrative&action=edit&redlink=1 "Administrative (page does not exist)"), [Diplomatic](/index.php?title=Diplomatic&action=edit&redlink=1 "Diplomatic (page does not exist)"), and [Military](/Military "Military") performance, and gain score out of their rank there.
Larger countries also gain score from holding [Victory Cards](#Victory_Cards) which they get one each [Age](/Age "Age").

### Sea Current

*Aliases: Sea Currents*

A Sea Current is a particularity of certain [Seazones](/index.php?title=Seazones&action=edit&redlink=1 "Seazones (page does not exist)") that allows for faster movement of [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)") in a specific direction.

### Sect

*Aliases: Sects*

A Sect is one of the many branches of Buddhism, each one focusing on different teachings and ways of following the path of the Buddha to reach enlightenment.

### Sell Price

The price at which [Goods](/Goods "Goods") are sold in a [Location](/Location "Location").
The Sell Price can be lower than the [Market Price](#Market_Price) if there is low [Market Access](#Market_Access).

### Separatism

[Pops](#Pops) that are not of the [Primary Culture](/Primary_Culture "Primary Culture") will have a lower [Pop Satisfaction](#Pop_Satisfaction) when they live in a [Location](/Location "Location") that has been conquered and not yet [Integrated](#Integrated).

### Settled Country

*Aliases: Settle, Settled, Land Based Country*

A Settled Country is a [Country](/Country "Country") that needs to [Own](#Own) [Locations](/Locations "Locations") in order to exist.

### Share of Power

This is the measure of strength of a particular opposing side during the Fall of Delhi [Situation](/Situation "Situation"). Delhi can increase it through the following means:
- Situation Actions
- [Events](#Events)
- Special Peace Treaties
- Diplomatic Support from other [Countries](/Countries "Countries")

Opposers can increase theirs through offensive wars and victories against Delhi.

### Ship

*Aliases: Ships*

This is the main building block of a [Navy](/Navy "Navy"). A ship is a large boat able to carry [Food](/Food "Food"), [Regiments](#Regiments), and its own crew. Each ship is of a specific [Ship Type](#Ship_Type).

### Siege

*Aliases: Sieged, Sieges, Besieged*

A siege occurs when an [Army](/Army "Army") arrives in a [Location](/Location "Location") when the [Controller](#Controller) and the Army are [War Enemies](#War_Enemies).

A siege against a Location without a [Fortification](#Fortification) will take about a month, but if there is a Fortification present it will take far longer, depending on the level of the Fortification.

If the Army wins against a Location [Owned](#Owned) by the War Enemy, it will then become [Occupied](#Occupied) and it will increase the [Warscore](/Warscore "Warscore") for the Army's Owner.

For a [Siege](#Siege) to progress there needs to be at least as many men besieging it as the number of men in the [Garrison](#Garrison). For a [Location](/Location "Location") without [Fortifications](#Fortifications), there needs to be at least 100 men.

### Situation Action

*Aliases: Situation Actions*

These are the [Actions](#Actions) we can do while a certain [Situation](/Situation "Situation") is active. They vanish once the situation is resolved.

Some examples are Move Court into Hiding and Rekindle the Revolt.

### Society of Pops

*Aliases: Societies of Pops, Pop Based Country*

A Society of Pops is a [Country](/Country "Country") that exists by virtue of its [Pops](#Pops), and does not need to [Own](#Own) entire [Locations](/Locations "Locations").

### Sound Toll

*Aliases: Sound Tolls*

There are some straits in the world where the controller can exact a fee from [Trades](#Trades) traveling past it. For example:

* Øresund
* Sea of Marmara

Each Sound Toll is linked to a specific land [Location](/Location "Location") you need to own in order to be the controller of the strait, and the [Income](#Income) generated from the toll is scaled by your [Control](/Control "Control") in that [Location](/Location "Location").

### Special Status

*Aliases: Special Statuses*

Members with a Special Status in an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization") enjoy certain privileges or have certain duties to fulfill. In some cases, the [Leader](#Leader) of the organization has a special status to emphasize their unique position. Other organizations have [Electors](#Electors) instead which are represented with a special status.

### Special Status Power

*Aliases: Power*

This is the political power that a group of [Members](#Members) with a specific [Special Status](#Special_Status) has in an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization"). This influence can be relevant for passing certain [Parliament Issues](#Parliament_Issues) in the [Parliament](/Parliament "Parliament") unless those are resolved via a [Resolution](#Resolution).

### Stability Investment

*Aliases: Stability*

Spending part of the [Balance](#Balance) on [Stability](#Stability) is one of three main ways to increase stability, the others involve using a [Cabinet Actions](/index.php?title=Cabinet_Actions&action=edit&redlink=1 "Cabinet Actions (page does not exist)") on it or having enough Clergy of the [Primary Religion](/Primary_Religion "Primary Religion").

### Stockpile

This is the quantity of the [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") that is currently stored in warehouses in the [Market](/Market "Market"). It increases if [Supply](#Supply) is higher than [Demand](#Demand), but will decrease if the opposite. [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"), [Constructions](#Constructions), [Units](#Units), and [Pops](#Pops) in the market will use the stockpile for their needs.

### Stored Research Progress

Stored Research Progress is accumulated when there is no [Advance](/Advance "Advance") selected. Stored Research is instantly applied once [Advance](/Advance "Advance") is selected.

### Strength

This how many men a [Regiment](#Regiment) currently has.

### Strength Damage Done

This impacts the amount of damage dealt to an enemy. For [Ships](#Ships), this damages the actual [Ship](#Ship), while for [Regiments](#Regiments) this determines how much [Manpower](/Manpower "Manpower") they will kill.

### Strength Damage Taken

This impacts the amount of damage a unit takes in [Combat](#Combat). For [Ships](#Ships), it is the damage to the actual [Ship](#Ship), while for [Regiments](#Regiments) this determines how much [Manpower](/Manpower "Manpower") dies from combat.

### Subcontinent

*Aliases: Subcontinents*

A Subcontinent is a group of [Regions](#Regions) that is part of a [Continent](#Continent). Western Europe or India are examples of a Subcontinent.

### Subject Taxation

Subject Taxation is the [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") that an [Overlord](#Overlord) receives from their [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects"). The exact amount is based on the Economy of the [Subject](/Subject "Subject"), and is then scaled by the rules in the [Subject Type](/Subject_Type "Subject Type").

### Subsidization

*Aliases: Subsidize, Subsidized*

Some production [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") may have their financial losses [Subsidized](#Subsidized) by their [Owner](#Owner). Any [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") losses that such a building may be incurring will be paid directly from the [Country](/Country "Country")'s [Balance](#Balance), allowing it to operate as normal.

### Subsidy

When a [Building](/Building "Building") is not profitable, it will not buy the [Goods](/Goods "Goods") it needs, and will not function. A [Country](/Country "Country") can elect to subsidy a [Building](/Building "Building"), paying it enough [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") each month to survive.

### Subunit

*Aliases: Subunits*

Subunits are the distinct troops in the [Unit](#Unit) which fight either as [Regiments](#Regiments) or as [Ships](#Ships). They are always of a specific [Unit Type](#Unit_Type) and as such belong to a [Unit Category](#Unit_Category).

### Supply

This is the quantity of the [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") that is produced from [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings"), gathered as [Raw Goods](/Raw_Goods "Raw Goods"), or [Imported](#Imported) into the [Market](/Market "Market").

### Supply Depot

*Aliases: Supply Depots*

[Armies](/Armies "Armies") can create Supply Depots in [Hostile](#Hostile) [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), where they can deposit and store [Food](/Food "Food"). [Friendly](#Friendly) armies can then draw on that supply up to 2 [Locations](/Locations "Locations") away.

### Supply Limit

This is the maximum [Unit Weight](#Unit_Weight) that a [Location](/Location "Location") can supply without causing additional [Daily Food Consumption](#Daily_Food_Consumption) to the [Army](/Army "Army").

### Support Rebels

*Aliases: Support Rebels*

This is when a [Country](/Country "Country") decides to offer assistance to a group of [Rebels](#Rebels) in a different [Country](/Country "Country"), with the hope that if the [Rebels](#Rebels) are victorious then the new government in place will be friendly towards them.

### Target Price

Target Price is determined by [Supply](#Supply), [Demand](#Demand), and [Price Stability](#Price_Stability).
As a proportion of the base price, it will scale between [Price Stability](#Price_Stability) at overwhelming supply; and 1 / [Price Stability](#Price_Stability) at overwhelming demand.

### Tax

*Aliases: Taxes*

We can elect to collect taxes from our [Estates](/Estates "Estates"). This will increase our [Treasury](#Treasury), but higher taxes will decrease their [Estate Satisfaction](/Estate_Satisfaction "Estate Satisfaction").

The amount of [Wealth](#Wealth) that can be taxed depends on the [Control](/Control "Control") of the [Locations](/Locations "Locations") generating [Wealth](#Wealth) for the [Estates](/Estates "Estates").

### Tax Efficiency

This is how efficient our [Country](/Country "Country") is at collecting [Tax](#Tax). It affects the amount of Taxes that reach the [Treasury](#Treasury) when taxing the [Estates](/Estates "Estates").

### Tax Rate

The percentage of the [Max Tax](#Max_Tax) an [Estate](/Estate "Estate") pays as [Taxes](#Taxes).

### Terra Incognita

Before a [Location](/Location "Location") has been [Discovered](#Discovered) by a [Country](/Country "Country") through an [Exploration](#Exploration) or other sources, it is not visible on the map, nor can it be interacted with by that country, and is referred to as Terra Incognita, Latin for unknown land.

### Tier

*Aliases: Tiers*

This is the name for the level that a [Formable Country](/Formable_Country "Formable Country") has. Every [Country](/Country "Country") belongs to a Tier from I to IV, depending on how hard the formation requirements are. Most importantly, [Countries](/Countries "Countries") can only form countries of higher Tiers than their current one.

### Tithe

This is the amount of [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") collected from all [Pops](#Pops) of the Catholicism faith in countries following the same faith, and given to the Papacy. Countries with negative [Opinion](/Opinion "Opinion") of the Papacy will not pay the tithe.

### Tolerance

Tolerance represents how respectfully the population of a certain [Religion](/Religion "Religion") is treated in a society. Every [Country](/Country "Country") has a different Tolerance value towards its [Primary Religion](/Primary_Religion "Primary Religion"), towards [Heretics](/index.php?title=Heretics&action=edit&redlink=1 "Heretics (page does not exist)"), and towards [Heathens](/index.php?title=Heathens&action=edit&redlink=1 "Heathens (page does not exist)").

A positive Tolerance value increases the [Pop Satisfaction](#Pop_Satisfaction) of [Pops](#Pops), as well as the [Diplomatic Opinion](/index.php?title=Diplomatic_Opinion&action=edit&redlink=1 "Diplomatic Opinion (page does not exist)") towards Countries of that Religion. Reversely, a negative Tolerance increases [Unrest](#Unrest) and lowers Diplomatic Opinions.

### Tolerance of Heathens

The Tolerance of Heathens represents the social and political discrimination [Heathens](/index.php?title=Heathens&action=edit&redlink=1 "Heathens (page does not exist)") suffer in a [Country](/Country "Country"). It impacts the [Pop Satisfaction](#Pop_Satisfaction) of heathen [Pops](#Pops), and modifies a Country's [Diplomatic Opinion](/index.php?title=Diplomatic_Opinion&action=edit&redlink=1 "Diplomatic Opinion (page does not exist)") towards heathen Countries.

Tolerance of Heathens is generally very low by default, and can be modified by certain [Laws](/Laws "Laws"), [Privileges](/Privileges "Privileges"), and [Societal Values](/Societal_Values "Societal Values").

### Tolerance of Heretics

The Tolerance of Heretics represents the social and political discrimination [Heretics](/index.php?title=Heretics&action=edit&redlink=1 "Heretics (page does not exist)") suffer in a [Country](/Country "Country"). It impacts the [Pop Satisfaction](#Pop_Satisfaction) of heretic [Pops](#Pops), and modifies a Country's [Diplomatic Opinion](/index.php?title=Diplomatic_Opinion&action=edit&redlink=1 "Diplomatic Opinion (page does not exist)") towards heretic Countries.

Tolerance of Heretics is generally low by default, and can be modified by certain [Laws](/Laws "Laws"), [Privileges](/Privileges "Privileges"), and [Societal Values](/Societal_Values "Societal Values").

### Total Ability

The Total Ability of a [Character](/Character "Character") is the sum of all their [Administrative](/index.php?title=Administrative&action=edit&redlink=1 "Administrative (page does not exist)"), [Diplomatic](/index.php?title=Diplomatic&action=edit&redlink=1 "Diplomatic (page does not exist)"), and [Military](/Military "Military") [Ability](/Ability "Ability") scores added together.

### Total Population

This is the total amount of [Pops](#Pops) that live in a [Country](/Country "Country").

### Total Strength

This how many men an [Army](/Army "Army") has in total, adding together all the individual [Strength](#Strength) of each [Regiment](#Regiment).

### Trade

*Aliases: Trades, Trading*

A trade is the movement of a [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)") from one [Market](/Market "Market") to another. The goods are bought in one market and then sold in the other, hopefully giving a [Trade Profit](#Trade_Profit) to the owner of the trade. The amount of [Trade Capacity](#Trade_Capacity) required for the trade depends both on the distance between the markets and the amount of [Goods](/Goods "Goods") that the trade is handling.

[Trade Income](#Trade_Income) is split between the Crown and the [Estates](/Estates "Estates") according to their [Estate Power](/Estate_Power "Estate Power"). Only the percentage of [Crown Power](#Crown_Power) goes to the [Treasury](#Treasury).

### Trade Advantage

The Trade Advantage of [Countries](/Countries "Countries") in a [Market](/Market "Market") determines who will be able to get their [Exports](#Exports) fulfilled first.

If there is a limited amount of [Supply](#Supply) of [Goods](/Goods "Goods") in a Market they get distributed by the Trade Advantage that each [Country](/Country "Country") has in that Market.

### Trade Capacity

This represents how much a [Country](/Country "Country") can [Export](#Export) and/or [Import](#Import) from a [Market](/Market "Market").

Each [Trade](#Trade) created from this [Market](/Market "Market") uses [Trade Capacity](#Trade_Capacity) depending on the distance to the other [Market](/Market "Market"), and the volume of [Goods](/Goods "Goods") that [Trade](#Trade) is doing.

### Trade Efficiency

This modifies how profitable a [Trade](#Trade) will be, by directly impacting the price you sell [Goods](/Goods "Goods") to the target [Market](/Market "Market").

### Trade Expense

This is the money spent on [Trades](#Trades), buying [Goods](/Goods "Goods") from [Markets](/index.php?title=Markets&action=edit&redlink=1 "Markets (page does not exist)"), and the cost of the [Trade Route Maintenance](#Trade_Route_Maintenance).

### Trade Income

This is the money gained from [Trades](#Trades) when selling [Goods](/Goods "Goods") to [Markets](/index.php?title=Markets&action=edit&redlink=1 "Markets (page does not exist)").

Trade income is split between the Crown and the [Estates](/Estates "Estates") according to their [Estate Power](/Estate_Power "Estate Power"). Only the percentage of [Crown Power](#Crown_Power) goes to the [Treasury](#Treasury).

### Trade Maintenance

Trading is not free, as the [Ships](#Ships), wagons, and caravans need to be maintained, and the merchants themselves need to be paid.

### Trade Profit

The profit of trade primarily depends on the difference between the [Market Prices](#Market_Prices) of the [Goods](/Goods "Goods") in the [Export Market](#Export_Market) and the [Import Market](#Import_Market). There is also a maintenance cost that will reduce the profit, and some trades may be forced to pay a [Sound Toll](#Sound_Toll).

### Trade Range

Defines how far a [Country](/Country "Country") can trade without extra cost. Routes within Trade Range have no additional transport penalty. Routes that exceed it remain possible, but transport costs increase with the distance beyond the range limit.

A larger Trade Range makes long-haul routes — such as colonial goods from the New World or spices from Asia — more affordable by pushing more of each route into the free zone.

Trade Range can be increased primarily through [Advances](/Advances "Advances"), although some [Laws](/Laws "Laws"), [Privileges](/Privileges "Privileges") and [Reforms](/Reforms "Reforms") may affect it too.

### Trade Route

*Aliases: Trade Routes*

Merchants are a company loyal to a [Country](/Country "Country") that are present in a [Market](/Market "Market") and can use their [Trade Advantage](#Trade_Advantage) and [Trade Capacity](#Trade_Capacity) to [Trade](#Trade) [Goods](/Goods "Goods") with another [Market](/Market "Market") in the hope of a profit.

### Trade Route Maintenance

This represents the cost of establishing [Trades](#Trades) between [Markets](/index.php?title=Markets&action=edit&redlink=1 "Markets (page does not exist)"). Trading is not free, as the ships, wagons, and caravans need to be maintained, and the merchants themselves need to be paid.

Trade Maintenance increases with distance, and is affected by the existence of [Sound Tolls](#Sound_Tolls).

### Treasury

This is the amount of [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") a [Country](/Country "Country") has. If it goes negative, a [Loan](/Loan "Loan") will automatically be taken. If no more [Loans](/index.php?title=Loans&action=edit&redlink=1 "Loans (page does not exist)") can be taken, the country will go [Bankrupt](#Bankrupt).

### Treaty

*Aliases: Treaties*

A Treaty is a signed diplomatic state of affairs between two or more [Countries](/Countries "Countries"). They could be for a limited time, or until further notice.
Treaties include [Alliances](#Alliances), [Military Access](#Military_Access), [Truce](#Truce), and many more than can be created from [Diplomacy](#Diplomacy).

### Trigger

*Aliases: Triggers, Condition, Conditions*

Triggers are conditions which need to be fulfilled in order for certain [Effects](#Effects), [Events](#Events), [Tasks](#Tasks) and other kind of game elements to be active.

### Truce

A truce is automatically created when a [Peace Treaty](#Peace_Treaty) is signed between two [Countries](/Countries "Countries"). Declaring [War](#War) on someone we have a truce with will have severe penalties. Some diplomatic actions may be entirely blocked with nations we have a truce with.

### Unavoidable Attrition

Usually [Armies](/Armies "Armies") do not take [Attrition](/Attrition "Attrition") if they are within the [Zone of Control](#Zone_of_Control) of a [Fort](#Fort) owned and controlled by themselves, their direct [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects"), or their direct [Overlord](#Overlord), but some things like the Black Death can not be avoided like that.

### Unified Culture Group

*Aliases: Unified, Unify, Unifies, Unifying, Unified Culture*

Some [Culture Groups](/index.php?title=Culture_Groups&action=edit&redlink=1 "Culture Groups (page does not exist)") can be Unified into a single [Culture](/Culture "Culture").

A country can Unify one of their [Primary Culture](/Primary_Culture "Primary Culture")'s [Culture Groups](/index.php?title=Culture_Groups&action=edit&redlink=1 "Culture Groups (page does not exist)"), which will cause a new [Culture](/Culture "Culture") to be created. [Pops](#Pops) of the old [Primary Culture](/Primary_Culture "Primary Culture") and those of [Accepted Cultures](/index.php?title=Accepted_Cultures&action=edit&redlink=1 "Accepted Cultures (page does not exist)") that are in the same [Group](/index.php?title=Group&action=edit&redlink=1 "Group (page does not exist)") and use the same [Language](/Language "Language") will be immediately assimilated.

Unified Culture Groups have a significant bonus to [Assimilating](/index.php?title=Assimilating&action=edit&redlink=1 "Assimilating (page does not exist)") Cultures of that Culture Group.

A [Culture Group](/Culture_Group "Culture Group") can only be Unified once.

### War

*Aliases: Wars, Offensive War, Defensive War, Offensive Wars, Defensive Wars*

A war is created when one [Country](/Country "Country") uses a [Diplomat](#Diplomat) on another country to start a conflict. Countries that are [Allied](#Allied) with either side may get called into the war.

During a war, [Armies](/Armies "Armies") and [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)") from each side that encounter each other will [Battle](#Battle) each other. [Armies](/Armies "Armies") will be able to [Siege](#Siege) the [War Enemies](#War_Enemies) [Locations](/Locations "Locations"), while [Navies](/index.php?title=Navies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Navies (page does not exist)") can [Blockade](#Blockade) their [Ports](#Ports).

A war continue until a [Peace Treaty](#Peace_Treaty) is signed, and [Peace](#Peace) reigns again between the involved [Countries](/Countries "Countries").

### War Reparations

This is a peace [Treaty](#Treaty) where one [Country](/Country "Country") is made to send [GetDefine('NDiplomacy','WAR\_REPARATIONS\_FACTOR')|Y%0] of their income in [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") each month to support the [Finances](#Finances) of the other [Country](/Country "Country"), usually the victor, in retaliation for the war that just ended.

An invading [Army](/Army "Army") standing on an [War Enemy](#War_Enemy) [Location](/Location "Location") adjacent to an [War Enemy](#War_Enemy) [Fort](#Fort) will have to [Siege](#Siege) the [Fort](#Fort) first before it can move past it to any other adjacent [Location](/Location "Location") of that [Fort](#Fort).

Friendly Zone of Control also shelters [Armies](/Armies "Armies") from local [Attrition](/Attrition "Attrition") sources, including [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). This shelter extends to forts owned by your direct [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects") and to your direct [Overlord](#Overlord).

The Potential Tax Base of a [Location](/Location "Location") is the combined profit of its [R.G.O.](/R.G.O. "R.G.O.") and [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") from selling their [Goods](/Goods "Goods") to the [Market](/Market "Market"). A K'atun is a measure of time of the Maya people, it corresponds to 19.7 years. The end of one K'atun and start of a new one was marked by many ceremonies and festivities, making the occasion a truly important date for rulers and their people.

If by the time the current K'atun ends we have invested enough resources to prepare for the celebrations, we will gain katun\_celebration for 10 years. On the other hand, if we are not ready we might gain katun\_celebration\_bad for 10 years. We will be fully ready once our [Religious Influence](/Religious_Influence "Religious Influence") is at least 90.

### Leader Change Method

This determines how the [Leader](#Leader) of an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization") gets chosen. There are five methods to accomplish this:
- Rotation - The current leader will be the last in line for leadership once their turn is up
- Vote - The leader will be elected via a [Leadership Election Resolution](#Leadership_Election_Resolution)
- Lottery - The leader will be chosen from possible candidates at random
- Score - The leader will be chosen dynamically based on an internal point system specific to the International Organization
- Undefined - The terms of how a leader for the International Organization is chosen are not defined, as no leader is needed or the International Organization has not codified the leadership yet

### Leader Change Trigger

This determines under what conditions the [Leader](#Leader) of an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization") gets changed. There are three triggers when the leader could change:
- On Ruler Change - Whenever the ruler changes, a new leader for the International Organization is considered
- Timed - A new leader of the International Organization is considered after a set amount of months
- Indefinite - The leader remains in power indefinitely

### Leader Type

This determines who actually is the [Leader](#Leader) of an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization"). There are three types of leadership for International Organizations:
- Character - The organization is led by a [Character](/Character "Character") and the country it belongs to
- Country - The organization is led by determined [Country](/Country "Country") which is in charge even if its own [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler") gets replaced
- Leaderless - The organization is in a perpetual state of an interregnum and neither character nor country will represent the organization

This is the special [Resolution](#Resolution) used to determine how the [Leader](#Leader) of an [International Organization](/International_Organization "International Organization") is elected.

### Subject Territory Connectivity

A land connection starts at 100 and loses points for each area traversed based on how low its average [Control](/Control "Control") is. Areas with full control cost nothing; areas with no control reduce the score by up to 20 per area.

A maritime connection is scored from three components: the [Maritime Presence](/Maritime_Presence "Maritime Presence") at the subject's exit port, the naval distance between the ports, and the [Maritime Presence](/Maritime_Presence "Maritime Presence") at the overlord's arrival port. Upon offering unconditional surrender, all of the currently unoccupied [locations|e]] will fall under enemy control and the enemy will gain 100% [Warscore](/Warscore "Warscore"). [Armies](/Armies "Armies") of the [Country](/Country "Country") that surrendered will become [Exiled](#Exiled) and unable to fight in future [Battles](#Battles) until [Peace](#Peace) is signed. The recipient of an unconditional surrender will be alerted of the enemy's surrender and from then on will be able to enforce any possible peace up to 100% warscore cost. If the recipient country does not sign peace after a couple of months, they will get a call for peace giving them monthly [War Exhaustion](/War_Exhaustion "War Exhaustion") which increases faster than normal. The peace will be automatically accepted by the country that surrendered. When naturally reaching 100% warscore, the AI will usually unconditionally surrender. There are also some other cases in which the AI unconditionally surrenders.

## location\_rank

### Urban Rights

Urban Rights are special privileges that a [Country](/Country "Country") can grant to a [Urban Location](#Urban_Location). These can impact the [Location](/Location "Location") and/or the [Country](/Country "Country").

Some [Countries](/Countries "Countries") start with access to historical Urban Rights, and a number of them are granted from 1337. Later on, starting in the Discovery, all [Countries](/Countries "Countries") can research the [game\_concept\_advance\_icon](/index.php?title=Game_concept_advance_icon&action=edit&redlink=1 "Game concept advance icon (page does not exist)")Borough Privileges [Advance](/Advance "Advance"), giving access to Urban Rights that allow for the specialization of [Urban Locations](#Urban_Locations) in a particular purpose, like producing a specific [Good](/index.php?title=Good&action=edit&redlink=1 "Good (page does not exist)").

By default, a Town can have 1 Urban Right, a City can have 2, and a Megalopolis can have 3, although some [Government Reforms](/Government_Reforms "Government Reforms") can increase these values.

# New concepts added 2026-07-09

## Uncategorized

### Alliance Bloc

*Aliases: Alliance Blocs*

An alliance bloc is a coalition of [Great Powers](/index.php?title=Great_Powers&action=edit&redlink=1 "Great Powers (page does not exist)") and [Regional Powers](#Regional_Powers) bound together by [Alliances](#Alliances). The more powerful allies a [Country](/Country "Country") has, the higher its alliance bloc saturation, which increases the [Diplomatic Capacity](/Diplomatic_Capacity "Diplomatic Capacity") cost of each [Alliance](#Alliance) it maintains.

A [Great Power](/Great_Power "Great Power") ally contributes 1.0 to saturation; a [Regional Power](#Regional_Power) ally contributes 0.5. These costs rise for every powerful [Ally](#Ally) added to the bloc, discouraging any single [Country](/Country "Country") from accumulating an overwhelming network of powerful [Allies](#Allies).

### Bond Interest

The monthly [Interest](#Interest) rate paid on outstanding [Government Bonds](#Government_Bonds). Unlike [Estate](/Estate "Estate") loan interest, this cost is a drain on the treasury with no recipient — it represents the ongoing cost of sovereign debt.

### Bureaucracy Type

A Bureaucracy Type is the blueprint of a [Bureaucracy](#Bureaucracy), and defines its characteristics.

### Central Bank

A central bank is a [Building](/Building "Building") in the [Capital](/Capital "Capital") that lets a [Country](/Country "Country") issue [Government Bonds](#Government_Bonds). It provides bond capacity, reduces [Bond Interest](#Bond_Interest), and increases the size of each bond issued.

### Circle Leader

*Aliases: Circle Leaders*

The country which has been designated as as the leader of a [Imperial Circle](#Imperial_Circle). The [Circle Leader](#Circle_Leader) acts as an intermediary between the members of their [Imperial Circle](#Imperial_Circle) and the [Emperor](#Emperor).

### Circle Satisfaction

A measure of how content the members of an [Imperial Circle](#Imperial_Circle) are with their [Circle Leader](#Circle_Leader). Higher satisfaction rewards the [Circle Leader](#Circle_Leader) with [Prestige](/Prestige "Prestige") and [Diplomatic Reputation](#Diplomatic_Reputation).

### Court Dialect

The main [Dialect](#Dialect) of a [Country](/Country "Country")'s [Court Language](/Court_Language "Court Language").

### Creditworthiness

Creditworthiness measures how reliably a [Country](/Country "Country") repays its [Debts](#Debts). It ranges from 0 to 100 with 50 as neutral. Higher values reduce the [Interest](#Interest) rate on all [Loans](/index.php?title=Loans&action=edit&redlink=1 "Loans (page does not exist)") and make foreign lenders more willing to offer credit; lower values raise interest rates and make lenders reluctant. Going [Bankrupt](#Bankrupt) causes a large drain on creditworthiness that recovers slowly over time.

### Establishment

Newly constructed [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") start unestablished. Below the establishment threshold, they operate at reduced throughput, both consuming fewer inputs and producing less output. Once the threshold is reached, the building operates at full capacity and begins earning a bonus to [Production Efficiency](#Production_Efficiency) proportional to how established it is, growing up to +[GetDefine('NEconomy','STARTUP\_MAX\_PE\_BONUS')|+=2%] at full establishment. [Establishment](#Establishment) is gained each month and is lost if the building closes.

### Ethnicity

Ethnicity is what defines the main physical features of a [Character](/Character "Character").

### GDP per capita

The Gross Domestic Product per capita is the total [Income](#Income) of a [Country](/Country "Country") divied by its total [Population](#Population).

### Government Bond

*Aliases: Government Bonds*

A government bond is a form of long-term [Debt](#Debt) issued by a [Country](/Country "Country"). Unlike an [Estate](/Estate "Estate") [Loan](/Loan "Loan"), a bond has no expiry date and is never repaid automatically. The [Country](/Country "Country") receives gold upfront and pays a monthly [Bond Interest](#Bond_Interest) indefinitely until choosing to redeem the bond by paying back the face value.

### Graphical Culture

The main [Culture](/Culture "Culture") used for the representation of the in-game graphics and visuals.

### Great Power Points

*Aliases: Points*

Great Power Points measure how much of the world's economic territory a [Country](/Country "Country") dominates. A [Country](/Country "Country") earns points by controlling at least two-thirds of the ownable [Locations](/Locations "Locations") in an [Area](#Area), counting [Subjects](/Subjects "Subjects") at a reduced rate. Each area is worth 1-10 points based on its potential tax base relative to the world median.

Accumulate enough points to become a [Regional Power](#Regional_Power) or [Great Power](/Great_Power "Great Power").

### Imperial Circle

*Aliases: Imperial Circles*

A regional grouping of [Holy Roman Empire](#Holy_Roman_Empire) members. The [Emperor](#Emperor) can target certain actions directly at an entire [Imperial Circle](#Imperial_Circle) through the [Circle Leader](#Circle_Leader).

### Intelligence Fog

Some information about foreign [Countries](/Countries "Countries") and their [Locations](/Locations "Locations") is hidden unless you have a sufficient [Spy Network](/Spy_Network "Spy Network") in that country. Building a stronger spy network reveals more detailed data, including [Population](#Population) composition, economic output, military strength, and more.

### Liturgical Dialect

The main [Dialect](#Dialect) of a [Country](/Country "Country")'s [Liturgical Language](/Liturgical_Language "Liturgical Language").

### Regional Power

*Aliases: Regional Powers*

A [Regional Power](#Regional_Power) is a [Country](/Country "Country") that has accumulated enough [Great Power Points](#Great_Power_Points) to dominate its region but has not yet reached [Great Power](/Great_Power "Great Power") status.

A [Regional Power](#Regional_Power) earns half the bonuses of a [Great Power](/Great_Power "Great Power") — extra [Diplomatic Capacity](/Diplomatic_Capacity "Diplomatic Capacity"), [Diplomats](#Diplomats), [Power Projection](/Power_Projection "Power Projection"), [Prestige](/Prestige "Prestige"), and mercenary\_range — but its Enforce Peace, Intervene in Wars, and Threaten War anywhere in the world actions are limited to [Countries](/Countries "Countries") whose capital lies in an adjacent [Region](#Region).

### Tension

Measures how inflamed the Italian Wars have become. It rises passively each month and faster when League leaders are actively at war. Higher Tension makes all League leaders more aggressive and shortens the time between their wars.

Tension drops significantly when a League is dissolved via a peace deal.

### Wealth Impact

Wealth Impact is the ratio between an [Estate](/Estate "Estate")'s monthly [Ducats](/Ducats "Ducats") income and its monthly expenses.

A value above 100% means the estate earns more than it spends, letting it accumulate wealth and invest in [Buildings](/Buildings "Buildings") and [Roads](/index.php?title=Roads&action=edit&redlink=1 "Roads (page does not exist)"). A low Wealth Impact makes an estate cautious, restricting it to building only for its own [Estate Power](/Estate_Power "Estate Power") and limiting how much it is willing to fund.

## estate

### Estate Culture

*Aliases: Estate Cultures*

Each [Estate](/Estate "Estate") has a dominant Estate Culture, weighted by the [Pops](#Pops) holding political weight in a [Country](/Country "Country"). The dominant [Language](/Language "Language") among those pops is picked first, and then the dominant [Culture](/Culture "Culture") among pops who speak that language.

An estate whose culture is the [Primary Culture](/Primary_Culture "Primary Culture") or an [Accepted Culture](/Accepted_Culture "Accepted Culture") enjoys higher [Estate Satisfaction](/Estate_Satisfaction "Estate Satisfaction") and [Estate Power](/Estate_Power "Estate Power"). A [Tolerated Culture](/Tolerated_Culture "Tolerated Culture") is neutral, while any other culture lowers both.

### Estate Religion

*Aliases: Estate Religions*

Each [Estate](/Estate "Estate") has a dominant Estate Religion, picked from whichever [Religion](/Religion "Religion") holds the most political weight among the estate's [Pops](#Pops).

The estate's [Estate Satisfaction](/Estate_Satisfaction "Estate Satisfaction") and [Estate Power](/Estate_Power "Estate Power") are modified based on the [Country](/Country "Country")'s tolerance of the estate's religion — [Primary Religion Tolerance](#Primary_Religion_Tolerance) when it matches the [Primary Religion](/Primary_Religion "Primary Religion"), [Tolerance of Heretics](#Tolerance_of_Heretics) when it is a [Heretic](/Heretic "Heretic") faith of the same group, and [Tolerance of Heathens](#Tolerance_of_Heathens) when it belongs to a different religion group.
