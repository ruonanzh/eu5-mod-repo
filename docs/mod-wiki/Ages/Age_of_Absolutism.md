The **Age of Absolutism** is the fifth [age](/Age "Age") in [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V"). The Age of Absolutism starts on January 1st, 1637 when the [Age of Reformation](/Age_of_Reformation "Age of Reformation") ends, and lasts for 100 years until January 1st, 1737 when the [Age of Revolutions](/Age_of_Revolutions "Age of Revolutions") starts.

## Advances
Age of Absolutism has three advance trees; each tree requires embracing an institution.
All Age of Absolutism advances require an additional **+60%** Research Progress from the base cost of **25**. Once this age has begun, advances from the previous Ages cost **-8** flat Research Progress, with the the previous Age increase modifiers still applying.
