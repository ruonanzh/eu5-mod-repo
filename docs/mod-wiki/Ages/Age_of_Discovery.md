The **Age of Discovery** is the third [age](/Age "Age") in [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V"). The Age of Discovery starts on 1 January 1437 when the [Age of Renaissance](/Age_of_Renaissance "Age of Renaissance") ends, and lasts for 100 years until 1 January 1537 when the [Age of Reformation](/Age_of_Reformation "Age of Reformation") starts.
## Effects
The Age of Discovery has the following global effects:
*  +0.25 Exploration Monthly Progress
*  +50% Colonial Maintenance Efficiency
*  +40% Control Impact on Warscore
*  +200% Expected Army Size
*  −48% Expected Navy Size
*  +15% Antagonism Religion Influence
*  +25% Levy Maintenance
*  −75% Navy Levy Size
*  Unlocks  Outward and  Inward [societal values](/Societal_values "Societal values")
*  Unlocks [hegemons](/Hegemon "Hegemon") for  great powers

## Advances
Age of Discovery has four advance trees; one tree does not require embracing an institution.
All Age of Discovery advances require an additional **+30%** Research Progress from the base cost of **25**. Once this age has begun, advances from the previous Ages cost **−8** Research Progress, with the Age of Renaissance's **+15%** modifier still applying.
