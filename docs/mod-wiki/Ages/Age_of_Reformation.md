The **Age of Reformation** is the fourth [age](/Age "Age") in [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V"). The Age of Reformation starts on January 1st, 1537 when the [Age of Discovery](/Age_of_Discovery "Age of Discovery") ends, and lasts for 100 years until January 1st, 1637 when the [Age of Absolutism](/Age_of_Absolutism "Age of Absolutism") starts.
## Effects
The Age of Reformation has the following global effects:
*  −60% Control Impact on Warscore
*  +300% Expected Army Size
*  +330 Pop Conversion Speed
* (Unrecognized modifier type "merchant\_maintenance\_cost" for [Module:Modifier type](/Module%3AModifier_type "Module:Modifier type"))
*  +20% Antagonism Religion Influence
*  +10% Antagonism Culture Influence
*  +10% Antagonism Government Type Influence
*  −30% Levy Combat Efficiency
*  +50% Levy Maintenance
*  −100% Navy Levy Size
*  Unlocks  Mercantilism and  Free Trade [societal values](/Societal_values "Societal values")
*  **500%** Market price max
*  **6%** Market price stability

## Advances
Age of Reformation has three advance trees; each tree requires embracing an institution.
All Age of Reformation advances require an additional **+45%** Research Progress from the base cost of **25**. Once this age has begun, advances from the previous Ages cost **-8** flat Research Progress, with the the previous Age increase modifiers still applying.
