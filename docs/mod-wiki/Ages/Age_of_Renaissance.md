The **Age of Renaissance** is the second [age](/Age "Age") in [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V"). The Age of Renaissance starts on January 1st, 1342 when the [Age of Traditions](/Age_of_Traditions "Age of Traditions") ends, and lasts for 95 years until January 1st, 1437 when the [Age of Discovery](/Age_of_Discovery "Age of Discovery") starts.
## Effects
The Age of Renaissance has the following global effects:
*  +20% Control Impact on Warscore
*  +100% Expected Army Size
*  −64% Expected Navy Size
*  +25% Diplomatic Capacity
*  +25% Cultural Tradition %
*  +10% Antagonism Religion Influence
*  +10% Levy Maintenance
*  −20% Navy Levy Size
*  +25% Mercenary Size

## Advances
Age of Renaissance has four advance trees; one tree does not require embracing an institution.
All Age of Renaissance advances require an additional **+15%** Research Progress from the base cost of **25**. Once this age has begun, advances from the previous Age costs **-8** Research Progress.
