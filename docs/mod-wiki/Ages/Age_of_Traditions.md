The **Age of Traditions** is the first [age](/Age "Age") in [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V"). Every playthrough starts in this age. The Age of Traditions ends after five years, on January 1st, 1342 when the [Age of Renaissance](/Age_of_Renaissance "Age of Renaissance") begins. As the age has already begun at the start of the game, players and countries cannot select an Age Focus.
## Effects
The Age of Traditions has the following global effects:
*  +10% Control Impact on Warscore
*  −80% Expected Navy Size

## Advances
Age of Traditions has several advance trees; only one relies on embracing an institution for the full tree - Meritocracy. The other trees in this Age have branches that require having embraced Feudalism or Legalism (many more advances are locked behind Feudalism). There is also one branch that is locked behind features related to Horses, as a pseudo-institution. Countries outside the historical context for horses will not be able to unlock these advances until later.
Like the other Ages, each advance costs a base rate of **25** Research Progress. The Age of Traditions has two other important values to consider.
#### Starting Technology Level
Each advance is assigned a starting technology level. Each Country in the game has a starting level, and will already have advances researched at the start of game according to their level. Each advance that could be researched where the technology level is not higher than the country's will already be completed, along with any available downtree advances without their own Starting Technology Level value.
For example, Cahokia has starting technology level 1, so they have about half of the Age of Traditions tree completed at game start, including most of the Agriculture branch, but not Alchemy or Apothecary. All advances in the Written Alphabet tree, which has Starting Technology Level 2, are not researched, but are available.
#### Research Cost Modifier
Some advances in the Age of Traditions take extra time to research. Important advances such as Written Alphabet or Agriculture require three times as much research to complete, represented as an additional modifier from the base cost of **25**. Please note this modifier is additive, so Written Alphabet costs **+200%** Research Progress for a total of **75**. It is unclear as of 1.0.7 if this is intended or if this is supposed to represent a multiplicative modifier, as there are no advances listed with a modifier of **+100%**.
