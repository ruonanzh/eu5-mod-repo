*This page is about the Clergy estate; for the pop type, see [Clerics](/Clerics_%28pop%29 "Clerics (pop)")*
Clergy is the [estate](/Estate "Estate") made up of pops with the ") Clerics social class. Every pop produces 10 Power and 25 Tax.
## Estate modifiers
While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  +25% Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate
* Up to  −6 Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language
While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  −50% Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate
* Up to  +200% Pop Conversion Speed %
* Up to  +100% Stability Investment Efficiency
* Up to  +0.33 Monthly Progress to Spiritualist
* Up to  +12 Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language
Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:
*  **−25%** to **+25%** Monthly research progress
*  **−1** to **+1** Diplomatic reputation
## Opinion of other countries
The estate's opinion of other countries scales with that country's religious unity and is also affected by the following:
* **+20** from Catholic countries towards the Papal States
* **+10** if both countries have the Military Order reform or privilege
* **+10** if both countries have Theocracy government
* **+10** if both countries have the same liturgical language
* **+10** if target's court language is the country's liturgical language
* **−0.05** per number of additional advances that the target has
* **−10** towards countries with a heretic religion
* **−20** towards countries with a heathen religion
