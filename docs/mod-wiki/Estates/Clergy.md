*This page is about the Clergy estate; for the pop type, see [Clerics](/Clerics_%28pop%29 "Clerics (pop)")*

Clergy is the [estate](/Estate "Estate") made up of pops with the [![Pop clerics.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_clergy.png&width=24)](/Clerics_%28pop%29 "Clerics (pop)") Clerics social class. Every pop produces 10 Power and 25 Tax.

## Estate modifiers

While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate](/thumb.php?f=Clergy_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate") +25% Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate
* Up to ![Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language](/thumb.php?f=Court_language_is_liturgical_language_importance_modifier.png&width=30 "Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language") −6 Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language

While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate](/thumb.php?f=Clergy_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate") −50% Maximum Tax for Clergy Estate
* Up to ![Pop Conversion Speed %](/thumb.php?f=Global_pop_conversion_speed.png&width=30 "Pop Conversion Speed %") +200% Pop Conversion Speed %
* Up to ![Stability Investment Efficiency](/thumb.php?f=Stability_cost.png&width=30 "Stability Investment Efficiency") +100% Stability Investment Efficiency
* Up to ![Monthly Progress to Spiritualist](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_towards_spiritualist.png&width=30 "Monthly Progress to Spiritualist") +0.33 Monthly Progress to Spiritualist
* Up to ![Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language](/thumb.php?f=Court_language_is_liturgical_language_importance_modifier.png&width=30 "Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language") +12 Prefers Court Language to be Liturgical Language

Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:

* [![Research speed.png](/thumb.php?f=Research_speed.png&width=24)](/File%3AResearch_speed.png) **−25%** to **+25%** Monthly research progress
* [![Diplomatic reputation.png](/thumb.php?f=Diplomatic_reputation.png&width=24)](/File%3ADiplomatic_reputation.png) **−1** to **+1** Diplomatic reputation

## Opinion of other countries

The estate's opinion of other countries scales with that country's religious unity and is also affected by the following:

* **+20** from Catholic countries towards the Papal States
* **+10** if both countries have the Military Order reform or privilege
* **+10** if both countries have Theocracy government
* **+10** if both countries have the same liturgical language
* **+10** if target's court language is the country's liturgical language
* **−0.05** per number of additional advances that the target has
* **−10** towards countries with a heretic religion
* **−20** towards countries with a heathen religion
