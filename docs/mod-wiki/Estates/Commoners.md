Commoners is the [estate](/Estate "Estate") made up of pops with the  Soldiers,  Laborers and  Peasants social classes. Every pop produces 0.025 Power and 1 Tax.
## Estate modifiers
While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  +25% Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate
* Up to  −6 Prefers Court Language to be Common Language
While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  −50% Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate
* Up to  +100% Food Purchase Efficiency
* Up to  +5% Estates Satisfaction Equilibrium
* Up to  +0.33 Monthly Progress to Free Subjects
* Up to  +12 Prefers Court Language to be Common Language
Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:
*  **-20%** to **+20%** Stability Investment Efficiency
*  **−12.5%** to **+12.5%** Food Production %
*  **-25%** to **+25%** Levy Recovery
## Opinion of other countries
The estate's opinion of other countries is affected by the following:
* **+10** towards countries with the Peasant Republic reform
* **+5** if both countries have the same common language
* **−5** towards countries with a different culture group
* **−5** towards countries with a heretic religion
* **−10** towards countries with a heathen religion
* **−20** towards rivals
