Commoners is the [estate](/Estate "Estate") made up of pops with the [![Pop soldiers.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_soldiers.png&width=24)](/Soldiers "Soldiers") Soldiers, [![Pop laborers.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_laborers.png&width=24)](/Laborers "Laborers") Laborers and [![Pop peasants.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_peasants.png&width=24)](/Peasants "Peasants") Peasants social classes. Every pop produces 0.025 Power and 1 Tax.

## Estate modifiers

While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate](/thumb.php?f=Peasants_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate") +25% Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate
* Up to ![Prefers Court Language to be Common Language](/thumb.php?f=Court_language_is_liturgical_language_importance_modifier.png&width=30 "Prefers Court Language to be Common Language") −6 Prefers Court Language to be Common Language

While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate](/thumb.php?f=Peasants_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate") −50% Maximum Tax for Peasants Estate
* Up to ![Food Purchase Efficiency](/thumb.php?f=Food_purchase_cost.png&width=30 "Food Purchase Efficiency") +100% Food Purchase Efficiency
* Up to ![Estates Satisfaction Equilibrium](/thumb.php?f=Global_estate_target_satisfaction.png&width=30 "Estates Satisfaction Equilibrium") +5% Estates Satisfaction Equilibrium
* Up to ![Monthly Progress to Free Subjects](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_towards_free_subjects.png&width=30 "Monthly Progress to Free Subjects") +0.33 Monthly Progress to Free Subjects
* Up to ![Prefers Court Language to be Common Language](/thumb.php?f=Court_language_is_liturgical_language_importance_modifier.png&width=30 "Prefers Court Language to be Common Language") +12 Prefers Court Language to be Common Language

Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:

* [![Stability cost.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability_cost.png&width=24)](/File%3AStability_cost.png) **-20%** to **+20%** Stability Investment Efficiency
* [![Global monthly food modifier.png](/thumb.php?f=Global_monthly_food_modifier.png&width=24)](/File%3AGlobal_monthly_food_modifier.png) **−12.5%** to **+12.5%** Food Production %
* [![Levy recovery modifier.png](/thumb.php?f=Levy_recovery_modifier.png&width=24)](/File%3ALevy_recovery_modifier.png) **-25%** to **+25%** Levy Recovery

## Opinion of other countries

The estate's opinion of other countries is affected by the following:

* **+10** towards countries with the Peasant Republic reform
* **+5** if both countries have the same common language
* **−5** towards countries with a different culture group
* **−5** towards countries with a heretic religion
* **−10** towards countries with a heathen religion
* **−20** towards rivals
