 Cossacks
Cossacks is an [estate](/Estate "Estate") available only to countries whose primary [culture](/Culture "Culture") is part of the East Slavic, Lithuanian or Polish culture group and only pops that have the  Tribesmen social class and Cossack can join it. Every pop produces 0.5 Power and 0.02 Tax.
## Estate modifiers
While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  +50% Maximum Tax for Cossacks
* Up to  −10% Pop Join Rebels Threshold
While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  −30% Maximum Tax for Cossacks
* Up to  +0.1 Military Tactics
* Up to  +0.33 Monthly Progress to Offensive
Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:
*  **+0.025** to **−0.025** Monthly war exhaustion
*  **−5%** to **+5%** Food Production %
## Opinion of other countries
The estate's opinion of other countries is affected by the following:
* **+5** if both countries have the same common language
* **−5** towards countries with a heretic religion
* **−10** towards countries with a heathen religion
* **−20** towards rivals
