Dhimmi is an [estate](/Estate "Estate") available only to countries whose primary [religion](/Religion "Religion") is part of the Islam religion group and only pops from a different religion group who aren't part of the  Tribesmen social class can join it. Every pop produces 0.02 Power and 1 Tax.
## Estate modifiers
While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  +50% Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate
* Up to  −10% Pop Join Rebels Threshold
While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  −30% Maximum Tax for Ḏimmī Estate
* Up to  +0.33 Monthly Progress to Humanist
Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:
*  **−1** to **+1** Tolerance of Heathen Beliefs
*  **−5%** to **+5%** Efficiency of Our Cabinet
