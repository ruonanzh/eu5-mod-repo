*This page is about the Nobility estate; for the pop type, see [Nobles](/Nobles_%28pop%29 "Nobles (pop)")*

Nobility is the [estate](/Estate "Estate") made up of pops with the [![Pop nobles.png](/thumb.php?f=Pop_nobles.png&width=24)](/Nobles_%28pop%29 "Nobles (pop)") Nobles social class. Every pop produces 25 Power and 100 Tax.

## Estate modifiers

While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Noble Estate](/thumb.php?f=Nobles_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Noble Estate") +50% Maximum Tax for Noble Estate

While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:

* Up to ![Maximum Tax for Noble Estate](/thumb.php?f=Nobles_estate_max_tax.png&width=30 "Maximum Tax for Noble Estate") −100% Maximum Tax for Noble Estate
* Up to ![Levy Combat Efficiency](/thumb.php?f=Levy_combat_efficiency.png&width=30 "Levy Combat Efficiency") +100% Levy Combat Efficiency
* Up to ![Fortification Maintenance Efficiency](/thumb.php?f=Fort_maintenance_cost.png&width=30 "Fortification Maintenance Efficiency") +100% Fortification Maintenance Efficiency

Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:

* [![Monthly prestige.png](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_prestige.png&width=24)](/File%3AMonthly_prestige.png) **−0.1** to **+0.1** Monthly prestige
* [![Monthly legitimacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_legitimacy.png&width=24)](/File%3AMonthly_legitimacy.png) **−0.25** to **+0.25** Monthly legitimacy

## Opinion of other countries

The estate's opinion of other countries scales with the difference in prestige and power projection and also affected by the following:

* **+5** if both countries have [![Monarchy](/thumb.php?f=Monarchy.png&width=30)](/Government_type#Monarchy "Monarchy") [Monarchy](/Government_type#Monarchy "Government type") government
* **+5** towards countries with higher cultural influence
* **−5** towards neighbor countries
* **−10** towards countries with higher expected army size
* **−10** towards countries with less works of art
