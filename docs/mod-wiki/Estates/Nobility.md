*This page is about the Nobility estate; for the pop type, see [Nobles](/Nobles_%28pop%29 "Nobles (pop)")*
Nobility is the [estate](/Estate "Estate") made up of pops with the ") Nobles social class. Every pop produces 25 Power and 100 Tax.
## Estate modifiers
While estate power is below **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  +50% Maximum Tax for Noble Estate
While estate power is above **25%**, the country will gain the following modifiers:
* Up to  −100% Maximum Tax for Noble Estate
* Up to  +100% Levy Combat Efficiency
* Up to  +100% Fortification Maintenance Efficiency
Regardless of estate power, the country will gain the following effects depending on the estate satisfaction:
*  **−0.1** to **+0.1** Monthly prestige
*  **−0.25** to **+0.25** Monthly legitimacy
## Opinion of other countries
The estate's opinion of other countries scales with the difference in prestige and power projection and also affected by the following:
* **+5** if both countries have  [Monarchy](/Government_type#Monarchy "Government type") government
* **+5** towards countries with higher cultural influence
* **−5** towards neighbor countries
* **−10** towards countries with higher expected army size
* **−10** towards countries with less works of art
