**Ages** represent distinct periods of history. Most ages in [Europa Universalis V](/Europa_Universalis_V "Europa Universalis V") last 100 years. Each age has three [institutions](#Institutions), which each represent specific cultural or technological paradigm shifts. Each institution has an associated tree of [advances](#advances) which represent smaller innovations or specific technologies such as ship designs or production methods.

## List of ages

Every century is its own age. Each age has its own rules, mechanics, and advance trees that can be progressed through.

At the start of an age, each country will select one of three focuses, [Administrative Ability](#Administrative_Ability), [Diplomatic Ability](#Diplomatic_Ability), or [Military Ability](#Military_Ability), which determines the options in the advance tree for that age.

## Institutions

Each age has three different institutions that slowly spread between locations and along trade routes. When a new age is reached, each institution starts with a 1% chance to spawn each month, growing over time so it spawns soon after the age starts. The location where an institution spawns depends on the game rules. Hovering over an institution that can spawn but has not appeared yet shows all valid spawn locations and the chance for each location to be selected. The location where an institution spawns gains a permanent modifier related to that institution.

Once an institution has spread at least 20% in a country, it can be embraced at a scaled cost of [![Resource stability.png](/thumb.php?f=Stability.png&width=24)](/Stability "Stability") Stability and [![Resource gold.png](/thumb.php?f=Resource_gold.png&width=24)](/Gold "Gold") Gold. Embracing an institution will unlock a new branch in the advances tree and begin to spread the institution from the country's capital. Embracing an institution also doubles its spread throughout the country but this bonus decreases with lack of control.

Institutions spread to locations at the following rates:

* Multiplied by [![Literacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Literacy.png&width=24)](/Literacy "Literacy") Literacy:
  + **0.02** if the market center has the institution
  + **0.05** if the country capital and the country embraced the institution
  + **0.005** if shares a sea zone with a location where the institution is present
  + **0.01** if neighbors a location that has the institution, and both locations are in the same country or countries that have positive opinion of each other
  + **0.1** if the location met the institution spawning requirements and the Institution Spawn [game rule](/Game_rule "Game rule") is set to *Dynamic*
* **2** times [![Control.png](/thumb.php?f=Control.png&width=24)](/Control "Control") Control if the country embraced the institution
* **0.05** times [![Literacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Literacy.png&width=24)](/Literacy "Literacy") Literacy plus **0.02** times [![Development.png](/thumb.php?f=Development.png&width=24)](/Development "Development") Development if importing from a location that has the institution

## Advances

*To see lists of advances, see the age pages, [linked above](#top).*

Advances represent ideas, inventions, technologies, and other examples of progress that are unlocked over time. Each country can research one advance at a time, and each age has its own set of advances.

If a country is not researching an advance, it can store up to 12 months of research progress. Once an advance is selected for research, it begins with research progress equal to the stored value.

The base research cost for an advance is 25[[1]](#cite_note-1). This cost is modified by **+15%[[2]](#cite_note-2)** for each age after the Age of Tradition, up to **+75%** more in the Age of Revolutions. Advances from previous ages have an **−8[[3]](#cite_note-3)** research point discount.
