A **formable country** is a new country identity that can be formed (or re-formed) from an existing country fulfilling certain conditions. Players can form countries of higher formation tier. The AI can form countries of higher formation tier. Hovering over a formable country will highlight the areas where locations must be owned to form it, and if the formable country has advancements specific to it, its tooltip will list all the new available advances.
There are three types of formable nations:
* Historical - nations that existed at some point during the timeframe of the game
* Plausible - nations that likely could have existed if things had changed only slightly
* Ahistorical - nations that are highly unlikely to have ever formed realistically.
The formation of ahistorical nations can be disabled via the game rules.
