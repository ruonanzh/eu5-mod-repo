# Laws
Each law has to have a [Policy](/index.php?title=Policy&action=edit&redlink=1 "Policy (page does not exist)") selected for it, else it is not active. Different [Government Types](/Government_Types "Government Types") and [Religions](/Religions "Religions") gain different laws, and more laws are given through [Advances](/Advances "Advances"). Depending on what [Policy](/index.php?title=Policy&action=edit&redlink=1 "Policy (page does not exist)") we pick in a law, we will often impact our [Societal Values](/Societal_Values "Societal Values").
Each country has an Administrative Efficiency, which ranges from 0% to 100%. By default, law changes take 2 years to implement, reduced by the administrative efficiency of the nation enacting the law.
Changing most laws has a base cost of 100  Stability.
Laws may be changed with no stability cost through [Parliament](/Parliament "Parliament") request, at the cost of 50% approval in Parliament.
