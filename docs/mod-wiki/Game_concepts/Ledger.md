Ledger
The ledger allows a player to browse various information and statistics about their country or the rest of the world. Only information from locations and countries that have been discovered will be displayed in the ledger.
* The **Population** tab shows the population of both the player's country and their subjects. It will display total population, pie charts for culture, religion, and social class, and a breakdown of population and all its stats by location.
* The **Countries** tab displays and allows sorting countries by name, country type, population, income, primary religion, primary culture, works of art, prestige, and great power score.
* The **Score** tab displays and allows sorting countries by name, score rank, administrative ability rating, diplomatic ability rating, military ability rating, score that cannot be lost, and score including victory cards.
* The **Wars** tab displays and allows sorting wars by name, primary attacker country, number of attacker countries, primary defender country, number of defender countries and, warscore.
* The **Dynasties** tab displays and allows sorting dynasties by name, head of dynasty name, origin location, power, number of living characters, and number of countries that have been ruled.
* The **Cultures** tab displays and allows sorting cultures by name, population, language, cultural influence, cultural tradition, and primary country.
* The **Religions** tab displays and allows sorting religions by name, population, religion group, and number of countries that have it as primary religion.
* The **Locations** tab displays and allows sorting locations by name, rank, population, tax, religion, culture, development, control, sailors, manpower, food, migration, and raw goods.
* The **Markets** tab displays and allows sorting markets by name, owner, total value traded, trade capacity, food balance, surplus goods, and highest demanded goods.
* The **Goods** tab displays and allows sorting goods by name, amount produced, total amount, market where they're sold the most, demand, and category.
* The **Works of Art** tab displays and allows sorting works of art by name, location, origin, category, artist, quality, date, and whether they're destroyed or not.
* The **Modifiers** tab ab displays and allows sorting stat modifiers by name and value in the player's country.
