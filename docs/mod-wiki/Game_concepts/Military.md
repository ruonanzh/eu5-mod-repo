Military

[Navy](/Navy "Navy")

Armies and navies can only enter another country's borders during a war. If they are still within another country's borders after a peace treaty has been signed, the army or navy will be marked as In Exile. They will be unable to siege or fight until they have returned to their own country's borders.

After a unit has moved at least half of the distance between two adjacent locations, its movement towards the destination location will be locked and cannot be changed.

If a unit has more than 40 regiments or ships, it will lose **-2%** Speed for every one above 40, up to a maximum penalty of **-50%**.

During a war, the unit UI will display whether the unit is visible to the opposing side or not.

Navies move 3 times faster than armies.

## Morale

Every army and navy has a morale, which shows how long it will fight before leaving the battle. In battle, every regiment or ship inflicts morale damage upon its opponent, which can be increased or reduced by a variety of factors. Once morale hits zero, the army, or navy will retreat. Morale will slowly recover if the unit is not in combat or moving.

## Experience

Every regiment and ship has an experience level, which ranges from 0% to 100%. Every 1% experience reduces the damage taken by 0.5%. Experience is gained through participating in a battle, a siege, or a blockade, and slowly decays over time. Regiments can also gain experience by the [![Drill army.png](/thumb.php?f=Drill_army.png&width=24)](/File%3ADrill_army.png) Drill Army unit ability after it has been researched.

## Attrition

Attrition is applied at the start of each month, which for [Regiments](/index.php?title=Regiments&action=edit&redlink=1 "Regiments (page does not exist)") means that people die, while for [Ships](/index.php?title=Ships&action=edit&redlink=1 "Ships (page does not exist)") it also damages the actual ship. Some attrition is [Unavoidable Attrition](/index.php?title=Unavoidable_Attrition&action=edit&redlink=1 "Unavoidable Attrition (page does not exist)") and the main sources for attrition include the following:

* [Army](/Army "Army") without [Food](/Food "Food")
* Total [Unit Weight](/Unit_Weight "Unit Weight") in [Location](/Location "Location") above [Supply Limit](/index.php?title=Supply_Limit&action=edit&redlink=1 "Supply Limit (page does not exist)")
* [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), [Topography](/Topography "Topography"), [Vegetation](/Vegetation "Vegetation"), or [Climate](/Climate "Climate")

Each country has 4 modifiers that improve its military:

* [![Discipline.png](/thumb.php?f=Discipline.png&width=30)](/File%3ADiscipline.png) Discipline affects the damage armies inflict and takes. A Discipline value is halved between the two damage types just like terrain bonuses. E.g. 24% discipline applies a 12% modifier to the strength and Morale values individually. Do note that Discipline's defensive value scales non-linearly like military tactics. 10% discipline is actually a 9% reduction and 50% would be a 33%.
* [![Military tactics.png](/thumb.php?f=Military_tactics.png&width=30)](/File%3AMilitary_tactics.png) Military Tactics affects an army's incoming damage. This reduction is applied to the unit with the higher military tactics and is calculated by taking the difference between the two fighting unit's tactic values. This value scales non-linearly. For example, a military tactics advantage of 0.2 gives a 16% damage reduction to the higher tactic unit, not a 20%.

## Levies

Levies are military units that can be raised from the remaining manpower when the country is at war or facing rebels. Raising levies reduces population by an amount equal to the raised soldiers or ships, and disbanding levies will increase population by an amount equal to the remaining levies. Hovering over the button to raise levies will list all locations where levies will be raised from. Levies will only be raised from pops whose estates have at least [![Satisfaction.png](/thumb.php?f=Satisfaction.png&width=24)](/Satisfaction "Satisfaction") **25%[[1]](#cite_note-1)** estate satisfaction, but a higher satisfaction will not increase the amount of levies. Estates who are below the threshold will not contribute any levies.
Levies are generally less efficient than regulars and even more in later ages.

Army levies have the modifier *Levy*

* ![Discipline](/thumb.php?f=Discipline.png&width=24 "Discipline") −10% Discipline
* ![Monthly Experience Decay](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_experience_gain.png&width=24 "Monthly Experience Decay") +2% Monthly Experience Decay
* ![Unit Food Consumption](/thumb.php?f=Food_consumption_modifier.png&width=24 "Unit Food Consumption") −75% Unit Food Consumption

Navy levies have the modifier *Levy*

* ![Monthly Experience Decay](/thumb.php?f=Monthly_experience_gain.png&width=24 "Monthly Experience Decay") +2% Monthly Experience Decay

A location in which levies have been raised gains the modifier *Raised Levies* until all its levies are disbanded or killed

* ![Local Food Production %](/thumb.php?f=Global_monthly_food_modifier.png&width=24 "Local Food Production %") −20% Local Food Production %
* ![Raw Materials Output](/thumb.php?f=Global_raw_material_output.png&width=24 "Raw Materials Output") −20% Raw Materials Output

There are multiple types of levies that can be raised, depending on the pop used to raise them.

## Mercenaries

Mercenaries are private individuals who take part in military conflict for [![Resource gold.png](/thumb.php?f=Resource_gold.png&width=24)](/Gold "Gold") Gold, but are otherwise outsiders to the conflict. The base cost of Mercenaries is 50% higher than regulars.

A country that is at peace can make its own units available for hire to other countries as mercenaries. The country will set up the [![Resource gold.png](/thumb.php?f=Resource_gold.png&width=24)](/Gold "Gold") Gold fee and duration in months of the contract.

If a mercenary unit is inside or next to another country's borders, the other country can bribe them to switch sides by paying 5 times their [![Resource gold.png](/thumb.php?f=Resource_gold.png&width=24)](/Gold "Gold") Gold fee.

In addition to mercenaries made available by countries, each country can hire mercenary leaders from among the characters in any nearby country to raise a mercenary army. The types and amounts of units a mercenary leader can raise depends on what is available for recruitment in the character's country of origin. Hiring mercenary leaders costs 50 Gold, increased by the mercenary leader's abilities. The cost is also affected by the mercenary leader's opinion of the hiring country's primary culture and religion.
