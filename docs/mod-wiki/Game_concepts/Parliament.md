# Parliament
Parliament debating an issue
A [Country](/Country "Country") can call its [Estates](/Estates "Estates") into a session of Parliament, to solve any [Parliament Issue](/Parliament_Issue "Parliament Issue") they may have, while also giving the opportunities to create a [Casus Belli](/Casus_Belli "Casus Belli"), raise [Taxes](/index.php?title=Taxes&action=edit&redlink=1 "Taxes (page does not exist)"), increase [Army Levies](/index.php?title=Army_Levies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Army Levies (page does not exist)") and [Naval Levies](/index.php?title=Naval_Levies&action=edit&redlink=1 "Naval Levies (page does not exist)"), or change any of the current [Laws](/Laws "Laws"). Any [Estate](/Estate "Estate") may have a [Parliament Agenda](/index.php?title=Parliament_Agenda&action=edit&redlink=1 "Parliament Agenda (page does not exist)") to resolve before they will pledge their support.
Parliament may take place in the [Capital](/Capital "Capital") or another [Location](/Location "Location") which receives some small benefits. If the [Location](/Location "Location") becomes [Occupied](/index.php?title=Occupied&action=edit&redlink=1 "Occupied (page does not exist)"), the Parliament will fail.
Which estates can suggest parliament agendas depends on the parliament type. The Nobility estate can always suggest parliament agendas.

## Parliament requests
Parliament requests are actions that a country can spend 50% [Parliament Support](#Parliament_Support) on to get through. The support required can be reduced by 20% by the Full College reform and increased by certain Nobles estate privilege. The request takes effect even if the parliament later fails. (E.g., you have 55% Parliament Support, 'spend' 50% on a new law and then fail at the end of parliament with only 5% support: the new law stays in effect.)
| Parliament request | Effects |
| **Request Additional Taxes** | * **+25%** Maximum tax for 1 year * Cannot be used again for 1 year |
| **Prepare for War** | * Select a country within diplomatic range to gain a Dubious Claims on Province casus belli * Cannot target countries with a truce, casus belli on, own subjects, or a rebel country |
| **Ask for Larger Levies** | * **+25%** levy size for 3 years * Cannot be used again for 3 years |
| **Ask for Law Changes** | Select a law to change its policy |
| **Force Parliament Issue** | * Passes the current parliament issue * Cannot be used again for 5 years * Requires either a ruler with the Absolute Rule modifier or the Autocratic Parliament type |
## Call parliament
The parliament can be called every 5 years in an attempt to get support to resolve a parliament issue. When the parliament is called, a location must be chosen as the parliament seat. Picking the capital will move societal values towards  Centralization while picking another location will move them towards  Decentralization and give the location the following for the duration of the parliament:
*  +5% Max Control
*  +10% Monthly Control
*  +0.01 Local Monthly Development
*  +1% Local Monthly Prosperity
Once the parliament is called, the chosen parliament issue is debated for 180 days. Whether it passes or fails depends on the estate power used to support or oppose it. Each estate will support or oppose an issue, which adds its estate power to either side. The estate which proposed an issue will always support it. Accepting a parliament agenda costs varying amount of  stability and will shift societal values, enact a policy, affect opinion with another country, or reduce the taxes on the supporting estate for 5 years.
If the parliament has not been called within the last 120 months, for every months afterwards that it is not called all estates will lose **−1%** Satisfaction Equilibrium.
### Parliament issues
If a parliament issue passes, the country gains a bonus, but if it fails, the country loses 7  Stability. Each estate will propose up to two parliament issues each time.

### Parliament agendas
While a parliament issue is being debated, estates that didn't propose the issue and can vote in the parliament will offer a parliament agenda. Accepting a parliament agenda will cost resources and reduce the satisfaction of the other estates that can vote but will also add support to the parliament issue that scales with the proposing estate's power and satisfaction and the importance of the agenda. Once a parliament agenda is accepted the same estate will offer another one if it can but the support provided by the same estate will be reduced by **-25%** for each accepted agenda.
