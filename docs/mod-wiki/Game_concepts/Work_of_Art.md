Various works of art

A **work of art** is something that an artist has created. When the art is created, it will directly increase the cultural influence of the primary [culture](/Culture "Culture") of the [country](/Country "Country") that hired the artist. Each work of art is present in a [location](/Location "Location"), but can be destroyed or captured if that location becomes occupied. Each country has an [![Skill of new artists.png](/thumb.php?f=Skill_of_new_artists.png&width=24)](/File%3ASkill_of_new_artists.png) Artistic Skill, which starts at 10% and is increased by the country's [![Prestige.png](/thumb.php?f=Prestige.png&width=24)](/Prestige "Prestige") Prestige and [![Literacy](/thumb.php?f=Literacy.png&width=24)](/Population#Literacy "Literacy") Average Literacy.

Works of art are created by hiring artists and using the Commission Art character interaction. Each country can support a limited number of artists, which depends on the country rank and is increased by various [advances](/Advances "Advances"). In order to hire artists, a country must research either the [![Advancement background.png](/thumb.php?f=Advancement_background.png&width=24)](/File%3AAdvancement_background.png)[![patron of art](/thumb.php?f=Advance_patron_of_art.png&width=24)](/Advances#patron_of_art "patron of art") Patron of the Arts generic advance, or one of more conditional advances. Each artist has an Artistic Skill ability, and its effects are increased by the country's [![Skill of new artists.png](/thumb.php?f=Skill_of_new_artists.png&width=24)](/File%3ASkill_of_new_artists.png) Artistic Skill. In order for artists to make progress on a work of art, they must be paid a salary via the [![Has cultural maintenance.png](/thumb.php?f=Has_cultural_maintenance.png&width=28)](/File%3AHas_cultural_maintenance.png)Culture Investment slider in the Balance tab of the Economy menu.

When a location is conquered, any work of art there has a 10% chance of being destroyed and a 40% chance of being captured.

There are various types of works of art. Most of them have the same effects, but some works of art cannot be captured. New scriptures cannot be created.
