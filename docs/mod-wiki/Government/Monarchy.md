# Monarchy

Monarchy is a [government](/Government "Government") type ruled by the first among elites and the successor is usually the heir of the [ruler](/Characters#Ruler "Characters"). Their government power is determined by [![Resource legitimacy.png](/thumb.php?f=Legitimacy.png&width=24)](/Legitimacy "Legitimacy") Legitimacy. Monarchies can gain alliances via royal marriages and can inherit foreign countries through personal Unions.
