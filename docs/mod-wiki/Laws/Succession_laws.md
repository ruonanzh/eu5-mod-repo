Every [Country](/Country "Country") has a Succession Law which defines which [Character](/Characters "Characters") shall be the next [Ruler](/Ruler "Ruler") once the current one dies or abdicates. Many Succession Laws define a heir before the succession occurs based on the Heir Selection Score of that character, while others trigger an election. Each different Succession Law has its own rules, such as whether an heir may be of the same Dynasty, or if they must belong to specific [Estates](/Estates "Estates").
Changing the succession law costs 50  Stability and Government Power. In order to do it, click the icon below your heir's portrait on you ruler's portrait.
